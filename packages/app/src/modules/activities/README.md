## Scope

Activities and sessions are read with the grant kinds `coach` (the group of the record — and a substitute's session id, later), `coordinator` (its section) and `club_admin`, least-privileged first. They are planned — series created, changed, deleted, sessions moved — by the coordinator of the group's section and the club administrator. A one-off event is also created by a coach of the group without the coordinator (REQ-027; a group they do not coach → 403), and a session is also cancelled by a coach of its group. The coach role holds `activities.coach` and `activities.events.create` but not `activities.view` (club spec feature table), so in this PR a coach creates events and cancels sessions through the API; their reads and screens are the `/club` pages of step 4.

## Rules

- **Generation (DD-10, DD-G02).** A series generates its sessions from today (never the past) to 16 weeks ahead, not beyond `ends_on` or the rule's `UNTIL`, at the club's wall time (`17:00` stays `17:00` across DST). The daily job extends `generated_until`; a slot that already has a session (generated, moved or cancelled) is never generated again and the unique slot index makes a retry a no-op. A rule without any session, or a series ending before today, answers 422 `activity_no_sessions`.
- **Series edits (DD-G03).** Changing the rule, dates, times, place, coach or `is_active` re-plans the future sessions: past sessions, detached (moved) sessions and pinned sessions (cancelled, done, or with checked-off attendance since #33; substitutions join with their story) are never touched; the other future sessions are updated in place, removed when the rule no longer produces their slot, and missing slots are created. Deactivating an activity removes its future movable sessions.
- **Detaching.** `PATCH /api/activities/occurrences/{id}` moves or re-staffs one planned session and sets `is_detached`; the session keeps its slot date, so the generator does not refill the slot.
- **Conflicts (club spec glossary _Session conflict_, DD-G05).** On a place, its ancestors and descendants, the planned sessions in use at one instant must not exceed the place's `capacity_groups`; a coach leads one session at a time; the optional `vereino.occupancyProvider` (phase 5) adds bookings, closures and maintenance. The preview lists every conflict per session; every save (create, edit, move, event, undo) is refused with 409 `occurrence_place_conflict` (or `occurrence_coach_conflict` when only the coach collides) and `conflicts: [{ key, startAt, endAt, kind, sessions[], blocks[] }]`. All session writes of a club are serialized by an advisory lock, so two writes cannot overbook together. The daily job cannot refuse: a new session over a conflict is created with `needs_reschedule` for the coordinator. A failing provider fails a save closed (uncoded 500, fingerprint `activities.occupancy.provider_failed`) and only warns in the preview.
- **Cancellation.** `planned → cancelled` with a reason (`invalid_transition` for a done or cancelled session). Where the club setting `coach_may_cancel` is off (#199), a caller who holds only a coach grant on the session gets 403 `coach_cancel_disabled` (`cancelRoleOf` in `commands/occurrences.ts`; a coach who also coordinates the section cancels as the coordinator), and the member reads of M-04 (`calendar/{id}?as=coach`) and M-05 (the attendance list) say `mayCancel: false`, so the pages offer no "Cancel session". After commit the participants with an account, the guardians with an account of every participant not known to be 18, and the coaches (the session's coach and the group's coaches and assistants on the session date) get the in-app notification `activities.occurrence_cancelled`; the user who cancelled is not notified. The notification is grouped by the session id, so a retried delivery refreshes it.
- **Deleting.** An activity whose sessions have history (a past, cancelled or done session) is not deleted (409 `activity_has_sessions`): deactivate it.

## Attendance check-off and session close (#33)

| Piece   | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Command | `activities.attendance.mark` (a coach of the group, the session's assigned coach, the coordinator, the club admin; undoable): `present` / `absent` / `late` (or `null`) for persons on the list, against the session version; `complete` closes the session (`planned → done`) |
| Route   | `GET`, `PUT /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/attendance` (`activities.coach`; the record scope decides the session) — the list, and the save answering with the refreshed list                                                                                                 |
| Events  | `activities.attendance.marked` (session, changed persons, acting role, `completed`, `undo`), `activities.occurrence.completed`                                                                                                                                                 |
| Pages   | `/club/occurrences/[id]/attendance` (`activities.coach`), linked from the coach perspective of `/club/calendar` and from `/backend/activities/occurrences/[id]`                                                                                                                |

Rules:

- **The list.** The group's participants on the session date and anyone with an attendance row; the summary line counts the participants per the kind (`absence_only`: expected / absent; `confirm`: confirmed / declined / unanswered). A person not on the list is refused (422 `attendance_person_not_listed`).
- **When.** From the session's start until it is closed (409 `attendance_not_started` before; 409 `occurrence_not_planned` when cancelled or done). Before the start the list shows the expectations only.
- **Who marked it.** A changed row records `marked_by_person_id` and `marked_as_role` (the session's assigned coach from another group marks as `coach`); an unchanged row keeps its marker. A row created by the check-off has `declared = unknown`, `source = staff`.
- **Version.** Every save sends the session's `updatedAt` (400 `attendance_version_required` without it) and moves it; a save with an older one answers 409, and the page refreshes the list while keeping the coach's selections.
- **Undo.** Reverts only the marks of the last save (the declaration fields stay, whatever the expiry job did meanwhile) and removes the rows it created; refused (409) after a later save, and for a save that closed the session — reopening is an administrator command with a reason.
- **Series edits.** A session with checked-off attendance is pinned: a series edit no longer moves or removes it. Declarations alone do not pin a future session.
- **The page (#99, E06-S17).** "All declared present" marks every person with a final "yes" and no mark as present (`lib/attendance-sheet.ts`). Closing sends every unmarked person as `absent`, so the actual attendance is always complete (R11); the confirmation says how many, whether the session is still running and to whom the unit is credited. Ctrl/Cmd+Enter starts the close. A closed save shows "Session closed — {n} present" (present and late) and returns to `/club`. An empty list links a coordinator or the club administrator to the group roster. On a phone the actions stay in a sticky bar above the bottom navigation.

## Personal declaration links (#105)

Club spec REQ-017, AC-017, TEST-017; foundation P-10 (token links), P-19 (`TokenPageShell`, `DeclarationControls`); UX concept M-13, M-14, F-06:

- **Table.** `activities_declaration_tokens`: the hash of the raw token only, `guardian_person_id` (the person the link was sent to — a guardian of participants or a participant), `occurrence_id`, `expires_at` (the session's end, at most 31 days), `used_count`, `last_used_at`. Usable any number of times until it expires (`until_expiry`); grants are never stored.
- **Request (`POST /api/activities/declaration-links`, `/club/link`).** `{ email, clubSlug, occurrencePublicRef }` without a session; the club is the DD-35 slug lookup, the session is read inside it. For a planned session that has not started, every person of the session's family (a participant or an active guardian of one on the session date) whose address matches — compared in memory over that family, so no address lookup hash exists — gets an e-mail with `/club/d/<token>` through the foundation `dispatch` (the raw token only in the delivery's encrypted secret), at most 3 per person and session in an hour. Every well-formed request answers the same 202 after at least one second; a failure is reported to telemetry, never shown. 10 requests per 15 minutes per IP.
- **The link (`GET`, `POST /api/activities/declaration-links/{token}`, `/club/d/[token]`).** `GET` peeks (spends nothing) and lists the session's participants within the holder's current `self` and `guardian` grants — first names only — with the session's kind and `window` (`open`, `closed` after the start, `not_planned`). `POST { personId, declared }` runs `activities.attendance.declare_by_link`: the link is redeemed in the command's transaction, `authorizeScoped` checks the person with the `{ token }` scope source (403 `scope_denied` for another family's child or an ended guardianship), and the shared declaration rules apply with `source = link` and the holder as `declared_by_person_id`. The audit entry has no command payload (the input carries the raw token) and the command is not undoable. 404 `declaration_link_not_found` for an unknown, malformed or foreign token; 410 `token_link_expired`. Responses are `no-store`.
- **Reminders (`activities:send-declaration-reminders`).** Daily at 18:00 club time (registered by `setup.ts`, re-registered with the generator when the time zone changes; `yarn mercato activities send-declaration-reminders --tenant --org [--now]`): tomorrow's planned `confirm` sessions — an `absence_only` session counts silence as expected (D-022) — e-mail a link to the guardians of every participant without a declaration, or to the participant without a guardian; one dispatch key per person and session, so a rerun sends nothing twice.

## Public schedule (#91)

`activities.publicSchedule` (club spec phase 2 step 9, DD-28, DD-29, REQ-025; `services/public-schedule.ts`, pure rules in `lib/public-schedule.ts`) is the optional block provider `club_core` reads with `hasRegistration` for a club that publishes `description_schedule`. On a section page (key `schedule`) it returns, for each public group `groups.directory.publicGroups` names in the section, the weekdays, times and place of every active series that has not ended and the next three sessions of those series, a cancelled one flagged (`cancelled: true`; the page strikes it through). On the club page it returns the upcoming one-off activities (no RRULE) of public groups in the public sections `club_core` passes (`PublicClubBlockContext.sections`), at most ten, with date, times, place, section and type. Every query carries the tenant and organization of the slug lookup; no roster, participant or coach enters the block.

- **Today.** A person with the `coach` context gets the tile "My sessions today and tomorrow" (`as=coach`) next to the member tiles, so a coach who is also a guardian sees both; acting as a coach (the header switcher) leaves only the coach tile. Each session opens `/club/occurrences/[id]?as=coach` and carries an "Attendance list" button and a "Needs a coach" marker (`needsCoach`: planned, no coach assigned).
- **Session page.** `GET /api/activities/calendar/{id}?as=coach` reads the session under the scope of `as=coach` in the calendar (a coach of its group or its assigned coach, `activities.coach`) with the declaration summary and no persons; any other session of the club answers 403 `scope_denied`. The page reads it next to the member perspective (`lib/member-session.ts`), so a coach who is also a guardian gets the declarations and the "Running the session" block together, and a coach of another group sees "This is not your session" with none of the session's data.
- **Cancellation.** "Cancel session" asks for a reason of 3–200 characters (`cancelReasonProblem`; the API keeps 1–500) and warns that participants, guardians and the coach are told. `POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/cancel` now also answers `notified` (`cancellationNotifiedCount`: the persons the cancellation concerns, without the canceller), which the toast names.
- **Not here.** "Message the group" arrives with the phone composer of E06-S20 (#102); "Report absence" came with E06-S13 (#95, below).

## The coach creates a one-off event from the phone (#140)

Club spec REQ-027, AC-027 (*UI and Interaction Contracts* `/club/events/new`):

- **Entry.** "New event" on the coach tile of Today and on `/club/calendar` acting as a coach opens `/club/events/new` (`people.self` + `activities.events.create`). `newEventHref` takes a proposed date, place and group, so the facility calendar's "Book for group" (M-11, phase 5) can open the same form (`ClubEventForm`) with a slot.
- **Options.** `GET /api/activities/events/options` (`activities.events.create`) answers the live active groups of the caller's `coach` grant with their section, and the club's bookable places (leaves, "Parent › Place") through `clubCoreDirectory.placeOptions` — the coach has neither `groups.manage` nor `club_core.view`. Invited teams come from `GET /api/groups/external-teams`, which a coach reads. `lib/member-event.ts` shapes both lists.
- **Form.** Group (preselected when the coach has one), tournament / other, name, place, one date with start and end in the club's time zone (`eventRequestBody` via `zonedInstant`), invited teams for a tournament, note. `eventDraftProblems` names missing or wrong fields before sending; the submit stays in a sticky bar above the bottom navigation.
- **Answers.** 201 opens `/club/occurrences/[id]?as=coach`. 409 lists the colliding entries in the page (`ConflictList` with `linkSessions={false}`: their pages are the backend's) and saves nothing; 403 and the coded 422s (`eventFieldOfError`) point at their field. The command and its rules are unchanged (#31).

## Coach absence, substitutes and unsettled sessions (#95)

E06-S13 (README M-04, M-05, B-15, B-01, flow F-03 path A; club spec REQ-008, DD-12, TEST-010; D-030 lifted the A05 hold, the migration approved by the owner on 2026-11-05):

- **History.** `activities_substitution_entries` (`SubstitutionEntry`, append-only through the foundation guard): the session, the previous and the new coach, `kind` (`absence_reported`, `substitute_assigned`, `coach_restored`), the reason (encrypted, `encryption.ts`), who decided and in which role. Nothing edits or deletes an entry; a correction is a new assignment. A session with an entry is pinned against a series edit like a checked-off one (`actedOnSessionIds`).
- **Absence (`POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/absence`, `activities.occurrences.report_absence`).** Only the session's assigned coach (`self` on the coach's person; a coach of another group or a non-assigned coach of the group gets 403), for a planned session that has not started (409 `occurrence_not_planned` / `absence_after_start`), with an optional reason of up to 200 characters and the lock token. The session is left without a coach; after commit `activities.occurrence.coach_unassigned` sends `activities.coach_needed` to the coordinators of the section on the club's today, or to the club administrators when the section has none (`subscribers/notify-coach-needed.ts`). The button is on M-04's coach block and on M-05 for the assigned coach only (`canReportAbsence`); the toast reads "Absence reported — the coordinator has been notified".
- **Assignment (`GET`, `POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/coach`, `activities.occurrences.assign_coach`).** The coordinator of the section or the administrator (`activities.manage`). `GET` lists the coaches of the club on the session date (an active `coach` role or a coach / assistant membership) without the current one, each with the overlapping planned session that keeps them busy (`substituteOptions`); the dialog (`components/AssignCoachDialog.tsx`) disables those with "Busy: {session}". `POST { coachPersonId, reason? }` refuses a busy coach with 409 `occurrence_coach_conflict` and its `conflicts` (under the schedule lock), a person who is not a coach with 422 `activities_reference_invalid`, the current coach with 422 `occurrence_coach_unchanged` and a session that is not planned with 409. The kind is `coach_restored` when the session goes back to its own coach (the coach before the first entry). After commit `activities.occurrence.coach_changed` sends `activities.substitution_assigned` to the new coach. Neither command is undoable. The record's "Move" no longer edits the coach (the `PATCH` still accepts it), so every change of the coach goes through the history.
- **Reads.** List rows, the session detail and the calendars carry `substituteForPersonId` / `substituteForName` (the session's own coach while a substitute leads it); the detail adds `substitutions` (the history with names and reasons). B-15, M-03 and M-04 mark "Needs a coach" and "(substitution)"; the record shows "{substitute} for {coach}" and the history.
- **Lists.** `GET /api/activities/occurrences?unassigned=1` (planned, no coach) and `?unsettled=1` (planned, ended 24 hours ago or more — `isUnsettled`). B-15 adds the perspectives "Needing a coach" (from today for 14 days) and "Unsettled" (no window), an "Unsettled" status badge and the row action "Assign substitute" / "Change coach".
- **Widgets.** `activities.dashboard.sessionsNeedingCoach` and `activities.dashboard.unsettledSessions` (`dashboards.view` + `activities.view`; the E06-S06 pattern, `lib/dashboard-rules.ts`): the count, five sessions and "Open list" to the perspective, in the record scope of the list route. The coordinator role has no `dashboards.view` (D29: the coordinator works from the phone), so today they serve the administrator.
- **Today.** The coach tile opens with an "Unsettled session" alert for each of the coach's sessions of the last two weeks that is unsettled, linking to its attendance list.
- **Not here.** The unit credit and the settlement (#96).
