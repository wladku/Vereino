## Scope

Activities and sessions are read with the grant kinds `coach` (the group of the record — and a substitute's session id, later), `coordinator` (its section) and `club_admin`, least-privileged first. They are planned — series created, changed, deleted, sessions moved — by the coordinator of the group's section and the club administrator. A one-off event is also created by a coach of the group without the coordinator (REQ-027; a group they do not coach → 403), and a session is also cancelled by a coach of its group. The coach role holds `activities.coach` and `activities.events.create` but not `activities.view` (club spec feature table), so in this PR a coach creates events and cancels sessions through the API; their reads and screens are the `/club` pages of step 4.

## Rules

- **Generation (DD-10, DD-G02).** A series generates its sessions from today (never the past) to 16 weeks ahead, not beyond `ends_on` or the rule's `UNTIL`, at the club's wall time (`17:00` stays `17:00` across DST). The daily job extends `generated_until`; a slot that already has a session (generated, moved or cancelled) is never generated again and the unique slot index makes a retry a no-op. A rule without any session, or a series ending before today, answers 422 `activity_no_sessions`.
- **Series edits (DD-G03).** Changing the rule, dates, times, place, coach or `is_active` re-plans the future sessions: past sessions, detached (moved) sessions and pinned sessions (cancelled, done, or with checked-off attendance since #33; substitutions join with their story) are never touched; the other future sessions are updated in place, removed when the rule no longer produces their slot, and missing slots are created. Deactivating an activity removes its future movable sessions.
- **Detaching.** `PATCH /api/activities/occurrences/{id}` moves or re-staffs one planned session and sets `is_detached`; the session keeps its slot date, so the generator does not refill the slot.
- **Conflicts (club spec glossary _Session conflict_, DD-G05).** On a place, its ancestors and descendants, the planned sessions in use at one instant must not exceed the place's `capacity_groups`; a coach leads one session at a time; the optional `vereino.occupancyProvider` (phase 5) adds bookings, closures and maintenance. The preview lists every conflict per session; every save (create, edit, move, event, undo) is refused with 409 `occurrence_place_conflict` (or `occurrence_coach_conflict` when only the coach collides) and `conflicts: [{ key, startAt, endAt, kind, sessions[], blocks[] }]`. All session writes of a club are serialized by an advisory lock, so two writes cannot overbook together. The daily job cannot refuse: a new session over a conflict is created with `needs_reschedule` for the coordinator. A failing provider fails a save closed (uncoded 500, fingerprint `activities.occupancy.provider_failed`) and only warns in the preview.
- **Cancellation.** `planned → cancelled` with a reason (`invalid_transition` for a done or cancelled session). After commit the participants with an account, the guardians with an account of every participant not known to be 18, and the coaches (the session's coach and the group's coaches and assistants on the session date) get the in-app notification `activities.occurrence_cancelled`; the user who cancelled is not notified. The notification is grouped by the session id, so a retried delivery refreshes it.
- **Deleting.** An activity whose sessions have history (a past, cancelled or done session) is not deleted (409 `activity_has_sessions`): deactivate it.

Rules:

- **What and who (D-026).** What is declared is the club setting `declaration_kinds` per activity type; a policy only decides who declares for a minor with an account. A minor declaring for themself follows the policy; a guardian, an adult for themself and staff always declare finally. Declarations change until the session starts (409 `declaration_closed`).
- **Expiry.** A `pending_guardian` row carries its `pending_until` and the outcome the policy chose when it was declared, so a later policy change does not rewrite pending rows; the job moves only the rows still pending (a guardian's decision wins a race) and emits `activities.attendance.guardian_decided` with `byTimeout` for the rows it changed.
- **Member reads.** Member calendars never use `activities.view`: they read the sessions of the groups the member or the wards are participants of on each session date; anything else is 404 / 403. Called-up children (REQ-006) are shown only to a guardian under `show_called_up_children = consented`, and only the children whose active guardian gave `share_presence_with_guardians_at`.
- **Coach view.** `as=coach` reads the coach's groups and own sessions with the declaration summary, so a coach sees their schedule on `/club/calendar` without `activities.view`.

## Attendance check-off and session close (#33)

| Piece   | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Command | `activities.attendance.mark` (a coach of the group, the session's assigned coach, the coordinator, the club admin; undoable): `present` / `absent` / `late` (or `null`) for persons on the list, against the session version; `complete` closes the session (`planned → done`) |
| Route   | `GET`, `PUT /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/attendance` (`activities.coach`; the record scope decides the session) — the list, and the save answering with the refreshed list                                                                                                 |
| Events  | `activities.attendance.marked` (session, changed persons, acting role, `completed`, `undo`), `activities.occurrence.completed`                                                                                                                                                 |
| Pages   | `/club/occurrences/[id]/attendance` (`activities.coach`), linked from the coach perspective of `/club/calendar` and from `/backend/activities/occurrences/[id]`                                                                                                                |

Rules:

- **The list.** The group's participants on the session date and anyone with an attendance row; the summary line counts the participants per the kind (`absence_only`: expected / absent; `confirm`: confirmed / declined / unanswered). A person not on the list is refused (422 `attendance_person_not_listed`).
- **When.** From the session's start until it is closed (409 `attendance_not_started` before; 409 `occurrence_not_planned` when cancelled or done). Before the start the list shows the expectations only.
- **Who marked it.** A changed row records `marked_by_person_id` and `marked_as_role` (the session's assigned coach from another group marks as `coach`); an unchanged row keeps its marker. A row created by the check-off has `declared = unknown`, `source = staff`.
- **Version.** Every save sends the session's `updatedAt` (400 `attendance_version_required` without it) and moves it; a save with an older one answers 409, and the page refreshes the list while keeping the coach's selections.
- **Undo.** Reverts only the marks of the last save (the declaration fields stay, whatever the expiry job did meanwhile) and removes the rows it created; refused (409) after a later save, and for a save that closed the session — reopening is an administrator command with a reason.
- **Series edits.** A session with checked-off attendance is pinned: a series edit no longer moves or removes it. Declarations alone do not pin a future session.

## Gathering time (#136)

Club spec DD-43 (README B-14, B-15, M-02, M-03, M-04; messaging spec DD-M14):

- **Storage.** `gathering_minutes_before` on `activities_activities` (the series default, 1–720, null = none) and on `activities_occurrences` (the session's own). The planner (`services/series.ts`), the daily generator and the one-off event copy the activity's value; a series change re-plans it on the movable sessions only.
- **Reads.** The instant `gatheringAt = start_at − minutes` (`lib/gathering.ts`) on the session detail and list, every calendar entry, the attendance list, the public session page and `activities.scheduleDirectory`, so a moved session keeps its offset.
- **Writes.** Activity and event writes take `gatheringMinutesBefore`; `PATCH /api/activities/occurrences/{id}` takes `gatheringAt` (null removes it) and detaches the session like a move; at or after the start answers 422 `occurrence_gathering_after_start`, more than 12 hours before it 422 `occurrence_gathering_too_early`. The B-15 move form sends `gatheringAt` only when the field changed.
- **Display.** "Gathering {time}" before the time range on `/club` Today, the calendar list (`CalendarItem.gatheringAt`), the session page, the attendance list header and the public session page; nothing without one. The share text (`activities.shareText.*WithGathering`) and `communication.session_cancelled` (optional `gatheringAt` parameter) name it. The in-app cancellation notice and the one-off event form (B-14 events) do not carry it yet.

## Public schedule (#91)

`activities.publicSchedule` (club spec phase 2 step 9, DD-28, DD-29, REQ-025; `services/public-schedule.ts`, pure rules in `lib/public-schedule.ts`) is the optional block provider `club_core` reads with `hasRegistration` for a club that publishes `description_schedule`. On a section page (key `schedule`) it returns, for each public group `groups.directory.publicGroups` names in the section, the weekdays, times and place of every active series that has not ended and the next three sessions of those series, a cancelled one flagged (`cancelled: true`; the page strikes it through). On the club page it returns the upcoming one-off activities (no RRULE) of public groups in the public sections `club_core` passes (`PublicClubBlockContext.sections`), at most ten, with date, times, place, section and type. Every query carries the tenant and organization of the slug lookup; no roster, participant or coach enters the block.
