## Record scope

`personScope` turns the person linked to the user into grants: `club_admin` (an active club_admin assignment), `coordinator` (the sections of active coordinator assignments), `guardian` (active wards), `self`. A `coach` grant carries the groups of the person's active `coach`/`assistant` memberships, which the optional `groups` contribution returns (#30); without `groups`, or for a `coach` role assignment without a membership, no coach grant is returned. Reads use the foundation's `scopeWhere` (lists) and `assertInScope` (details) with one list of grant kinds and options per resource (`api/shared.ts`); commands use `authorizeScoped` with their allowed kinds, least-privileged first. Lists disable the platform's full export, which would skip `buildFilters`.

Persons and guardianships are read by the person (`self`), a guardian of the ward, the club administrator and — club-wide through `clubWideKinds: ['coordinator']` — a section coordinator, who gets the basic data only (`access: 'basic'`: no e-mail, phone, notes, PESEL or consents; club spec *Users, Permissions, and Scope*). The acting role of the read picks the view, so the coordinator kind comes last. Consents are returned only with `people.manage` or to a guardian of the ward: the guardianship list never projects them and adds them in `afterList` for the allowed rows (fail closed).

## Guardianships and consents

The club administrator links a guardian and a ward (relation, primary payer, consents, period), changes them and ends the guardianship through `valid_to`. At most one primary payer per ward at a time: overlapping periods → 409 `guardianship_primary_payer_conflict`, checked under the ward's row lock (concurrent writes are serialized) and backed by the partial unique index for open-ended rows; an undo that would recreate an overlap is refused the same way. A guardian of the ward or the administrator changes the consents (`set_consents`; the ward, a coordinator or a former guardian → 403); each change follows the P-08 transitions (a granted consent keeps its date, withdrawing one never granted changes nothing) and records `at` and `by_person_id`; the consents of an ended guardianship → 409 `guardianship_ended`. Role assignments of the same person, role and section whose periods overlap → 409 `role_assignment_duplicate`, checked under the person's row lock.

## Roles

A person's `auth` roles are always the full desired set: the roles outside `vereino_*`, the roles of the active assignments (`roleCatalogue` implications), `vereino_member` for an account and `vereino_payer` for an adult active guardian or an adult active participant of a group (`isPayerEligible`; the participant part through the optional `groups` contribution, #30). The subscriber syncs one person after each change (`groups:sync-member-roles` asks `people.roleSync` after a membership change); the daily job recomputes everyone with an account (validity starts and ends, 18th birthdays) and logs `{ processed, updated, unchanged, failed, skipped }`; a failure is retried by the queue or the next run. Coordinators read the club's people records club-wide, basic data only (since #26).

## Accounts

`people.persons.create_account` applies `accountAgePolicy` with `club_core`'s `own_account_min_age`, calls the foundation's `createAccount` with the person's desired roles (so an assignment saved earlier is granted with the account), writes `user_id` and emits `people.person.account_created`. The invitation is rendered in the request locale: `auth.users.create` takes no locale and offers no hook, so it cannot follow the recipient's `preferred_locale` yet (foundation AC-F04 deviation). A failed invitation e-mail returns `warning: invite_email_failed`; the platform's `POST /api/auth/users/resend-invite` resends it and needs `auth.users.create`, which no Vereino role carries yet.

## Member side (#27)

`GET /api/people/me` returns the person linked to the session's user in the session's organization (404 `person_not_linked` otherwise), the active wards from the scope provider's `guardian` grant with their guardianship consents and lock tokens, the active staff roles, the acting contexts, the resolved locale (DD-34: `preferred_locale` → club `default_locale` → the request's locale → `en`), the club's short name and time zone, and `paymentsVisible`. `PUT /api/people/me` (`people.persons.update_self`) changes the language (unknown → 422 `locale_unsupported`), phone, e-mail and the two consents — `true` gives with the instant, `false` withdraws (P-08); the audit log keeps the consent dates before and after and the changed consents. The `/club` pages write the resolved locale into the platform's `locale` cookie (`POST /api/auth/locale`), so the interface follows the member; club content stays as the club wrote it.

**R10.** A minor never sees payments: `vereino_payer` (`payments.view_own`) is granted only to adults, and `people.paymentsGate.assert` — which every own-payments route of phase 4 calls after `requireFeatures: ['payments.view_own']` — re-checks the person's age on the club's today, so a manually granted feature still answers 403 `payments_minor`. `paymentsVisible` comes from the same gate; the payments tile itself arrives with phase 4.

## Bulk import (#28)

Club spec REQ-036, DD-38, Q-044, TEST-051. The club administrator (`people.manage` + `people.pesel.view`) picks the club's CSV (UTF-8 with or without BOM, `,` or `;`, RFC 4180 quoting) or XLSX (first sheet; shared and inline strings, numbers, booleans, cached formula values, Excel serial dates where the column is a date); the page reads the header in the browser with the same pure reader and suggests the mapping from English or Polish column names. `POST /api/people/imports` re-reads the file from its bytes (5 MB, 1 000 data rows, 100 columns; a zip with more than 500 entries or more than 30 MB inflated is refused), stores it in the private partition (owner `people:person_import`, which the platform also records as the file's only assignment, so the purge can release it) and stages every row:

- one row = the participant, up to two guardians and a group label; a value that does not parse is `error` with the column (never the value): `import_date_invalid`, `import_pesel_invalid`, `import_email_invalid`, `import_value_too_long`, `import_name_missing`;
- a person is matched to the club only by `pesel_hash` or `external_id_hash`; a person without an identifier only through a matched relative of the same row (a guardian of the matched participant, or a ward of a matched guardian, with the same name) — never by name alone; a changed surname with the same PESEL is `matched` and the commit updates the surname;
- a row without any PESEL or identifier is `review` (`identifier_missing`) and waits for a decision: match to an existing person, create, or skip; an identified row can only be skipped;
- a row repeating an earlier one is `import_row_duplicate`; an identifier seen earlier with another name or birth date, or a PESEL and an identifier naming two members, is `import_identifier_conflict`; inside the file, the same identifier is one person, a guardian without one is the same person when names and e-mail (or phone) agree, a participant when names and birth date agree;
- the first guardian becomes the primary payer when the ward has none.

`POST …/commit` re-plans every row against the club as it is then (inside the commit's transaction), refuses a `review` row without a decision (422), and runs `people.persons.create|update` and `people.guardianships.create` inside that one transaction with the events and index updates deferred until it commits, so the import is written once or not at all; the same file imported again matches everyone and creates nothing. A row with a group keeps `group_name`; what becomes of its membership is in `membership_status` (below). `people:purge-import-rows` deletes the rows and the file 30 days after the commit or the cancellation (a preview left open that long is cancelled and purged); a row whose membership still waits keeps only its ids and the group label, and a waiting row processed later by a re-run is deleted by the next purge.

### Group memberships from the import (#65)

Club spec REQ-036, DD-38, D-026, AC-036. `groups` registers the optional hook `groups.membershipImporter` (a protected DI token); `people` declares its contract in `services/membership-importer.ts` and reads it with `hasRegistration`, so `people` never imports `groups`:

- `resolveGroups(em, scope, names)` → per name `resolved` (with the group id), `group_not_found` or `group_ambiguous`. A name matches a live (`deleted_at` null), active (`is_active`) group of the club — the import's tenant and organization only — after Unicode NFC normalization and trimming, case-insensitive; inner spaces and punctuation count. One group: resolved; none, or a blank name: `group_not_found`; several (e.g. the same name in two sections): `group_ambiguous`. A group is never created and never guessed.
- `ensureParticipantMemberships(ctx, scope, items)` → per item `created` or `existed` (with the membership id), `person_missing` or `group_not_found`, plus `emitSideEffects()`. It locks the group's row, treats a live participant membership of the person in the group that is open on `valid_from` or starts later as `existed` (the overlap the membership command would refuse), and otherwise runs `groups.memberships.create` nested in the import's transaction (`transactionalEm`, `bulkImport` suppression, no request), so the scope check, the overlap rule and the undoable audit entry are the command's; its `groups.membership.created` events are emitted after the import's transaction commits.

`membership_status` of a committed row with a group (`lib/import-rules.ts`): waiting — `groups_not_enabled` (no hook; the behaviour of #28), `group_not_found`, `group_ambiguous`; done — `membership_created`, `membership_existed`, `participant_missing` (the participant was deleted before the hook ran). The waiting value is the reason; no column was added. The preview (`GET …/rows` of an open import) shows per row what the commit would do (`membershipPreview`: `group_found`, `group_not_found`, `group_ambiguous` or `groups_not_enabled`); an unresolved group does not block the commit — the person and the guardianship are written and only the membership waits. The membership starts on the commit's day in the club zone (`valid_from`), open-ended, role `participant`.

`POST …/commit` on a committed import with waiting rows and the hook registered re-runs only those rows' memberships (through the same path) and writes nothing else; it is idempotent. Without waiting rows, or without the hook, it answers 409 `import_already_committed` as before. The gates stay `people.manage` + `people.pesel.view` and the `club_admin` grant; no `groups` feature is needed — the import is the administrator's write and the nested command checks the administrator's grant (`club_admin` may write every membership). The import's summary counts `membershipsCreated`, `membershipsExisting` (over the commit and its re-runs) and `membershipsPending`; the detail page shows each row's outcome and offers "Create memberships" for a committed import with waiting rows (`canCreateMemberships`).
