## Record scope

`personScope` turns the person linked to the user into grants: `club_admin` (an active club_admin assignment), `coordinator` (the sections of active coordinator assignments), `guardian` (active wards), `self`. A `coach` grant carries group ids, which come from memberships (#30); none is returned yet, so coaches see only their own records. Reads use the foundation's `scopeWhere` (lists) and `assertInScope` (details) with one list of grant kinds and options per resource (`api/shared.ts`); commands use `authorizeScoped` with their allowed kinds, least-privileged first. Lists disable the platform's full export, which would skip `buildFilters`.

Persons and guardianships are read by the person (`self`), a guardian of the ward, the club administrator and — club-wide through `clubWideKinds: ['coordinator']` — a section coordinator, who gets the basic data only (`access: 'basic'`: no e-mail, phone, notes, PESEL or consents; club spec *Users, Permissions, and Scope*). The acting role of the read picks the view, so the coordinator kind comes last. Consents are returned only with `people.manage` or to a guardian of the ward: the guardianship list never projects them and adds them in `afterList` for the allowed rows (fail closed).

## Guardianships and consents

The club administrator links a guardian and a ward (relation, primary payer, consents, period), changes them and ends the guardianship through `valid_to`. At most one primary payer per ward at a time: overlapping periods → 409 `guardianship_primary_payer_conflict`, checked under the ward's row lock (concurrent writes are serialized) and backed by the partial unique index for open-ended rows; an undo that would recreate an overlap is refused the same way. A guardian of the ward or the administrator changes the consents (`set_consents`; the ward, a coordinator or a former guardian → 403); each change follows the P-08 transitions (a granted consent keeps its date, withdrawing one never granted changes nothing) and records `at` and `by_person_id`; the consents of an ended guardianship → 409 `guardianship_ended`. Role assignments of the same person, role and section whose periods overlap → 409 `role_assignment_duplicate`, checked under the person's row lock.

## Roles

A person's `auth` roles are always the full desired set: the roles outside `vereino_*`, the roles of the active assignments (`roleCatalogue` implications), `vereino_member` for an account and `vereino_payer` for an adult active guardian (`isPayerEligible`). The subscriber syncs one person after each change; the daily job recomputes everyone with an account (validity starts and ends, 18th birthdays) and logs `{ processed, updated, unchanged, failed, skipped }`; a failure is retried by the queue or the next run. Coordinators read the club's people records club-wide, basic data only (since #26).

## Accounts

`people.persons.create_account` applies `accountAgePolicy` with `club_core`'s `own_account_min_age`, calls the foundation's `createAccount` with the person's desired roles (so an assignment saved earlier is granted with the account), writes `user_id` and emits `people.person.account_created`. The invitation is rendered in the request locale: `auth.users.create` takes no locale. A failed invitation e-mail returns `warning: invite_email_failed`; the platform's `POST /api/auth/users/resend-invite` resends it and needs `auth.users.create`, which no Vereino role carries yet.
