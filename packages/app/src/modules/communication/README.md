# `communication` — announcements, replies, the session-cancellation thread, the share text, the member inbox, cases and members' direct threads

Issue #35 (E04-S01), foundation III phase B ([`.ai/specs/2026-10-27-vereino-foundation-messaging-primitives.md`](../../../../../.ai/specs/2026-10-27-vereino-foundation-messaging-primitives.md): REQ-M05…REQ-M08, REQ-M10; DD-M01, DD-M06…DD-M14; P-32; J-M01, J-M02) for the club spec's phase 3 (REQ-009, the _Thread_ rule, DD-17, J-003 steps 1, 2, 4 and 5); issue #36 (E04-S02), foundation III phase C (REQ-M09, DD-M11, J-M03) for the club spec's REQ-010 (cases); issue #138, foundation III phase D (REQ-M12, DD-M18: a member's direct thread to the staff). Recipients come from the foundation's `resolveAudience` (P-29), delivery from its `dispatch` (P-30) with the templates of `emails/` (P-31); this module sends no e-mail and runs no recipient query of its own. Requires `people`, `groups` and `club_core` (`index.ts` `requires`), reached through DI and ids; `activities` is optional (`activities.scheduleDirectory`, `activities.shareText` read with `hasRegistration`: without it the activity and session kinds answer 422 `audience_kind_unavailable` and the session share text 404).

## Rules

- **Who posts where.** `club` — the administrator; `section` — its coordinator and the administrator; `group`, `activity`, `session` — also a coach of the group (a coach posting to a section gets 403 `scope_denied`); `persons` (`direct`) — the persons inside the author's scope (the `people` provider). The composer offers only the kinds and targets the author's grants allow (`GET /api/communication/audience/targets`); an unknown or foreign target answers 403 like one outside the grants. The author must be a person of the club.
- **Recipients (DD-M06).** The club spec _Thread_ rule through `resolveAudience`: members with an account, the active guardians of minor members, the coaches; account holders in the app and by e-mail, others by e-mail, the author excluded. The participants are the author and every person a message addressed (added, never removed).
- **Replies (DD-M12, R15).** A staff reply (the author, a participant reached as a coach, the section's coordinator, the administrator) reaches every participant; a member's reply is stored with `audience = staff` and reaches the thread's staff participants only (the author, the coaches, staff who replied — the coordinators of the section see the thread but are e-mailed only once they take part). A member sees the messages for everyone and their household's replies (themself, their wards and the wards' guardians), never another family's.
- **Visibility.** Participants ∪ the coordinators of the thread's section ∪ the administrator; `as=member` lists the caller's own threads only (the inbox).
- **Thread list (B-18, #103).** Filters `scopeType` and `sectionId`: a section lists its own threads and those of its groups, activities and sessions through the `section_id` snapshot every section, group, activity and session thread carries (club threads and staff direct threads carry none; a member's direct thread carries its group's section); the page offers the sections the reader oversees (the composer's section targets), and a section outside the reader's grants lists nothing — not even threads they take part in. Each item carries the number of messages the reader sees and `lastMessage` (author and time of the newest of them, the reader's own included; a member never sees another family's, R15).
- **History (DD-M10).** An edit appends a `revision`, a deletion a `tombstone`; the thread shows the latest revision with "edited" / "deleted". The author, the section's coordinator or the administrator may edit or delete; a system message cannot be edited (409 `message_not_editable`), a deleted message not again (409 `message_deleted`). The thread page offers "Delete message" where the read model's `canDelete` says so (never on a case), behind a confirmation and with the thread's version; the action log records `communication.messages.delete`.
- **Cases (REQ-M09, DD-M11; club spec REQ-010, R01).** The coordinator of the thread's section or the administrator (feature `communication.cases.manage`; a coach without it gets 403) marks a thread as a case with the thread's version, after a confirmation on the thread page: `case_opened_at`, `case_opened_by_person_id` and, in the action log, the acting role are recorded, and `communication.case.opened` is emitted. The marking is a conditional update (`WHERE is_case = false`): a second one answers 409 `case_already_open`, and there is neither an unmark command nor an undo. From then on every edit or deletion of its messages answers 409 `case_immutable` — for the author, the coordinator and the administrator alike — while replies are still appended; the full history (messages, revisions, tombstones) stays. A case keeps the thread's visibility: its participants, the section's coordinator and the administrator; another section's coordinator gets 403. The thread page shows a read-only banner with who marked it and when; `/backend/communication/cases` lists the cases the reader sees (`GET /api/communication/threads?isCase=1`).
- **Unread (DD-M13).** A read marker per thread and person, moved forward when the thread is opened and when the person replies; unread = a message the reader may see, written by someone else, newer than the marker. The tile counts the caller's own threads.
- **Session cancellation (REQ-M07).** `activities.occurrence.cancelled` → under the `system_job` source of the event's organization: the session thread (created once per occurrence under an advisory lock), a system message with the reason, and the e-mail `communication.session_cancelled` to the session audience (e-mail only — account holders without an address are left to `activities`' in-app notice; the canceller is left out). The dispatch is keyed by the cancellation event (`communication:cancel:<sessionId>:<updatedAt>`), so a replayed or concurrent delivery adds nothing; a later cancellation of the reopened session adds a message and one more e-mail. Since #139 the event names the canceller (`actorPersonId`, `actingRole`): the system message is a `cancellation` row with the canceller as author and their role (still a system message — never edited, deleted or unread for its author), the thread shows "Cancelled by {name} ({role}): {reason}", the e-mail carries `cancelledByName` / `cancelledByRole`, and a coach's cancellation also e-mails the live coordinators of the section (`people.personDirectory.sectionCoordinatorIdsOn`, reason `coach`, never the canceller), who join the thread as participants.
- **Share text (DD-M14).** A session thread's text comes from `activities.shareText`: the activity, the date, the time and, when cancelled, the status, in the club's language, with the public session page `/p/[clubSlug]/s/[occurrenceId]` (the member calendar when the club publishes no public page). Any other thread's text is its subject with the member link `/club/messages/[threadId]` (sign-in).
- **Delivery status.** Under every message of the thread page, for the thread's author, the section's coordinator and the administrator: the message's `deliveryCounts` by status (the foundation's `dispatch.countsOf`) and, behind "Show recipients" (open for the first message), its `DeliveryStatusList` (P-32, `messageId`): per recipient and channel `sent`, `queued`, `failed` ("not delivered" with the coded reason) or `skipped`, never an address.
- **Member inbox on the phone (E06-S20, #102; README M-06, M-07).** `/club/messages` marks a case with "Case", names the last author ("scope · author · time") and, for a guardian, filters by ward (`wardId`: the ward's groups' threads — with their activities' and sessions' through `group_id` —, those groups' sections' own threads and the direct threads the ward takes part in; never the club-wide threads, even those the ward itself receives; a person who is not the caller's ward lists nothing; memberships of any period count). Anyone with `communication.post` gets "New announcement": the backend composer embedded in a dialog, "To" limited to the author's own sections, groups and sessions (`lib/member-inbox.ts`), a toast "Sent to {n} people (in the app + e-mail)" from the `recipientCount` of `POST /api/communication/threads`, then the new thread. `/club/messages/[threadId]` shows the case mark and banner, "Copy text for group chat" for the thread's staff, and under the reader's own messages "E-mail: {n} delivered · {m} not delivered" (`emailCounts`: the e-mail channel of `dispatch.countsOf`; sent = delivered, failed and skipped = not delivered, queued = on the way). Subject 1–200 and message up to 10 000 characters stay the contract (Q1 of #102).
- **Direct threads by members (REQ-M12, DD-M18).** A guardian or a participant with an account starts a thread from `/club/messages/new` ("Write to the coach") about a member of their household — themself (`self` grant) or a ward (`guardian` grant), else 403 `scope_denied` — who holds a live `participant` membership of the chosen group today (club time; else 403 `scope_denied`), addressed to the group's coaches and assistants today (`to = coaches`) or to the live coordinators of its section (`to = coordinator`, `people.personDirectory.sectionCoordinatorIdsOn`); the author is never among the addressed staff, and none left answers 422 `direct_no_recipients`. The audience is the subject (`direct`) and the staff (`coach`) through the foundation's `resolveMembers`: a minor adds their active guardians and is reached only with an account; the author is excluded. The thread has the scope `direct`, no `scope_id`, the group's `section_id` and — for `coaches` — its `group_id`, so the section's coordinators and the administrator see it and may mark it as a case; its participants are the author (`author`), the household with their reasons and the addressed staff with theirs plus `staff`, so a household member's reply reaches them. The first message (audience `all`, template `communication.announcement`) reaches the staff in the app and by e-mail. A member starts at most 20 direct threads in 24 hours, counted under an advisory lock on (organization, author) over the `direct` threads with a section (staff `persons` threads carry none and do not count); the 21st answers 429 `direct_thread_limit`. No attachments. `GET /api/communication/direct` lists the household's groups today with the staff names only; the inbox offers "Write to the coach" when one of them has staff.
