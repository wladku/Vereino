## Scope

Club settings are organization-wide: read with the feature, written by the club administrator. Sections are listed through `scopeWhere` with the kinds `coordinator` (column `id`, the coordinator's `section_ids`) and `club_admin`; places and institutions carry no section, so only the club administrator's grant lists them (a coordinator gets an empty list; the option-source routes of the club spec arrive with their first consumer in phase 2). Every write is the club administrator's.

## Coordinators

Coordinators are `people` role assignments (`coordinator` + `section_id`); `club_core` never stores or imports them. `people` owns the response enricher `people.section-coordinators` on the section list (`enrichers.entityId: 'club_core:section'`), which adds `_people: { coordinators, coordinatorMissing }` — current and future assignments with the person's name, and a warning flag for an active section without an active coordinator (the section still works). The section edit page assigns and ends coordinators through `/api/people/role-assignments`.

## Places

A parent must be a live place of the club and must not be the place itself or a descendant (422 `place_hierarchy_cycle`); hierarchy writes of a club take a transaction-scoped advisory lock, so two concurrent moves cannot close a loop. A place with live children is not deleted (409), and an institution that owns a live place is not deleted (409).

## Public pages (#29)

The public pages run without a session. `clubCorePublicSite` makes exactly one read-only, index-backed system-scope query — the club settings row by its globally unique `public_slug` (AGENTS.md exception D-024/D-026, DD-35; V-12 still finds no installed slug contract) — and narrows every later query to the tenant and organization it found. An unknown slug, a section that is not public (or inactive, deleted, without a slug, or of another club), an internal material and a file the club does not publish answer the same 404 `public_page_not_found`; a club with its page off answers 404 `public_page_disabled` with nothing about the club. A block is published when the club enables it and the record is public; a disabled block is absent from the response. Logos and materials are stored in the private `privateAttachments` partition through `attachmentService` and served to guests by the public routes, which read exactly the file the slug points to (owner, assignment and partition must match); staff read them through the platform's `/api/attachments/image|file/{id}`. The effective section logo is the section's own, else the club's, else the initial of the club name. The club logo is passed to the backend AppShell header (`src/app/(backend)/backend/layout.tsx`; an organization branding logo of the platform still wins) and to the `/club` header (`GET /api/people/me` → `club.logoUrl`).

## Deferred

Deleting or deactivating a section that has groups (409, `groups`, phase 2); deleting a place with sessions or bookings (`activities`, `facilities`); the groups, schedule, instructor, price and facility blocks (their providers arrive with phases 2, 4 and 5); `publish_name_consent_at`; markdown rendering of the public description; a clean-up of logo and material files no longer referenced (they are kept so an undo finds them); the section logo next to groups in the `groups` pages; `opening_hours` (V-7), regulations, `is_public_schedule`, `accepts_online_requests`; coach contracts (A05); `search.ts` (no club_core entity is listed for search, and a global-search index of sections would bypass the coordinator's record scope).
