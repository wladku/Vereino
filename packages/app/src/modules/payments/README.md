## Generation

`payments.charges.generate { period: 'YYYY-MM' }` (12 months back to 12 ahead of the club month) runs in one transaction under the club's advisory lock, tried, not awaited: a held lock answers 409 `generation_in_progress`. For every fee valid in the period it reads the participants of its group (or of its activity's group) on the reference day — today in the current month, the first day of a future month, the last day of a past one — and creates one charge per fee, participant and label (`YYYY-MM`; `one_off` for one-off fees, so a camp is charged once; a per-session fee is `amount × sessions not cancelled` in the month and creates nothing without sessions). The payer is the primary-payer guardian active that day; an adult without one pays for themselves; otherwise the charge is `payer_missing`. Existing live charges are skipped and counted; they also take part in the sibling ranking, which runs per payer over all sections: debtors with a charge of a covered fee period are ranked by birth date (unknown last), the full-rate child (`oldest` by default) pays in full and every other one is discounted. When several rules match, the largest discount wins (ties by rule name, then id) and its rule is stored on the charge.

Undo (the owner's default of 2026-11-05 on #108) cancels the run's charges that are still `open` (`cancel_reason = 'generation_undone'`), never deletes them, and marks the run undone; the period can then be generated again.

The generation list (#403, owner decision (a) of 2026-11-19): `GET /api/payments/charge-generations` (`payments.manage`) lists every run, manual or automatic, newest first, with `open` — the charges an undo would still cancel — and `cancelled`/`undoneAt` once undone; `POST /api/payments/charge-generations/{id}/undo` → `payments.charge_generations.undo` runs the same undo for any run (version in the platform header; audited, not undoable; 409 `generation_already_undone`, `generation_in_progress`). The page `/backend/payments/generations` (link "Generations" on B-21) offers it as a row action, so an automatic run, whose audit entry has no undo, can be undone too.

## Scheduled jobs

Since #301 (payments parity spec phase 11, REQ-C13, DD-C08, DD-C09) `setup.ts` registers six schedules per organization through `registerModuleSchedule` (`services/club-setup.ts`; upsert on stable ids, so a second `seedDefaults` registers nothing twice): `payments:generate-charges` daily at 06:00 and `payments:overdue-reminders` daily at 09:00 in the club's time zone, `payments:reconcile-checkouts` and `payments:close-order-campaigns` every 15 minutes, and `payments:purge-order-links` once a day (engineering standards S9.5, #525: `payments.order_links.purge_dead` deletes the order links that expired 30 days ago or earlier; a run that deleted nothing writes no audit entry), and `payments:verify-invariants` nightly at 03:00 in the club's time zone (club spec REQ-043, #531; below). Each worker in `workers/` only dispatches its job command with `jobCommandContext` (grant `system_job`, input `{ now? }`):

- `payments.charges.generate_due` generates the current month with the same writer and advisory lock as `payments.charges.generate` when `lib/payments-rules.ts` `generationDue` says so: `auto_generate_charges` on, the month later than the one migration B (`Migration20261118201338`) was applied in (its `executed_at` in `mikro_orm_migrations_payments`, Q-C07; `data/queries.ts` `automaticGenerationSince` takes the earliest recorded migration whose name sorts at or after migration B's, so after a greenfield rebuild with regenerated migration names it is the first of those — the module's install — instead of an unknown month that stops the generation for good, #405), today on or after `generation_day` (1–28), and no generation of the month yet — an undone one counts, so an undone month is never regenerated. A held lock or a fee over its limit generates nothing and the next daily run looks again; so does a plan that would create no charge and skip none (a new club without fee schedules or rosters yet): it records no generation and the outcome reason is `nothing_to_generate` (`automaticGenerationRecorded`, #666). The manual generation still records an empty run. The run is audited, not undoable from the audit log (no user owns its undo); the administrator undoes it from the generation list (#403).
- `payments.charges.remind_overdue` (`services/overdue-reminders.ts`): a payer is reminded when one of their charges is due (`reminderDue`: the day after the due date, then every 7 days, at most 4); one dispatch of `payments.charge_reminder` per payer lists all their overdue charges below the cap, with the payer code, by e-mail and as the in-app notice `payments.charge_overdue` (`notifications.ts`). The marker (`reminder_count`, `last_reminded_at`) moves only on the listed charges due a reminder, so each charge keeps its own cadence (#404), in the enqueueing transaction by a conditional UPDATE on the count read. Rental charges and charges without a payer are never reminded; off when `overdue_reminders` is off.
- `payments.checkouts.reconcile` and `payments.order_campaigns.close_due` wrap `reconcileCheckouts` and `closeOrderCampaigns`, which the operator commands `payments reconcile-checkouts` and `payments close-order-campaigns` still call directly. A run that changed nothing (`lib/payments-rules.ts` `sweepChangedNothing`: no checkout completed, failed or expired and no error; no campaign closed) writes no audit entry, so a quiet club does not collect ~192 empty entries a day (#405); the facilities sweeps write none at all.
- `payments.invariants.verify` (`services/invariants.ts`, rules in `lib/invariants.ts`; engineering standards S10.6) only reads: it recomputes every live charge's net paid amount from its live payments (reversing entries included, overpayments to refund excluded) and checks the stored status against `settledStatus` (a charge with nothing due may also be `paid`; a cancelled or forfeited charge must have nothing paid; paid may never be negative or above the amount due), and checks that the club's `KP` receipt and `RG` handover numbers are well formed, issued once (a `KP` number is shared by the payments of one acceptance), in the month of their day and without gaps per month. Drift is an operations alert (`docs/ops/monitoring.md`): an `error` log line `Payments invariants drift` with the token `vereino.ops.payments.invariants.drift`, the drifted charge ids with the stored and expected status and the numbering problems (ids and counts only, at most 50 of each), and a ping of `<VEREINO_OPS_PAYMENTS_INVARIANTS_HEARTBEAT_URL>/fail`; a clean run logs `Payments invariants verified` and pings the URL. It writes no audit entry and changes no data.

The subscriber `payments:reregister-schedules` (`subscribers/reregister-schedules.ts`, persistent) re-registers the six schedules on `club_core.settings.updated`, so after a time zone change the generation, the reminders and the invariant check fire at 06:00, 09:00 and 03:00 of the new zone (#405); a failed registration throws and the event is retried.

B-20 "Coaches and payments" carries the group "Automatic charges and reminders" (`autoGenerateCharges`, `generationDay`, `overdueReminders` on `GET/PUT /api/payments/settings`; defaults on, 1, on). `yarn mercato payments set-up-club --tenant <id> --org <id>` registers a club's six schedules (the backfill for clubs created before #301 or without `seedDefaults`, e.g. through the API — a step of the deployment checklist in [`docs/ops/deploy.md`](../../../../../docs/ops/deploy.md)); `yarn mercato payments run-job --job <generate-charges|overdue-reminders|reconcile-checkouts|close-order-campaigns|purge-order-links|verify-invariants> --tenant <id> --org <id> [--now <ISO>]` runs one job now.

## Charge ledger

A payment is an immutable credit of a charge; what was paid is the sum of the charge's payments, reversing entries included, and the charge's status is derived from it (`open` → `partially_paid` → `paid`; `cancelled` and `forfeited` stay). Recording takes at most what remains and never a future date. Cancelling takes an `open` charge with nothing paid and a required reason, kept on the charge and in the audit log's context. Reversing (the owner's default of 2026-11-06 on #109) books a negative entry of the same charge and method, dated today, with the reason as its title; once per payment, never for an `online` payment (refunded, DD-42) or a reversing entry. Overdue is derived: `open` or `partially_paid` past the due date in the club's calendar.

Refunds (payments parity phase 6, REQ-C08, DD-C10; #296): B-22 gives an online payment with something left to refund the row action "Refund" (`components/RefundDialog.tsx`) over the unchanged `POST /api/payments/payments/{id}/refund`. The detail read adds, per payment, `refundable` (an online payment through the gateway less its refunds; 0 otherwise) and `refunds[]` (the online reversing entries naming it), both from `lib/charge-ledger.ts`. The dialog suggests the refundable part, requires a reason and sends one `operationId` per opening, so a retry returns the same refund; 422 `refund_exceeds_remaining` marks the amount field; 502 `gateway_unavailable` books nothing, keeps the dialog open and refreshes the record (the command renews the charge's lock token before it calls the gateway). A late capture on a terminal gateway transaction always answers 502 (base spec step 4, known gap 1). `GET /api/payments/charges?toRefund=1` lists the charges holding an online overpayment not refunded yet (`data/queries.ts` `chargeIdsToRefund`) and every list response counts them in `meta.toRefundCount`; B-21 shows "To refund" only while the count is above 0 and leaves it for the default perspective once it is empty.

## Accept cash

"Accept cash" (#114, REQ-031; the B-21 dialog and `/club/cash`, M-15) takes cash for selected charges of one payer: `GET /api/payments/cash/payers` lists the payers with something to pay (wards, total due), `GET /api/payments/cash/charges` their charges oldest first, and `POST /api/payments/cash` (`payments.cash.record`) spreads the amount over them oldest first, issues one `KP/YYYY/MM/NNN` number per acceptance (sequential per club and month, under an advisory lock), records the collector and queues the `payments.cash_received` e-mail to the payer unless `notify` is false. `GET /api/payments/cash` is "Received by me today" with the total to hand over. Like every write of phase 4 it needs the administrator's grant; the coach's path is "My groups' payments" below and the handover to the treasurer #118.

One form serves every screen (payments parity DD-C03, #292): `components/AcceptCashButton.tsx` wraps the button and `AcceptCashDialog` (full screen under 640 px, Ctrl/Cmd+Enter saves), mounted on B-21, B-22, B-29, the B-32 order rows and the dashboard widget "Bank statements and cash". `CashAcceptForm` takes `payerPersonId` (B-22 opens with the charge's payer chosen through `GET /api/payments/cash/payers?personId=` and the focus on the amount; never for a rental charge, Q-C16), `chargeIds` (only those ticked, for the B-32 order row) and `origin` (`charges`, `charge`, `bank`, `order_campaign`, `dashboard`, `phone`), which `POST /api/payments/cash` keeps in the audit context of `payments.cash.record`.

## Order campaigns

Order campaigns (#115, REQ-032, DD-30; B-30…B-32) are the kit order forms. `/api/payments/order-campaigns` (`payments.orders.manage`) lists them with their KPIs and creates or changes one with its products nested: one section and its groups, the orders deadline (not in the past) and the payment deadline (not before it), print name and number, a notes question, single products with children's and adult sizes and prices and at most one set of at least two of them. The form is editable in `draft` and `open` only; a price change leaves placed orders as they are (their lines keep the price) and a product on an order cannot be removed (409 `order_product_in_use`). The stages `draft` → `open` → `closed` → `ordered` → `delivered` → `completed` move one at a time through `POST …/{id}/open|close|mark-ordered|mark-delivered|complete` (409 out of order). `…/{id}/summary` is the supplier summary (units per product and size, set items under their products, and the print rows; `format=csv`), `…/{id}/missing` the children without an order, `…/{id}/remind` e-mails their guardians (`payments.order_reminder`) and `…/{id}/share-text` the Messenger text. `yarn mercato payments close-order-campaigns` closes the open campaigns past their deadline; since #301 the `payments:close-order-campaigns` schedule does it every 15 minutes.

Guardians order kit (#116, M-16, M-17, M-19) through `payments.orders.place|update|cancel` (`payments.orders.place`, held by `vereino_payer`; a minor gets 403 `payments_minor`): `GET /api/payments/my-orders` lists the campaigns covering their children with each child's state, `GET /api/payments/order-campaigns/{id}/form` is the form, `POST /api/payments/orders`, `PUT …/{id}` and `POST …/{id}/cancel` write it. An order creates an `order` charge (payer = the person ordering, due on the payment deadline) in the same transaction; a change follows the new total but never below what was paid (409 `order_below_paid`); only an unpaid order cancels (409 `order_cancel_paid`). The print number is unique in the child's group (422 naming who has it). After the deadline or once closed every write answers 409 `campaign_closed`. A guardian without a session asks for a personal link on `/club/orders/{id}?club=<slug>` (`POST /api/payments/public/order-campaigns/{id}/link-request`, always 202); the link (`payments_order_links`, foundation P-10, until the end of the deadline) opens `/club/o/{token}`, which orders through the same commands without online payment. A copy of each placed or changed order goes to the contact address (`payments.order_copy`).

## Bank statement

The statement import (#113, REQ-030; B-29 at `/backend/payments/bank`, feature `payments.bank.import`): `POST /api/payments/bank-imports` reads a CSV (the bank's columns, recognised by header or named in the column mapping) or an MT940 file (`lib/bank-statement.ts`), stores it in the private `attachments` partition and stores every fresh incoming transfer in `payments_bank_transactions` (sender, account and title encrypted) with its match (`lib/bank-matching.ts`): a payer code with an amount paying whole charges oldest first is recorded at once when `auto_book_bank_matches` is on; an overpayment, an underpayment or a whole-word surname with such an amount waits in To confirm; the rest is Unmatched. Debits and transfers imported before (same fingerprint) are counted and skipped. `POST /api/payments/bank-transactions/{id}/confirm|assign|ignore|restore|undo` decide on one transfer with its lock token; a recorded transfer's `transfer` payments carry `bank_transaction_id`, and Undo writes reversing entries. `GET /api/payments/bank-transactions` is the queue with the counts per status, `GET /api/payments/bank-imports` the history and `GET /api/payments/bank-payers` the Assign payer select with codes. Imports and decisions that move money take the club's statement advisory lock. Payments parity REQ-C04 (#294): B-29 shows each decision as a button in the row (folded into the row menu under 640 px; a decision moves the focus to the next row) and "Recorded automatically" for an `autoBooked` transfer; the history is a table whose items carry `importedBy`, `deletable` and `updatedAt`. `DELETE /api/payments/bank-imports/{id}` (`payments.bank_imports.delete`, with the import's lock token) removes an import none of whose transfers any payment row ever named (DD-C13, `deletableImports` in `lib/bank-matching.ts`) — its transactions, the import and its statement file — so the corrected file imports again; otherwise 409 `bank_import_has_bookings`. It emits `payments.bank_import.deleted` (id and counts), is audited with the file name and counts, and is not undoable.

## Rental charges

`payments.chargeService` (#291; payments parity spec REQ-C01, DD-C01, DD-C02; facilities DD-19; `services/charge-service.ts`, registered in `di.ts`): `facilities` resolves it with `hasRegistration` and calls it inside the rental's own transaction. `createForSource` writes one `rental` charge through the caller's `em` — `payee = owner_institution` with `payee_institution_id` (null reads "the facility owner"), the payer the first present of `payer_institution_id`, `payer_person_id` and the encrypted typed-in `payer_label`, no debtor, no discount; a free rental's charge is born `paid` — and returns `afterCommit`, which emits `payments.charge.created`. It is idempotent per rental (a second call returns the live charge; partial unique index `payments_charges_org_rental_unique`); no payer of any kind answers 422 `charge_payer_required` and an amount outside 0 … 1 000 000 zł or a malformed due date 422 `charge_amount_invalid`, so the rental rolls back. `cancelForSource` sets `cancelled` or `forfeited`, or leaves a charge with any payment as it is and returns the warning `charge_paid`. `statusOf(chargeIds, ctx)` returns the stored statuses of the caller's organization only. Tenant and organization always come from the caller's command context. B-21 shows "Rentals (municipality)" when `GET /api/payments/charges` answers `meta.rentalsPerspective` (`facilities` enabled, or a live rental charge), adds a Payee filter and shows institution and renter names; B-22 reads "to the club's account" or "to the account of {owner}". A rental charge is paid to the owner's account: `my-charges`, the payer's history, the Today tile and the transfer instructions leave it out, and the checkout answers 403 `checkout_charge_forbidden`.

## Manual charges and payers

A manual charge (#295; payments parity spec REQ-C06, DD-C12; `lib/manual-charges.ts`, `commands/ledger.ts`): `POST /api/payments/charges` runs `payments.charges.create_manual` — the debtor, a description of 3–200 characters, 1 grosz to PLN 100 000 and a due date from 12 months back to 12 months ahead of the club's today (422 `charge_due_date_out_of_range` with `from` and `to`), payable to the club, `source_type = manual`, no period. The payer is the one named (an active guardian of the debtor or the adult debtor, else 422 `charge_payer_not_eligible`) or follows the *Payer* rule (primary payer, else the adult debtor); otherwise the charge is created with `payer_missing`. It answers 201 `{ chargeId, payerMissing }`, emits `payments.charge.created` and is never edited or undone: a wrong one is cancelled and added again. "Add charge" opens `components/AddChargeDialog.tsx` on B-21 and on the person record's tab.

`POST /api/payments/charges/{id}/payer` runs `payments.charges.assign_payer` with the charge's lock token: an `open` or `partially_paid` charge with `payer_missing` gets one of the `payerOptions` the charge detail lists (409 `charge_payer_already_set`, `charge_not_payable`; 422 `charge_payer_not_eligible`) and emits `payments.charge.payer_assigned` `{ id, payerPersonId }`. B-22 shows "No payer" with "Assign payer" (`components/AssignPayerDialog.tsx`) and a link to the debtor's guardianship tab. The subscribers `payments:guardianship-payer-created` and `-updated` (persistent) run the same command with a worker context and the event's `wardPersonId` (Contract 6): the command re-reads the ward's current primary payer and assigns them to the ward's open charges without a payer, so a replay or a stale event changes nothing; an event without `wardPersonId` is ignored. Known gaps, as the spec allows (no other path than the events and B-22): a primary payer whose guardianship starts later is read on the event's day and is not assigned when it begins, and a manual charge committed while a primary-payer event is being handled may stay without a payer; B-22 assigns both.

The person record's "Charges" tab (REQ-C07, DD-C05) is the widget `payments.injection.person-charges` on the spot `vereino:people:person-record:charges` (feature `payments.view`): the B-21 columns over `GET /api/payments/charges?involvesPersonId=` (charges the person owes or pays), rows open B-22, "Add charge" with the person fixed.
