## Generation

`payments.charges.generate { period: 'YYYY-MM' }` (12 months back to 12 ahead of the club month) runs in one transaction under the club's advisory lock, tried, not awaited: a held lock answers 409 `generation_in_progress`. For every fee valid in the period it reads the participants of its group (or of its activity's group) on the reference day — today in the current month, the first day of a future month, the last day of a past one — and creates one charge per fee, participant and label (`YYYY-MM`; `one_off` for one-off fees, so a camp is charged once; a per-session fee is `amount × sessions not cancelled` in the month and creates nothing without sessions). The payer is the primary-payer guardian active that day; an adult without one pays for themselves; otherwise the charge is `payer_missing`. Existing live charges are skipped and counted; they also take part in the sibling ranking, which runs per payer over all sections: debtors with a charge of a covered fee period are ranked by birth date (unknown last), the full-rate child (`oldest` by default) pays in full and every other one is discounted. When several rules match, the largest discount wins (ties by rule name, then id) and its rule is stored on the charge.

Undo (the owner's default of 2026-11-05 on #108) cancels the run's charges that are still `open` (`cancel_reason = 'generation_undone'`), never deletes them, and marks the run undone; the period can then be generated again.
