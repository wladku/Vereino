## Generation

`payments.charges.generate { period: 'YYYY-MM' }` (12 months back to 12 ahead of the club month) runs in one transaction under the club's advisory lock, tried, not awaited: a held lock answers 409 `generation_in_progress`. For every fee valid in the period it reads the participants of its group (or of its activity's group) on the reference day — today in the current month, the first day of a future month, the last day of a past one — and creates one charge per fee, participant and label (`YYYY-MM`; `one_off` for one-off fees, so a camp is charged once; a per-session fee is `amount × sessions not cancelled` in the month and creates nothing without sessions). The payer is the primary-payer guardian active that day; an adult without one pays for themselves; otherwise the charge is `payer_missing`. Existing live charges are skipped and counted; they also take part in the sibling ranking, which runs per payer over all sections: debtors with a charge of a covered fee period are ranked by birth date (unknown last), the full-rate child (`oldest` by default) pays in full and every other one is discounted. When several rules match, the largest discount wins (ties by rule name, then id) and its rule is stored on the charge.

Undo (the owner's default of 2026-11-05 on #108) cancels the run's charges that are still `open` (`cancel_reason = 'generation_undone'`), never deletes them, and marks the run undone; the period can then be generated again.

## Charge ledger

A payment is an immutable credit of a charge; what was paid is the sum of the charge's payments, reversing entries included, and the charge's status is derived from it (`open` → `partially_paid` → `paid`; `cancelled` and `forfeited` stay). Recording takes at most what remains and never a future date. Cancelling takes an `open` charge with nothing paid and a required reason, kept on the charge and in the audit log's context. Reversing (the owner's default of 2026-11-06 on #109) books a negative entry of the same charge and method, dated today, with the reason as its title; once per payment, never for an `online` payment (refunded, DD-42) or a reversing entry. Overdue is derived: `open` or `partially_paid` past the due date in the club's calendar.

## Accept cash

"Accept cash" (#114, REQ-031; the B-21 dialog and `/club/cash`, M-15) takes cash for selected charges of one payer: `GET /api/payments/cash/payers` lists the payers with something to pay (wards, total due), `GET /api/payments/cash/charges` their charges oldest first, and `POST /api/payments/cash` (`payments.cash.record`) spreads the amount over them oldest first, issues one `KP/YYYY/MM/NNN` number per acceptance (sequential per club and month, under an advisory lock), records the collector and queues the `payments.cash_received` e-mail to the payer unless `notify` is false. `GET /api/payments/cash` is "Received by me today" with the total to hand over. Like every write of phase 4 it needs the administrator's grant; the coach's path is "My groups' payments" below and the handover to the treasurer #118.

## Payer code, Today and the dashboard

Phase 4 step 5 (#111, E07-S04; REQ-012, REQ-030; README M-02, M-08, B-01):

- **Payer code.** `PayerRef` / `payments_payer_refs` holds each payer's permanent `GW-XXXX` code, unique in the club (`lib/payer-summary.ts`, `services/payer-overview.ts`). It is assigned on first use under both unique indexes.
- **M-08.** `GET /api/payments/my-charges` adds `transfer` (the club's account from `club_core`'s `bank_account`, its name and the payer code; null for someone who pays nothing), `history` (the six most recently due paid charges) and each charge's `discountAmount`.
- **Today tile.** The body of the "Payments" tile on `/club` is the injection widget `payments.injection.club-today-payments` on the spot `vereino:club:today:payments`, fed by `GET /api/payments/my-charges/summary`. It shows the amount, the nearest due date and "Pay", or "Everything is paid", or "The guardian pays".
- **B-01 widgets.** The dashboard widgets `payments.dashboard.overdue` and `payments.dashboard.withoutPayer` read `GET /api/payments/dashboard` (`payments.view` and the `club_admin` grant) and open B-21 with `?perspective=overdue` or `?perspective=payer_missing`.

## Order campaigns

Order campaigns (#115, REQ-032, DD-30; B-30…B-32) are the kit order forms. `/api/payments/order-campaigns` (`payments.orders.manage`) lists them with their KPIs and creates or changes one with its products nested: one section and its groups, the orders deadline (not in the past) and the payment deadline (not before it), print name and number, a notes question, single products with children's and adult sizes and prices and at most one set of at least two of them. The form is editable in `draft` and `open` only; a price change leaves placed orders as they are (their lines keep the price) and a product on an order cannot be removed (409 `order_product_in_use`). The stages `draft` → `open` → `closed` → `ordered` → `delivered` → `completed` move one at a time through `POST …/{id}/open|close|mark-ordered|mark-delivered|complete` (409 out of order). `…/{id}/summary` is the supplier summary (units per product and size, set items under their products, and the print rows; `format=csv`), `…/{id}/missing` the children without an order, `…/{id}/remind` e-mails their guardians (`payments.order_reminder`) and `…/{id}/share-text` the Messenger text. `yarn mercato payments close-order-campaigns` closes the open campaigns past their deadline.

Guardians order kit (#116, M-16, M-17, M-19) through `payments.orders.place|update|cancel` (`payments.orders.place`, held by `vereino_payer`; a minor gets 403 `payments_minor`): `GET /api/payments/my-orders` lists the campaigns covering their children with each child's state, `GET /api/payments/order-campaigns/{id}/form` is the form, `POST /api/payments/orders`, `PUT …/{id}` and `POST …/{id}/cancel` write it. An order creates an `order` charge (payer = the person ordering, due on the payment deadline) in the same transaction; a change follows the new total but never below what was paid (409 `order_below_paid`); only an unpaid order cancels (409 `order_cancel_paid`). The print number is unique in the child's group (422 naming who has it). After the deadline or once closed every write answers 409 `campaign_closed`. A guardian without a session asks for a personal link on `/club/orders/{id}?club=<slug>` (`POST /api/payments/public/order-campaigns/{id}/link-request`, always 202); the link (`payments_order_links`, foundation P-10, until the end of the deadline) opens `/club/o/{token}`, which orders through the same commands without online payment. A copy of each placed or changed order goes to the contact address (`payments.order_copy`).

## Bank statement

The statement import (#113, REQ-030; B-29 at `/backend/payments/bank`, feature `payments.bank.import`): `POST /api/payments/bank-imports` reads a CSV (the bank's columns, recognised by header or named in the column mapping) or an MT940 file (`lib/bank-statement.ts`), stores it in the private `attachments` partition and stores every fresh incoming transfer in `payments_bank_transactions` (sender, account and title encrypted) with its match (`lib/bank-matching.ts`): a payer code with an amount paying whole charges oldest first is recorded at once when `auto_book_bank_matches` is on; an overpayment, an underpayment or a whole-word surname with such an amount waits in To confirm; the rest is Unmatched. Debits and transfers imported before (same fingerprint) are counted and skipped. `POST /api/payments/bank-transactions/{id}/confirm|assign|ignore|restore|undo` decide on one transfer with its lock token; a recorded transfer's `transfer` payments carry `bank_transaction_id`, and Undo writes reversing entries. `GET /api/payments/bank-transactions` is the queue with the counts per status, `GET /api/payments/bank-imports` the history and `GET /api/payments/bank-payers` the Assign payer select with codes. Imports and decisions that move money take the club's statement advisory lock.
