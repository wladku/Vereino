# Trademark guidelines (draft)

"Vereino" is to be registered as a trademark (D-035 point 5; owner action in epic #646: EUIPO or the Polish patent
office, classes 9 and 42). Until the registration is granted, these guidelines describe how Vereino will license the
name, and the partner agreement applies them by contract. The lawyer confirms the wording before they are published.

The code is free under AGPL-3.0-only; the **name is not**. Anyone may use, change and run the code under the licence;
only Vereino and the partners it licenses may use the name for a product or a service.

## The marks

| Mark | Who may use it |
|---|---|
| **Vereino** (the word) and the Vereino logo | the Vereino company; everyone for a plain reference (below) |
| **Vereino Partner**, **Vereino Certified Partner**, **Vereino Gold Partner** | partners of that tier, while the partner agreement is in force |
| **Vereino Implementer**, **Vereino Consultant** | people who hold the valid certificate ([`certification.md`](certification.md)) |
| the partner logo | partners, in the artwork Vereino supplies; its design and whether it shows the tier are *PD-13* |

## Partners may

- call themselves "Vereino Partner" (or their tier's title) on their website, offers, invoices, social media and
  business cards, next to their own name and logo;
- use the partner logo in the supplied artwork, unchanged, linked to the partner directory on `vereino.org`;
- describe their services truthfully: "set-up and training for Vereino", "import of your club's data into Vereino";
- name their certified people's titles ("Vereino Implementer") while the certificate is valid;
- show screenshots of Vereino for their own clubs, without personal data.

## Partners may not

- use a title above their tier, or any title after the agreement ends (a transition period to remove it is set in the
  agreement);
- use "Vereino" in their own company name, product name, domain name or social media handle (for example
  `vereino-krakow.pl`, "Vereino Pro", "VereinoSoft");
- change the logo, combine it with their own into a new sign, or use it bigger than their own;
- suggest they are Vereino, speak for it, or offer warranties or commitments in its name;
- sell, host or offer a fork, a modified copy or a rebranded copy as "Vereino", "based on Vereino and the same as
  Vereino", or in any way that makes a buyer believe it is the Vereino product;
- register a trademark or a domain that contains "Vereino" or something confusingly similar.

## Everyone (not only partners) may

- refer to Vereino by name to say what something is or works with: "compatible with Vereino", "we migrate clubs from
  spreadsheets to Vereino", "a review of Vereino";
- run, change and publish the code under AGPL-3.0-only, including forks;
- write about Vereino in articles, talks, courses and comparisons.

## Forks

- A fork is welcome under the licence, but it must carry **its own name** and not use the Vereino logo.
- Its README states that it is a fork and not the Vereino product: "Based on Vereino (`vereino.org`), not affiliated
  with or endorsed by Vereino."
- A fork may not be sold, hosted or advertised as Vereino (D-035 point 5). Inside the code, the module ids, package
  names and copyright notices may stay as they are; user-facing names (the product name in the interface, e-mails,
  the page title) must be changed when the fork is offered to others.

## Misuse

Vereino first asks the partner in writing to correct a misuse within a stated time. A partner that does not, or
misuses the name again, loses the right to the title and may lose the partnership ([`tiers.md`](tiers.md), *Out*).
Misuse by someone who is not a partner is handled by the owner with the lawyer.
