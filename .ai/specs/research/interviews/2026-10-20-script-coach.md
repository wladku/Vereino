# Interview with a coach

Introduction (1 min): "I want to understand what a coach's week looks like in a club like ours. I won't be selling or showing you anything."

1. **Tell me about the last training session before which you didn't know how many players would come.** → What did you do to find out? How many actually came vs how many signed up?
2. **When did you last have to find a substitute, or substitute for someone yourself?** → How did you arrange it? How was it counted in the settlement afterwards? Was there any dispute about it?
3. **How did you receive messages from parents in the last month?** → How many channels? What was the most annoying? What did you do with a difficult message (complaint, conflict)?
4. **What does your month-end settlement look like?** → Who counts the units? Where do they get the data? Has it ever not added up?
5. **What tools do you use to plan training sessions and to stay in touch with the group?** → Anything paid? What does the club give you? What have you tried and abandoned, and why?
6. **Tell me about the last away match: who knew what, and when?** → How did you put together the squad list, transport, parents on duty?
7. **What takes up most of your time in this job apart from the training itself?** → How much in the last week?
8. **Who else should I talk to? What should I have asked?**

**Bad data signals:** "nice idea" (compliment → go back: "and how did it go last time?"); "always / everyone" (generalization → "and the last time?"); "the app should have X" (idea → "what problem would that solve? when did you last have it?").

**Commitment at the end:** time — "Could you note down for a week how much time it takes you to handle attendance and messages?" or reputation — "Will you introduce me to a coach from another club?"
