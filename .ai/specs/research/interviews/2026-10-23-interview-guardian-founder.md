# Interview — guardian (also co-founder and vice-president), 2026-10-23

Conducted in Polish in a chat session. Quotes are kept in the original language with an English rendering. Names of children and other parents are removed.

## Limits of this account

- One guardian with one child, in one age group.
- The interviewee co-founded the product and sits on the club board. His beliefs about the club and about other parents are recorded separately below and are not findings.
- The Messenger excerpt the interviewee pasted is a real artifact from one week in one group channel. It shows one event, not frequency.

## Recent situation or task the person described

- **Regular training:** the times are fixed, and he learns them at the start of the season.
- **Friendly matches and training tournaments** are the harder part. The group plays in no league. The coach arranges friendlies with coaches of the same age category in other clubs, and the venue rotates between clubs (Gwiazda, Skawina, Czernichów).
- **The excerpt pasted from the group's Messenger channel**, from the past week, with names removed:
  - The coach posts the venue address, the start time (9:00), the gathering time (8:30), an approximate end time, the squad ("all players") and a request to fill in the poll "as soon as possible".
  - The poll's title states the gathering at **9:30**, which contradicts the 8:30 in the post. Five guardians answered "we'll be there"; at least one answered "we won't be there".
  - In the same channel, three guardians report a child's absence from regular training, giving illness, a doctor's visit and a six-week absence.
  - One guardian asks whether someone can take her child to the tournament. Another answers that she would, but is not going because she is at work.

## What they did, step by step

- He answers the coach's poll.
- He checks the time and place the day before the trip, by scrolling back through the channel history to find the coach's message.
- He reports his child's absence from a friendly to the coach in a private message and gives the reason. He sees other guardians report absences in the group channel instead.
- He pays membership fees by bank transfer to the club account through a standing order. Nobody reminds him.

## What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described

- He did not notice the 8:30 / 9:30 discrepancy, because he focused on the poll. He would have found the time the day before, and he said he might or might not have picked the right one.
- **Once, a message gave the address of an Orlik pitch in a neighbouring municipality instead of a map pin. They got lost and arrived about 30 minutes late.**
- He values the Messenger group for arranging lifts with other guardians, because trips fall on Saturdays or Sundays.
  - "nie jest to głupia funkcjonalność" — "it is not a dumb feature" (quote)
- **His standing order kept running through two summer months, when no fees are charged because there is no training.** Nobody in the club noticed the overpayment. He does not remember whether he then skipped September or let it go "for the good of the club".
- He named two more ways a standing order goes wrong: it can carry over last year's rate after the rate changes, or it can simply be wrong.

## Channels and apps

- He has five club-related channels on his phone:
  - the board (about 10 people), on Messenger;
  - all association members, on Messenger;
  - the whole football academy, all age groups, on Messenger;
  - his son's group, on Messenger;
  - once a year, a WhatsApp group the administrator creates for parents of children going on the 8-day summer camp.
- **He has muted all of them.** In fact he mutes the whole apps (WhatsApp, Messenger, Slack and other messengers), because notifications would disturb his work and life. He uses each app only because a particular group prefers it.
- The club has never asked him to install an app, and the club uses none today.

## Other approaches they tried and how those worked

None described.

## The outcome they were trying to achieve and what happened

He wants to get the child to the right place at the right time and to pay the correct amount. The section above lists the cases where this failed: a wrong venue address, a contradictory gathering time, and fees paid in months with no fee.

## Interviewer's own remarks (kept separate from what was said)

- **Notifications and channel count.** The muted apps suggest that a notification channel alone may not reach this guardian, whatever the tool. The schedule is checked by pulling it the day before, not by being pushed.
- **Lifts.** Arranging lifts is a use of the group chat that the current material does not mention. D-009 already keeps the group chat alongside the app; this account supports keeping it.
- **Contradictory data.** The 8:30 / 9:30 discrepancy came from the same author writing the same fact twice, in the post and in the poll. One source of truth for an event's time and place would address it; this is an interpretation.
- **Venue address.** The wrong-address case concerns an away venue, so it is not a facility the club manages.
- **Fee errors.** The summer overpayment points to a reconciliation gap: payments are not matched against the expected charges. This one case does not show how often it happens.
- **Unclear wording.** A phrase in one answer ("ktoś z uczniów zauważył, że rozwinęła się dyskusja" — "one of the students noticed that a discussion developed") was not clarified and is not used.
