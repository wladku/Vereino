# Interview with the treasurer

1. **Tell me about the last month-end close.** → Step by step: where did the list come from, who paid, who didn't, what did you do about them? How long did it take?
2. **When did someone last pay incorrectly (wrong amount, for the wrong child)?** → How did you sort it out? How did you know how much they should pay for the second child?
3. **How did you settle with the coaches for the last month?** → Where did the data on units come from? What about substitutions? Did anyone question it?
4. **What does the Orlik settlement with the municipality look like (if applicable)?** → What do you count, from where, how often? Was there a dispute?
5. **What have you tried to improve fee collection?** → What worked, what didn't? Did you consider online payments? What stood in the way (commissions? the board?)?
6. **Who in the club decides on buying a tool?** → What did the last such decision look like (anything: software, an app)?
7. **What would you do if someone took Excel away from you tomorrow?**
8. **Who else should I talk to?**

**Bad data:** as in the coach script. In addition: "collection is fine" → ask for the figure for the last month (how many families, how many paid on time).

**Commitment:** data — "Will you share an anonymous figure: how many families, how many overdue at the end of last month?"
