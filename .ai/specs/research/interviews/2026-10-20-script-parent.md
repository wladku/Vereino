# Interview with a parent

1. **How did you find out about your child's last training session/match?** → Where did you look? How long did it take? Was it clear where and at what time?
2. **When was a training session last cancelled or moved?** → How did you find out? When? What happened if it was too late?
3. **How do you answer the coach's "who's coming"?** → In how many groups? Did you forget recently? What happened then?
4. **How did you pay the last membership fee?** → Where did the amount come from? When? Did anyone remind you? How did you feel about it?
5. **When did you last write to the coach or the club about something other than "attending/not attending"?** → What was it about? How did it go?
6. **How many club groups/channels do you have on your phone right now?** → Which ones have you muted? Why?
7. **Has the club ever asked you to install an app?** → What did you do? Do you use it? Why or why not?
8. **What should I have asked? Who else should tell me about this?**

**Bad data:** "it would be great to have an app" → "and how do you manage today? what let you down recently?".

**Commitment:** time — "In two weeks, when I have a prototype of the attendance declaration link, will you click it and tell me what didn't work?"
