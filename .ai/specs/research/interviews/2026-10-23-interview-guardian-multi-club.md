# Interview — guardian of three sons in another football academy, 2026-10-23

Quotes are in Polish with an English rendering. Names of children, other parents and club staff are removed.

## Limits of this account

- One guardian and one club, the academy he calls "Lotnik". He said little about the dance organisation: he handles football, and his wife handles dance.
- The transcript lacks most of the questions, so short answers ("Tak", "Trójkę") cannot always be matched to a question. Only answers with clear content are used below.
- The labels "Speaker 3" and "Speaker 4" in the later part of the transcript are the interviewee: the interviewer confirmed that only the two of them took part.
- The name of the club's app is unclear in the transcript ("TB? TM?"). Screenshots he sent afterwards show it is SportBM (`../2026-10-23-benchmark-sportbm.md`).

## Recent situation or task the person described

- **Children and division of labour:** three sons in the football academy. Their dance classes are handled by his wife: "ja ogarniam lotnik, a żona ogarnia tańce" — "I handle Lotnik, my wife handles dance".
- **Football rhythm:** training twice a week, plus friendlies or tournaments, currently frequent and on weekends.
- **Dance rhythm:** training twice a week too. Performances are seasonal and more intense around June.
- **The club's app:** the club uses a dedicated app with an account for each son. Its calendar shows the day, the hours and the pitch, in a list view and a calendar view.
  - An event can carry a map pin and a description from the coach, for example "we play in the hall today, bring these kits and shoes" or "bring more drinks".
  - An event has a start time and an end time.
  - He offered to send screenshots.
- **The latest friendly:** the coach called up one son for Sunday's friendly through the app.
  - On Monday, the coach announces Sunday's friendly with a deadline to accept or decline, for example Thursday 12:00.
  - He missed the deadline. "I gdzieś sobie przegapiłem ten termin, no i to wtedy ci wygasa w tej aplikacji" — "and I somehow missed the deadline, and then it expires in the app".
  - Without a response the event is no longer visible to the parent; with an acceptance it is.
  - On what happens after a decline: "nie było takiej sytuacji" — "there was no such situation". He assumes the coach picks someone else.

## What they did, step by step

- He checks times and places in the app's calendar.
- **Fees:** on the 1st of the month a notification arrives. He adds all three sons to a basket and pays once in the app. A "paid / unpaid" status stays visible.
- **Lifts:** for training, three families rotate driving, so each family drives every third training. A fourth family trains at other times, so they could not include it.

## What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described

- **Confirmations missed:** parents forget to click the confirmation, and then the coach phones to ask whether the child will come. "ludzie mają mnóstwo... teraz rodzice mają mnóstwo przeróżnych zajęć i rzeczy na głowie i po prostu zapominają tego klikać" — "parents have loads of activities and things on their mind and simply forget to click it".
- **Who is called up:** he cannot see who else was called up or how many. With WhatsApp, earlier, everyone saw the names and the thumbs-up confirmations, which made it easier to arrange with other parents who drives.
  - "pod względem logistycznym ... to było fajniejsze, jak się widziało, kto jest na sparingu, no bo można się było z rodzicami dogadać, kto gdzie jedzie" — "logistically it was nicer when you could see who is at the friendly, because you could arrange with the parents who goes where".
- **Groups:** his son's age group has close to 40 children, split into two training groups. He would like to see which children are in which group, for contact between parents. This is a stated preference.
- **Urgent changes:** the WhatsApp groups (one per age group) are still used, "in a smaller degree", for things the app cannot do. His examples:
  - a new child without a kit, asking who can lend one for the friendly;
  - a match moved from 10:00 to 11:00 the day before, when everyone has to be told quickly.
  
  "przez aplikację tego nie zrobisz, bo nikt tego nie zobaczy. Tylko przez WhatsAppa się to robi" — "you won't do it through the app, because nobody will see it. It's only done through WhatsApp".
- **Payment commission:** paying in the app costs a commission of about PLN 2 per basket, and it annoys him. "wkurza mnie ta prowizja w płatnościach" — "the commission on payments annoys me". A friend ignores in-app payment and pays by a bank standing order instead.
- **Payment status:** the visible status helps. "to bardziej człowieka mobilizuje" — "it mobilises a person more".
- **Kit orders:** the app can take kit orders, but the coordinator does not use that and collects orders through WhatsApp. He believes the reason is that the coordinator wants cash; that is a guess about someone else.

## Other approaches they tried and how those worked

- WhatsApp groups before the app, still used alongside it.
- The family splits the organisations between the two parents.
- He has not tried to combine the app's calendar with his wife's calendar for dance.

## The outcome they were trying to achieve and what happened

He wants the right son at the right place and time, lifts shared with other families, and fees paid. Calendar and fees work in the app. Confirmations get missed, urgent changes and lifts still go through WhatsApp, and he pays a commission he dislikes.

## Adoption of the app

He does not know whether parents resisted the switch, or even whether all of them have the app ("chyba mają" — "they probably do"). His view: "jak słyszą, że jest aplikacja, no to każdy ... instaluje, bo nie ma wyjścia" — "when they hear there is an app, everyone installs it, because there is no choice".

## What he said is most missing

- **A messenger** in the app: "tego komunikatora najbardziej brakuje" — "the messenger is what's missing most".
- **Visibility for parents** of which children are in their group and who is called up: "żeby rodzic widział więcej ... z punktu widzenia rodzica byłoby na plus" — "for the parent to see more would be a plus from the parent's point of view". He added that he does not know whether that would be a plus for the coach.

## What they explicitly did not care about

Not discussed.
