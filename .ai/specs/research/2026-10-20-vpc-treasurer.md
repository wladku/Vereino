# VPC — treasurer of a small academy

## Customer profile
**Jobs:** generate and collect membership fees (functional); settle with coaches (functional); settle the Orlik with the municipality (functional); not be "the one who keeps sending reminders" (social); have peace of mind that nothing slipped through (emotional). [ASSUMPTION]
**Pains (top 3):** 1) can't see overdue charges at any given moment; 2) manual reminders; 3) counting coaches' units and Orlik hours from memory. [ASSUMPTION]
**Gains (top 3):** 1) the overdue list and online payments without her involvement; 2) the municipality report and coach settlement with one button; 3) discounts calculated automatically. [ASSUMPTION]

## Value map
**Products/services:** `payments` module (fee schedule, discounts, charges, online payment), coach report from `occurrence.coach_id`, facility report for the owner.
**Pain relievers:** automatic charge generation and reminders; online payment; coaches' units from `done` sessions; Orlik hours from zones and bookings.
**Gain creators:** one "who is overdue" screen; export for the accountant; payment history per family.

## Hypotheses to verify in interviews
- H1: the treasurer loses ≥3 h/month on reminders and settlements (ask about the last month).
- H2: online payment will improve timeliness (ask what parents did when the club introduced something similar — if it did).
- H3: the municipality report is done manually today and is sometimes a source of disputes.
