# [BENCHMARK] SportBM, as used by LKS Lotnik 1949

- **Product:** the app's header reads "sportbm". The club badge on the dashboard is LKS Lotnik 1949.
- **Link:** not yet checked. The public page, pricing and the commission model are still on the collection plan.
- **Source and limits:**
  - Four phone screenshots from one guardian account, taken on a day in September 2026, the week of 21–27 September.
  - The screenshots show the full names of the guardian's children. They are therefore not stored in the repository, and no names appear below.
  - Screens describe how the product behaves for this account. They do not show what other guardians or the club do with it.

## What the screens show

### Dashboard ("pulpit")

The dashboard shows the club badge and these tiles:

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| notifications ("powiadomienia") | a badge with **135** unread |
| messages ("wiadomości") | — |
| to pay ("do zapłaty") | a green check: nothing due |
| today ("dzisiaj") | 0 events |
| market | — |
| insure the player ("ubezpiecz zawodnika") | highlighted |
| offers ("oferty") | highlighted |
| registrations ("zgłoszenia") | — |
| my tasks ("moje zadania") | a green check |

- An advertising banner for a third-party football clothing shop sits at the bottom.
- The bottom navigation has dashboard, calendar, payments, more and profile.

### Calendar

- It has two tabs, "7 dni" (a list of the next seven days) and "kalendarz" (a month picker with a week strip).
- Each day in the strip shows the number of events.
- A day without events shows "brak wydarzeń" (no events).

### Day view

- Children in one account appear as separate entries in one calendar.
- On one Saturday the same tournament appears three times, once per child. Each entry shows its own start time and duration (two children at 07:15 for 4h45m, one at 12:30 for 4h), the venue name and the child's name.

### Event details

The event detail screen shows:
- the date, the event name, the start time and the duration;
- a green banner "powołanie zostało zaakceptowane" (call-up accepted);
- the child the event is for;
- the facility with its street address and a map-pin button;
- a free-text "event details" field, which here contains the gathering time and the kit: "zbiórka 12:30, pasiaste stroje" (gathering 12:30, striped kits).

## What it does for our users' job

A guardian with several children sees all their events in one calendar, with:
- per-child call-up status;
- a venue with an address and a map link;
- the coach's notes;
- the payment status on the dashboard.

## Where it falls short, as visible on these screens

These are observations, not user statements:

- **Gathering time.** The gathering time sits only in free text, separately from the event's start time. The same can happen in Vereino's current spec (brief, Goals gap).
- **Who else is coming.** Nothing on these screens shows who else is called up. This matches what the guardian said.
- **Unread notifications.** The guardian's account has 135 unread notifications.
- **Messages.** A "messages" tile exists, although the guardian said a messenger is what the app misses most. Whether it is one-way from the club or a conversation between parents is unknown.

## Commercial model, as visible

- An advertising banner.
- "Insure the player" and "offers" tiles.
- A payment commission charged to the parent, about PLN 2 per basket, per the interview.

The club's own cost is unknown.
