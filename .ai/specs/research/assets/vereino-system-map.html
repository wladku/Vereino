Vereino System Map

A shared picture of the sports club management system, using KS Gwiazda Ściejowice as the example: entities, relationships, roles and the boundary of the first release. Version 0.2 adds the sports facility (Orlik 2024) administered by the club on behalf of Gmina Liszki.

2. Four decisions from the meeting, plus clarifications

MVP scope

Calendar core plus payments. Projects, shop, contracts, rentals only as reserved modules.

A single Activity

Training, match, friendly and camp are one entity with a type field. Recurrence is an attribute, not a separate class.

A single Group

A football team and a zumba group are the same entity with a kind field. A team additionally has an age category and a league.

Child account

The guardian has an account and child profiles. A child aged 13+ gets their own login with a limited view. Consents and payments always stay with the guardian.

Many roles per person

A coordinator can also be a coach, a guardian can be a coach. Permissions add up, the UI has a context switcher, actions record the role.

Declaration by a child 13+

Configurable per club / group / activity type: immediately with a notification to the guardian, requires the guardian's confirmation, or only the guardian declares.

Substitution and pay

Coach = base pay + training units. The unit goes to whoever ran the session, so a substitution automatically transfers it to the substitute in the monthly report.

Orlik in the MVP

Full facility module: hour zones, public schedule, bookings by phone, rates, attendant log, incidents, bans, municipality report. Online booking later.

3. Roles and what each one sees after signing in

Participant MVP

  • Four tiles: today/calendar, messages, attendance, payments (view only)
  • "Attending / not attending" on a session
  • Profile with photo, my groups
  • History: groups, matches later

Legal guardian MVP

  • Calendar of all their children on one screen
  • Declares attendance on the child's behalf
  • Pays membership fees, sees discounts
  • Writes to the coach; signs up for duty

Coach MVP

  • My groups → sessions → attendance list
  • Cancels a session, reports an absence, names a substitute coach
  • Announcement to a group or a specific session
  • Preview of their own contract and rates later

Section coordinator MVP

  • Defines groups, activities, places in their section
  • Sees sessions without a coach (to be staffed)
  • Sends announcements to coaches and the whole section
  • Record of "cases" (difficult situations) with history

Club administrator MVP

  • Fee schedules, discounts, charges, payments
  • People and role assignments, section data
  • Institutions, contracts (management agreement with the municipality, rental), rental rates, hour zones
  • Records rental payments to the municipality's account; owner report
  • Projects, shop later

Facility attendant MVP

  • Facility calendar: club training sessions, bookings, open-to-public hours
  • Enters a booking after a phone call, changes its status
  • Daily log: opening, pitch condition before/after, groups present and their supervisors
  • Reports an incident, requests an entry ban

Guest (without sign-in) MVP

  • Public monthly Orlik schedule: open to public / club / free to book / booked / closed
  • Opening hours, rates, regulations, phone number for bookings
  • Does not see who made a booking

Technical administrator MVP

  • Configuration of tenants (clubs), modules, appearance
  • Does not see personal data beyond what is necessary

4. How the calendar works (the path of one training session)

The coordinator creates an Activity"Żaki training", group Żaki, Tuesdays 17:00–18:30, Orlik, coach Wojtek, rule: weekly until 30.06
The system generates SessionsOne Session for every Tuesday; each has its own status, coach and place (they may differ from the defaults)
The calendars fill upThe guardian sees the children's sessions, the participant their own, the coach their groups, the coordinator the whole section
Attendance declarationThe guardian or a participant aged 13+ clicks "attending / not attending". Whether a child's declaration requires the guardian's confirmation – a club / group / activity type setting
SubstitutionThe coach reports an absence → the session "without a coach" goes to the coordinator → a substitute is named. The training unit counts for the substitute, not for the default coach
After the training sessionThe coach checks off actual attendance. Session → "done". Attendance builds the participant's history and the basis for settlements

Session statuses: planned → confirmed → done | cancelled. A session without a coach is planned with an empty coach field, which the coordinator sees on a separate list.

4a. How Orlik rental works (from the regulations §2–§6)

A guest sees a free slotPublic schedule: Wednesday 19:30–21:00 "bookable". They call the number from the schedule
The attendant creates a bookingFootball pitch, 1 unit (90 min), renter "KS Sąsiedzi" (club from the municipality, winter → rate with lighting). The system calculates the amount, the payment deadline and the 24 h cancellation window
Slot blockedThe schedule shows "booked" without a name. The coordinator sees the clash if they wanted a training session there
Payment to the municipality's accountThe administrator records the payment → charge paid, booking confirmed. No payment by the deadline → the booking expires (to be decided)
Rental dayAttendant: opening, pitch condition before, presence of the group's adult supervisor, condition after, closing. Breach → incident, possibly an entry ban
Municipality reportMonthly: hours in zones, bookings, payments, incidents, maintenance and inspections

Booking statuses: requested → confirmed → paid → done | cancelled_free (≥ 24 h) | cancelled_late (fee forfeited) | no_show. Municipal events and school classes have priority – the administrator can move a booking with a notification.

6. Scope of the first release

In the MVP

  • Modules club_core, people, groups, activities, communication, payments, facilities
  • Calendar in four views (participant, guardian, coach, coordinator)
  • Declared and actual attendance (policy for 13+); substitutions with history; monthly calculation of training units per coach
  • Announcements to a group/session/section/club, threads, "case" flag
  • Fee schedule, sibling discounts, charges, manual recording of payments
  • List of external teams (opponents from a list)
  • Orlik: zones, public schedule, bookings by phone, rates, attendant log, incidents, bans, municipality report

Later

  • Projects and grants with settlement towards institutions
  • Bookkeeping of coach payouts (the calculation is in the MVP); regulations to be accepted by guardians
  • Shop: kits and merchandise
  • Online Orlik booking with payment and accounts for regular renters
  • Online payments (gateway), push notifications, WhatsApp/Messenger integration
  • Participant development history, data from watches

7. Questions to resolve before coding

Sections: which ones and who is the coordinator?Three emerge from the conversation: football academy (Wojtek?), swimming pool (Aneta?), extra classes. Are seniors a separate section? Without this, coordinator permissions cannot be granted.
Boundary between coordinator ↔ club administrator.Proposal: coordinator = "what is happening" (groups, activities, sessions, staffing), administrator = "money and people" (fee schedules, charges, roles, personal data, places). Agreed?
Membership fees: settlement model.A monthly fee per group, a fee for a specific activity (camp, away match), or both? Who is the payer for an adult participant (themselves)? In the MVP, does the administrator record payments manually, or do we go straight to a payment gateway?
Camp: activity or project?Proposal: an activity of type camp (multi-day, with a participant list and a fee), and a project only when there is external co-funding. Agreed?
Personal data and GDPR.Is PESEL really needed in the MVP (insurance, PZPN licenses)? Who has the right to see it? Which consents does the guardian confirm in the system (image, regulations, trip)?
Announcements: replace WhatsApp or complement it?For the MVP we propose: announcements only in the app, with an e-mail notification. Integration with messengers later. Should "cases" (complaints, conflicts) be opened manually by the coordinator, or can any thread be marked as a case?
Many clubs.Multi-tenant right away (1 tenant = 1 club, as in Open Mercato), or one instance for Gwiazda to start with?
Platform.Kacper mentioned a phone app. Open Mercato provides the web. Is the MVP = a responsive web app (PWA), with a native app later?

Product name: Vereino

From German Verein (association, club). Rejected: CoolClub (working name), Clubio and Klubio (taken). Project set up in Lead Hub. To be decided: domain (.org / .com / .app) – purchase on hold.

From the Orlik regulations and management agreement

Hours and zones.The regulations contain placeholders (8:00–22:00 / 9:00–20:30). What are the actual opening hours and the weekly split: open to public free of charge / club training / paid rental?
Payment deadline and expiry."No later than N days before" – how many? Should a booking without payment by the deadline expire on its own, or does the administrator decide?
Who enters bookings.The attendant, the club administrator, or both? Does the attendant see the amounts and the rates?
Rates.Which amounts from the ranges (PLN 80–150 / 150–220 / 220–350 per 90 min) go into the agreement? When does the "winter season / lighting" apply – from a date or from the hour after dark?
The municipality's money.Payments go to the municipality's account. Does the system only record "paid" based on a confirmation? Does part of the revenue go back to the club (renovation fund)?
Priority of the municipality and schools.The municipality has no account – who enters its events and school classes into the schedule, and with how much notice?
Attendant.An employee of the club or of the municipality? Should the log be mandatory (the day cannot be "closed" without an entry)?
Entry bans.Is a register enough, or is a procedure needed: who issues them, for how long, how to appeal?
Liability insurance, annual and 5-year inspections.Should the system keep track of deadlines (reminders for the administrator), or does that stay outside it?
Acceptance of the regulations.For a phone booking, is a note "accepted by phone" + the regulations in the confirmation e-mail enough?
