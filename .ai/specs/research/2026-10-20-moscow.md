# MoSCoW — Vereino 1.0

Test for each item: "what happens if it isn't there?" Must = we call off the release. Should = there is a workaround, even a manual one. Could = nice to have. Won't = deliberately not this time.

## Must Have
| Item | What if not | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Person with multiple roles, guardian account with child profiles | a parent of two children won't use it; a coordinator-coach has two accounts | D-005 |
| Sections (with a coordinator), groups (team / class group — both first-class), memberships | nothing to assign sessions to; the multi-section club is the product thesis (D-011) | D-003, D-011 |
| Activity with RRULE → sessions; per-role calendars | this is the product | D-002 |
| Declaration + actual attendance, policy for 13+ | we go back to Messenger polls | D-006 |
| Substitution with history and unit calculation | a dispute about money = rejection by coaches | D-007 |
| Announcements to a group/session/section + push + e-mail + "share to group" | the parent has no reason to come in | D-009 |
| Membership fees: fee schedule, sibling discount, charges, **online payment** | the treasurer stays in Excel; PTU wins with its first argument | D-010 |
| Facility: zones, public schedule, manual bookings, rates, municipality report | we lose our only edge over PTU | D-008 |

## Should Have
| Item | Workaround |
|---|---|
| Attendant log, incidents, entry bans | a notebook at the Orlik; "cases" in announcements |
| Magic link for declarations without sign-in | guardian sign-in |
| Camp as an activity of type camp with a fee and a list | activity "other" + manual charge |
| PZPN age categories as a dictionary, season as an entity | text field on the group |
| Match with a score and an opponent from a list | score in a note |
| Monthly coach report (base + units) for export | the treasurer counts from the session list |

## Could Have
| Item |
|---|
| Kit orders (size survey) |
| Player history (moves between groups, matches) |
| Facility maintenance/inspections with reminders |
| Profile photo, place pin on a map |

## Won't Have this time
Training diagrams and an exercise library · medical module · scouting and player assessments · individual statistics · Messenger/WhatsApp integration · online booking with payment for Orlik renters · projects and grants · fan shop · native app (a PWA is enough) · multi-tenant for many clubs on one instance (1 instance = 1 club in 1.0; the architecture must not block it) · integration with PZPN/Łączy nas piłka.

## Proportions (effort estimate)
Must ~60% (eight core modules, including the facility and online payments — that is the most work), Should ~25%, Could ~15%. If we run out of time: Could goes first, then Should from the bottom of the table. Warning: online payments and the facility together in Must is a lot; if Must exceeds 60%, the candidate to move to Should is the attendant log (already there) and… nothing else from Must without losing the product thesis — in that case we extend the deadline, we don't cut.

## Addendum 2026-10-20 — scope added after discovery (D-013)

The tables above are the state at the end of discovery. While writing the specification the product owner extended the Must set; the full list with sources is in [`decisions/D-013-scope-added-after-discovery.md`](decisions/D-013-scope-added-after-discovery.md). In short:

- **Must, added:** internal bookings for staff of every section, online booking requests with administrator approval (still without online payment for renters), a public club page with section pages and a prices block, club and section logos, bulk-set booking availability, bank statement import with automatic matching, cash payments with receipt (KP) numbers, the coach's view of their groups' payments with cash handover to the treasurer, a separate treasurer role, a multilingual interface (en + pl).
- **Could → Must:** "Kit orders (size survey)" becomes order campaigns (REQ-032) — it replaces the Google Form the club uses today.
- **Must, changed:** push is not in 1.0 (Q-002, D-009 confirmed) — announcements go by in-app notification and e-mail; online payment is via Stripe (Q-003, D-010 confirmed).
- **Won't, unchanged:** online booking *with payment* for renters stays out; requests without payment are in.

The effort estimate above no longer holds for the extended Must set; the specification's phases (1–5) are the current plan.
