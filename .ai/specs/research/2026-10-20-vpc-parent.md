# VPC — parent/guardian

## Customer profile
**Jobs:** know when and where each child's training is (functional); report attendance (functional); pay (functional); not come across as the parent who "didn't know again" (social); have peace of mind (emotional). [ASSUMPTION]
**Pains (top 3):** 1) information scattered across 3–4 groups; 2) missed cancellations/changes; 3) paying "when I remember". [ASSUMPTION]
**Gains (top 3):** 1) one "today/tomorrow" screen for all children; 2) a push about a change; 3) "attending" and payment in one click. [ASSUMPTION]

## Value map
**Products/services:** guardian account with child profiles; four tiles (calendar, messages, attendance, payments); push + e-mail; magic link for declarations without sign-in.
**Pain relievers:** combined calendar; a notification instead of scrolling through the group; a membership fee reminder with a payment link.
**Gain creators:** child's history (groups, matches); sibling discount visible right away.

## Hypotheses
- H6: a parent will click a link from Messenger to declare attendance if it does not require signing in.
- H7: a parent will not install another app until they get something from it that Messenger doesn't offer (push about a cancellation + payment).
