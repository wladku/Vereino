# Product Vision Board — Vereino

## Target group
Users: coordinator, treasurer/administrator, coaches, guardians, participants 13+, facility attendant — in a small multi-section club (association) whose main section is a football academy (~100–200 players), alongside a swimming pool and extra classes; usually suburban, on a municipal facility. [D-011]
Paying customer: the association (club) board; in the open-source model it "pays" with time and hosting, not a license. [ASSUMPTION]

## Needs (in order of importance — ASSUMPTION, to verify)
1. Treasurer: know who hasn't paid, without Excel; parent: pay in two clicks.
2. Coach: attendance and substitutions from the phone in 30 seconds.
3. Coordinator: a facility schedule without clashes between age groups; a public Orlik schedule and bookings without phone calls.
4. Parent: one calendar for all their children; know today whether there is training.
5. Board: a record of difficult cases as protection.

## Business goals (with a measure)
- Gwiazda runs on Vereino for the full 2027/28 season without going back to Messenger for attendance (measure: ≥80% of attendance declarations in the app). [ASSUMPTION: feasibility]
- 5 other academies on Vereino by the end of 2027 (measure: active instances). [ASSUMPTION]
- Fee collection at Gwiazda rises from X% to Y% (X to be measured before rollout — [DATA: none]).
- An open-source repository with ≥3 contributors from outside the team (measure: GitHub). [ASSUMPTION]

## Product (3–5 distinguishing features)
1. The municipal facility as a central feature: zones, public schedule, bookings, attendant log, municipality report.
2. A parent with children in different sections and a person with multiple roles — one record, one account, one calendar.
3. Membership fees with online payment and a sibling discount, generated automatically.
4. Substitutions that settle training units on their own.
5. Simplicity: the parent's screen is four tiles; rollout over a weekend.

## Vision (one sentence)
A small club runs the club, not Excel and Messenger.

## Biggest risk
Parent adoption: will they move into the app instead of Messenger. Check through a problem interview with 5 parents (how they last found out about a cancelled training session, what they did, what it cost them) — without showing the app. Second risk: the treasurer and coordinator will choose PTU because "it already exists". Check through conversations with 2 coordinators of other clubs.
