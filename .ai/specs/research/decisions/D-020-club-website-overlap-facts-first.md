# D-020 The club website overlaps Vereino: collect the facts before changing the 1.0 scope

**Context:**
- The academy coordinator described a website and system being built for the club by another person, paid by the club. Władek confirmed it is a separate project, independent of Vereino. By the coordinator's account it is to present:
  - the coaches, the groups and contact details;
  - documents and GDPR consents to download;
  - "enrol a child" and sign-up for the cheerleading, chess and zumba groups;
  - the Orlik schedule with booking, which the Orlik management agreement is expected to require to be public.
- This overlaps Vereino 1.0:
  - the public club page (REQ-025, REQ-026, logos REQ-028);
  - the public Orlik schedule and external booking requests (REQ-014, REQ-024).

**Decision:** the 1.0 scope stays unchanged for now. Before deciding how Vereino and the website divide the work, collect what the website will actually do:
- its scope;
- whether booking works there or only links out;
- its launch date;
- whether it can embed or link a calendar from Vereino;
- who maintains it.

**Why:** the only source is a short second-hand account in one conversation. Cutting Vereino's scope now could remove something the website will not do. Keeping both unchecked could make the club pay twice for the same thing.

**Considered:**
- Moving the public club page out of 1.0 and linking the website to Vereino's Orlik calendar.
- Keeping both without checking.

**Open:** Q17 in the brief — how Vereino and the club website divide the public page, the Orlik schedule and booking, and sign-ups. It blocks the planning of REQ-025, REQ-026, REQ-014 and REQ-024, but not phase 1's people and roles.

**To change:** a superseding record approved by the owner once the website's scope is known.

**Revisit:** when the website's scope is collected (the administrator, or the person building it).
