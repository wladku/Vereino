# D-018 A separate discovery track for grant settlement

**Context:**
- The academy coordinator named grant settlement as the work that takes most of his time (`interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`). The club settles about 12 grants a year. Invoices from KSeF are allocated between grants, described and tabulated, and a double claim risks losing a whole grant.
- Władek, as vice-president, called it "a nightmare". That is his statement; no account of a specific settlement is recorded yet.
- D-016 kept grants out of 1.0 (N07).

**Decision:**
- Grant settlement gets its own discovery track, separate from the Vereino 1.0 brief. It is treated as a possible separate product that could also run as a Vereino module.
- The 1.0 scope and D-016 stay as they are while the track runs.

**Open (Q13 in the brief):** Władek raised whether the team's energy would be better spent on a grant-settlement system for all associations than on Vereino, because the club-app market has many competitors. This is a hypothesis, not a decision.
- What supports raising it: the competitor benchmark found many club apps.
- What it rests on that is unknown: whether grant settlement is a common, costly problem outside KS Gwiazda, and which tools already address it.

**Evidence to collect:**
1. An account of the last grant settlement at KS Gwiazda, step by step: who, how long, what went wrong. Sources: Władek, the administrator, and the second part of the coordinator interview.
2. Two or three interviews with other associations, not only sports clubs.
3. A benchmark of existing grant-settlement tools, such as municipal grant platforms and NGO accounting software, checked on a date.

**To change:** a superseding record approved by the owner, after the evidence above.

**Revisit:** when the three items above are collected. The decision then is one of: a separate product, a Vereino module, or no product.
