# D-016 Grants and training plans stay out of 1.0 after the coordinator interview

**Context:** the academy coordinator described two needs that touch the brief's non-goals.
- Grant settlement is the work that takes most of his time. There were about 12 grants this year, and every invoice is split between grants, described and tabulated. A double claim risks losing a whole grant. This touches N07, projects and grants.
- He wants training plans linked to sessions in the calendar, for a possible PZPN bronze-star certification. This touches N01, coaching tools.

**Decision:**
- N07 stays: grants are not built in 1.0. The grant-settlement problem is recorded in the brief as supported by the coordinator's account.
- N01 stays: coaching tools, including training plans, are not built in 1.0. The coordinator's wish is recorded as his stated intention, not as an observed problem.

**Why:** each need rests on one account. The administrator, who collects and delivers the invoices, has not been interviewed. The certification is a possible goal for next year, not a decision.

**Considered:** reopening N07 or N01 as candidates for 1.0, or leaving both questions open.

**To change:**
- N07: the administrator's interview, or the coordinator's continued interview, shows that grant settlement is a frequent, costly and error-prone part of the club's finances that 1.0's payment flows could address.
- N01: the club commits to the certification and coaches confirm the need.

**Revisit:** at the brief refresh after the administrator's interview and the second part of the coordinator's interview.
