# D-008 Full facility module in 1.0
**Decision:** hour zones, closures, rates (3 categories), manual bookings (90 min, 24 h, payment to the municipality's account), public schedule without sign-in, attendant log, incidents, bans, maintenance/inspections, owner report.
**Why:** the regulations provide ready-made rules; it is the edge over PTU; Gwiazda already administers the Orlik (municipal sports pitch complex) today.
**To change:** if conversations with 5 other academies show that most of them do not administer a facility — then the module stays but moves down from Must to Should.
**Revisit:** after conversations with coordinators of other clubs.
