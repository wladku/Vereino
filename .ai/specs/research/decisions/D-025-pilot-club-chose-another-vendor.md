# D-025 The first pilot club will not use Vereino; the pilot club is to be chosen

**Facts:**
- The club where the discovery interviews took place chose a website and system from another vendor.
- It will not be a source of further material: no more interviews, data or screen tests there. The material collected so far stays.

**Decision:**
- Vereino continues. The target segment (D-011) is unchanged.
- The pilot club is to be chosen; no contact has been recorded.
- Every commitment specific to that club is withdrawn and becomes open until the pilot club is known:
  - the rollout owner (D-021 named the club's board);
  - the owner of the facility rules towards the municipality (D-021 named the club's administrator);
  - the business goal "the pilot club runs on Vereino for the full 2027/28 season" and the revisit point "after the 4-week pilot of phase 2";
  - D-023's reasoning that both the club website and Vereino's public page stay "at the cost of duplicated work for the club" — there is no duplication, because that club will not run Vereino.

**What it does not change:**
- The scope decisions D-001…D-024 as such. Which of them depend on the pilot club is listed in the brief's refresh of 2026-10-23.
- The evidence already collected: it describes the segment (a small multi-section club with an academy, a municipal facility), not only one club.

**Also decided (same session):**
- The facility module (D-008) stays Must, and administering a municipal facility is a condition for choosing the pilot club.
- The spec replaces every reference to the first club as the pilot with an example club with the same sections (football academy with its birth-year groups, swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading). Since 2026-11-25 the example club is the fictional "KS Przykład Dolina" in Gmina Zielony Las.
- No date is set by which the pilot club must be known; Q05 stays open.

**To change:** a superseding record approved by the owner, once a pilot club is chosen.

**Revisit:** when the pilot club is chosen.
