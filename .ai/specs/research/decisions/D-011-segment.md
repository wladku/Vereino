# D-011 Target segment: small multi-section clubs with a football academy as the main section
**Decision (corrected 2026-10-20):** the customer is the **multi-section club** (association), not the academy alone. The football academy is its main and largest section (indicatively up to ~200 players — a description of scale, not a system limit). The other sections (swimming pool, extra classes: zumba, pilates, gym, chess…) are a full-fledged use case, not an add-on. Not league clubs / large academies.
**Why:** Władek knows this segment from the inside; PTU is present there, but heavy and paid.
**Consequences:** the section remains a first-class entity with its own coordinator; the group with `kind = team | class_group` (D-003) stays; PZPN (Polish Football Association) age categories and the season as entities apply only to `team` (Should); the camp moves closer to 1.0; we do not build coaching tools. Edge over ProTrainUp: the municipal facility **and** multi-section support (PTU is football/sports-oriented).
**Revisit:** after 5 conversations with coordinators/presidents of other clubs (including the coordinator of a non-football section).
