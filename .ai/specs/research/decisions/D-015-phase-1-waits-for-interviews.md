# D-015 Implementation planning of phase 1 waits for the interviews

**Decision:** phase 1 of the spec (club and people) is not planned for implementation until the interviews requested on 2026-10-23 have taken place. Those are three guardians, the academy coordinator and the treasurer/administrator.

**Why:** phase 1 ends with the guardian and a 14-year-old signing in and seeing their profiles. Whether guardians will sign in and use the app at all is untested (brief A01, A07). The only account so far is the product owner's own.

**Considered:** planning phase 1 now and accepting the risk, or leaving the question open. Władek chose to wait.

**To change:** the interviews are delayed and the team decides to proceed with the risk accepted. That needs a superseding record approved by the owner.

**Revisit:** when the interview notes are in `research/interviews/` and the brief is refreshed.
