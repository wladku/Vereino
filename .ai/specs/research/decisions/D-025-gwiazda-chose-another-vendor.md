# D-025 KS Gwiazda will not use Vereino; the pilot club is to be chosen

**Facts (Władek, as vice-president, from a board decision; his first, shorter statement in the benchmark side session is quoted in `../2026-10-23-owner-statements-funding.md`):**
- The board of KS Gwiazda chose a website and system from a person who has a CMS and works with the academy coordinator professionally. This is the website the coordinator described in his second conversation (`interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator-2.md`).
- Władek knew this from the start, and it does not trouble him.
- Gwiazda will not be a source of further material either: no more interviews, data or screen tests there. The material collected so far stays.

**Decision:**
- Vereino continues. The target segment (D-011) is unchanged.
- The pilot club is to be chosen. Władek names Kaszubianka Kaszów as a possible candidate; no contact has been recorded.
- Every Gwiazda-specific commitment is withdrawn and becomes open until the pilot club is known:
  - the rollout owner (D-021 named the Gwiazda board);
  - the owner of the facility rules towards the municipality (D-021 named the Gwiazda administrator);
  - the business goal "Gwiazda runs on Vereino for the full 2027/28 season" and the revisit point "after the 4-week pilot of phase 2";
  - D-023's reasoning that both the club website and Vereino's public page stay "at the cost of duplicated work for the club" — there is no duplication, because Gwiazda will not run Vereino.

**What it does not change:**
- The scope decisions D-001…D-024 as such. Which of them depend on the pilot club is listed in the brief's refresh of 2026-10-23.
- The evidence already collected: it describes the segment (a small multi-section club with an academy, a municipal facility), not only Gwiazda.

**Also decided (Władek, same session):**
- The facility module (D-008) stays Must, and administering a municipal facility is a condition for choosing the pilot club.
- The spec replaces every reference to Gwiazda as the pilot with an example club, "Dev Academy", with the same sections as Gwiazda (football academy with its birth-year groups, swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading). Interview notes and other evidence keep their real attribution.
- The research tasks that named Gwiazda (#12, #13, #18, #20) stay as they are in the tracker for now.
- No date is set by which the pilot club must be known; Q05 stays open.

**To change:** a superseding record approved by the owner, once a pilot club is chosen.

**Revisit:** when the pilot club is chosen.
