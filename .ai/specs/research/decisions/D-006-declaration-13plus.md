# D-006 The attendance declaration of a child aged 13+ is configurable
**Decision:** attendance_policy per club/group/activity type: immediate / guardian_confirm / guardian_only, with a timeout and a default value. Proposed default: training immediate, matches and away trips guardian_confirm (Claude's suggestion — proposal).
**Why:** different clubs and different families want different things.
**Revisit:** after the conversations with parents (H6, H7).
