# VPC — coach

## Customer profile
**Jobs:** know who will come (functional); run the training session/match (functional); find a substitute (functional); be seen by parents as on top of things (social); not argue about units (emotional). [ASSUMPTION]
**Pains (top 3):** 1) the attendance poll gets lost in the group; 2) substitutions by phone and disputes about the settlement; 3) parents write privately about everything. [ASSUMPTION]
**Gains (top 3):** 1) attendance list in 30 s; 2) substitution reported in the app, settled automatically; 3) one announcement to the group with read receipts. [ASSUMPTION]

## Value map
**Products/services:** "my groups → sessions → attendance" view; absence report + naming a substitute coach; announcement to a group/session; "share to group" button (text + link).
**Pain relievers:** declarations flow into the list, not into the chat; `substitution_log` as evidence; units report.
**Gain creators:** preview of one's own settlement; player attendance history.

## Hypotheses
- H4: a coach who uses PTU Coach for exercises will accept Vereino for attendance if attendance takes less than a minute.
- H5: disputes about units around substitutions happen at least once a quarter (ask about the most recent one).
