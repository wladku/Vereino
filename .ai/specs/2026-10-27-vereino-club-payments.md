## TLDR

Phase 4 of Vereino: membership fee schedules with the sibling discount, automatic charges and an overdue list; online payment through the club's Stripe account; bank statement import with matching; cash with numbered receipts and its handover to the treasurer; order campaigns for kits; the coach's view of their groups' payments; the treasurer role. It builds on phases 1–3 of the club management specification and provides the optional rental charge the facilities specification uses (DD-19).

## Problem Statement

See `2026-10-20-vereino-club-management.md` (*Problem Statement*): fees are kept by hand, coaches collect cash and hand it over monthly, kit orders run through a Google Form. The treasurer's own account of the problem is missing — A02 is accepted untested (D-021), so the treasurer role and screens are held back from implementation planning (*Goals / Readiness* in `2026-10-20-vereino-club-management.md`).

## Overview and Success Measures

Outcome: charges are generated for every period without manual work, overdue amounts are visible at any moment, and every payment — online, transfer or cash — is attached to a charge. Measures: share of charges paid before `due_date`; share of transfers matched automatically. The primary outcome of the product (attendance in the app) is defined in `2026-10-20-vereino-club-management.md`.

Shared with the club management specification (`2026-10-20-vereino-club-management.md`) and not repeated here: the product context, the users and permissions table with the full feature list, record scope (`people.scopeService`), the reuse and ownership map, the architecture and module boundaries, the club settings (DD-37), the UI architecture per role, the security and tenant-isolation rules, DD-35, the API conventions, the test conventions and the rollout. Ids are global: REQ, DD, J, TEST, AC, Q and V numbers are the same in all three specifications (D-028).

## Goals

- **REQ-011** (Must; D-022, Q-006) — Membership fee schedule (per group or per activity), sibling discount, automatic charge generation and an overdue list. The sibling rule is set by the administrator in the discount rule's conditions: which child pays the full rate (default: the oldest) and which fee kinds it covers (default: monthly fees only).
- **REQ-012** (Must, D-010 confirmed in Q-003; D-022, Q-016) — A guardian or adult participant pays membership fees online via Stripe (the club's own Stripe account); the administrator manually records cash and bank transfers. The online payment commission is a club setting `online_fee_mode`: `club_pays` (default) — the payer pays exactly the charge; or `payer_pays` — a surcharge of `online_fee_percent` of the amount plus `online_fee_fixed` is shown as a separate line before payment and added to the checkout amount, never to the charge.
- **REQ-026** (Must, Q-024) — Prices block on the section page: membership fee from the group's fee schedule and sibling discount, published only when the club has enabled the prices block.
- **REQ-030** (Must) — Bank statement reconciliation: the administrator imports a statement of the club's bank account (CSV in the bank's column layout or MT940); the system matches incoming payments to charges — a transfer with the payer code in the reference and an amount covering whole charges (oldest first) is booked automatically when auto-booking is enabled; transfers matched by surname and amount, with an overpayment or underpayment, go to "to confirm"; the rest go to "unmatched" (assign manually or skip, e.g., a donation). Debits on the account are skipped, a repeated import does not create duplicates, and every booked entry can be undone. Each payer has a permanent payer code shown with payments.
- **REQ-031** (Must) — Cash payment: the administrator (and a person granted the feature `payments.cash.record`), in the admin panel or on a phone, selects the payer, their charges and the amount (a partial payment is applied from the oldest charge); the system assigns a receipt number (KP/year/month/no.), records who accepted it, and optionally sends the payer an email confirmation; the collector sees the total cash accepted that day to hand over to the treasurer, and the administrator — the "Cash payments" view.
- **REQ-032** (Must; MoSCoW Could → Must on the product owner's decision, Q-031…Q-033 — it replaces the existing Google Form) — Order campaign (e.g., kits): the administrator creates an order form in the admin panel — name, description, section and groups, closing date and payment due date, products with children's and adult sizes (the price may depend on the size) and without a size, an optional set of several products at a set price, a required answer (size or "not ordering"), name and number for printing (number unique within the group), a question for comments — and opens collection with a text containing a link to paste into the Messenger group chat. The guardian of a child from the selected groups orders in the app or — without an account — from a personal link sent to the email address known to the club; they receive a copy by email and may change or cancel the order until the deadline. Each order creates a charge for the person ordering, payable online, by bank transfer with the payer code (matched from the statement, REQ-030) or in cash. After the deadline collection closes automatically; the administrator downloads the supplier summary (quantities per product and size, prints, CSV), marks the order as placed with the supplier and as delivered (people who ordered receive an email "ready for pickup from the coach"), and the coach or administrator marks the handover — of an unpaid kit only after confirming a warning. A minor participant does not see orders or prices (REQ-002). The sales document for an order is a club setting `order_sales_document` (`none`, `receipt`, `invoice`; D-022, Q-034); 1.0 implements only `none`, and the other two values are reserved for a later release.
- **REQ-033** (Must) — My groups' payments for the coach and cash handover: the coach (and the coordinator in their sections) sees in the mobile app the charges of children from their groups — membership fees of that group (current period and overdue) and orders (e.g., kits) with their lines — with the amount due, due date and status, but without the discount reason and without charges from other sections. They accept cash for one or more of a child's charges at once (KP number, email confirmation to the payer, partial payment applied from the oldest), remind the payer of overdue amounts, remind guardians who have not ordered, and mark kit handover. In the payment settings the club decides whether coaches see membership fees and whether they may accept cash for them (`none` — orders only, `view` — fees view-only, `collect` — fees and cash; default `collect`). Cash accepted by a person without settlement rights is "held by the collector" until the treasurer confirms receiving it in "Cash to settle" based on the coach's entries; the handover has a number (RG/year/month/no.), a list of payments and an amount. The coach sees the total cash they hold. The payment is credited to the parent immediately, regardless of the handover.
- **REQ-034** (Must) — Treasurer role: a person responsible solely for the club's finances — fee schedules and discounts, charges and overdue amounts, statement imports, cash payments, order campaigns and accepting cash from coaches (handovers) — without access to people records, groups, classes, club settings and facilities. The administrator has the same rights, so in a small club the administrator can be the treasurer; the treasurer role is assigned by the administrator in the person record.

## Non-goals

- Gateways other than Stripe, including a custom Przelewy24/PayU adapter (Q-003).

## Proposed Solution

One app module, `payments` (DD-18): fee schedules and discounts, charges with sources (`fee`, `rental`, `order`, `manual`), payments by method, online checkout through `payment_gateways` + `gateway_stripe`, statement import, cash and handovers, order campaigns. It registers the DI service `payments.chargeService`, which `facilities` calls optionally to create a rental charge in the same transaction (DD-19 in the facilities specification), and the public block `payments.publicPrices` for the section page (DD-28).


### Design Decisions and Alternatives

| Decision | Rationale | Alternative considered | Why rejected / deferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DD-18** Charges and payments app-owned in `payments`; online payment via `payment_gateways` + `gateway_stripe` (**Q-003**); own table `payments_checkouts` links the gateway transaction to charges | A club charge (membership fee, rental with `payee` = municipality) is not an order; the mapping does not depend on gateway event metadata (confirmed by V-5; the gateway contract is DD-42) | `sales` (orders, invoices) | `sales` requires `catalog` + `customers` + `dictionaries` |
| **DD-42** Online payment against the installed 0.9.0 contract (V-5): `payments` calls `paymentGatewayService.createPaymentSession` through DI (never `POST /api/payment_gateways/sessions`, which needs `payment_gateways.manage`), with `providerKey: 'stripe'`, `paymentId` = the `payments_checkouts.id`, `idempotencyKey` = the checkout's key, `amount` in złoty (grosz ÷ 100 at this one boundary, S10.9), `currencyCode: 'PLN'`, `captureMethod: 'automatic'` and `paymentTypes` from the club's enabled methods (default `['card']`). `gateway_stripe` creates a PaymentIntent and returns an **embedded** client session (Stripe Payment Element: `clientSecret` + `publishableKey`), not a hosted redirect; `/club/payments` renders it and adds `returnUrl` to the session payload. One idempotent fulfilment `payments.checkouts.fulfil(checkoutId)` records the payments: it reads the provider state with `paymentGatewayService.getPaymentStatus` and books when the provider reports `captured` with `amountReceived` = the checkout total. It runs from the `payment_gateways.payment.captured` subscriber, from the return page and from the sweep `payments:reconcile-checkouts`. Refunds start only in Vereino (`payments.payments.refund` → `paymentGatewayService.refundPayment`) and are booked from the call's result, not from a webhook | The installed contract decides: event payloads carry ids only (`transactionId`, `paymentId`, `providerKey`, `previousStatus`, `organizationId`, `tenantId`); `failed`, `cancelled` and `expired` are terminal in the gateway status machine, so a capture that follows a failed attempt raises no event; `payment_gateways.session.expired` and the partial-refund status emit nothing; Stripe refund webhooks (`charge.refunded`, `charge.refund.updated`) cannot be matched to the transaction and get 401 | Hosted Stripe Checkout Session with a redirect (S10.8, the original DD-18 flow); trusting the gateway events alone; syncing refunds from the Stripe Dashboard | `gateway_stripe` 0.9.0 has no Checkout Session adapter — a second adapter would be an ejection (Ask First); events alone lose late captures; a Dashboard refund never reaches the transaction |
| **DD-30** Order campaigns (e.g., kits) app-owned in `payments` (`payments_order_campaigns`, `payments_order_products`, `payments_orders`, `payments_order_lines`, `payments_order_links`); an order creates a charge `source_type = order` in the same transaction (Q-031…Q-033) | An order exists in order to be settled — payer, payer code, statement, cash and reminders are already in `payments`; the form must know the guardian's children and their groups (`people`, `groups` via DI) | `catalog` + `sales` (sales orders); `forms` form (`/f/[slug]`); separate `orders` module | `sales` requires `catalog` + `customers` + `dictionaries` and a customer model the club does not have (DD-18); `forms` requires `customer_accounts`, saves responses asynchronously and does not know the guardian's children — the charge and number uniqueness must be checked in one transaction; an eighth module for a single cycle that ends entirely in charges |
| **DD-31** Cash "held by collector": a `cash` payment accepted by a person without `payments.cash.settle` has `cash_handover_id = null` until the treasurer (or administrator) confirms receipt in a handover (`payments_cash_handovers`); cash accepted by a person with `payments.cash.settle` is settled immediately. A coach accepts cash (`payments.groups.collect`) only for charges of children within their scope: orders always, membership fees of their group — when the club setting allows it | The payment is credited to the parent immediately (confirmation, no reminders), and the club knows who is holding the cash | Coach payment "pending" until handed over; granting coaches the full `payments.cash.record` | A parent who paid the coach would see an overdue amount and receive reminders; the full feature would let the coach record charges of any family |
| **DD-32** "My groups' payments": the coach sees only charges linked to their group — membership fees from that group's fee schedule and orders from campaigns covering the group — as amount due, due date and status; they do not see the reason or type of discount, charges from other sections or the family's total balance. The scope for membership fees is controlled by the setting `payments_settings.coach_fee_access` (`none`/`view`/`collect`, default `collect`) | In a small club the coach is the first person a parent hands money to; a full list of the family's payments would reveal financial data from outside the group (social relief discounts, other sections) | Separate "orders" and "fees" screens; full payer balance for the coach; no setting | Two screens for a single conversation with a parent at the pitch; the family balance is data outside the coach's scope; some clubs do not want the coach dealing with money (Q-029) |
| **DD-33** Treasurer role `vereino_treasurer` seeded in `setup.ts` with features `payments.view`, `payments.manage`, `payments.bank.import`, `payments.cash.record`, `payments.cash.settle`, `payments.orders.manage` (without `people.*`, `groups.*`, `activities.*`, `club_core.manage`, `facilities.*`); granted by an active `people_role_assignments.role = treasurer` (no section or place), for which `people.scopeService` returns `club_wide` limited to the `payments` module; the coach units report and coach settlement (`activities.reports.view`, `club_core.contracts.manage`) stay with the administrator. `vereino_club_admin` is seeded with the concrete module wildcards (`club_core.*`, `people.*`, `groups.*`, `activities.*`, `communication.*`, `payments.*`, `facilities.*`), so the administrator has the same finance rights (Q-037). This splits the draft's single `club_admin` role | Clubs separate the treasurer function from the president/administrator; the treasurer needs neither people records nor PESEL (Polish national ID number) | Only the administrator handles the cash | Would force giving the treasurer full access to personal data |

## Domain Vocabulary and Business Rules

Terms not listed here (club, section, place, person, group, activity, session, role assignment, record scope) are defined in the club management specification.


| Term / invariant | Precise meaning or rule | Source of truth | Failure behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fee schedule (fee) | `period` ∈ `monthly` / `one_off` / `per_occurrence`; attached to a group **or** an activity; amount in grosz (minor units) | `payments_fees` | Both or neither → 422 |
| Sibling discount | Calculated at club level, not section level: for a payer and period, wards with monthly charges across all sections are ranked by date of birth (oldest first); full rate for the first, the `sibling` rule discount for each subsequent one; which child pays the full rate and which fee kinds the rule covers are the rule's conditions (Q-006, D-022) | `payments_discount_rules` | Several matching rules → the most favorable one for the payer applies, stored in `discount_rule_id` |
| Charge | Amount to be paid: `source_type` `fee` / `rental` / `order` / `manual`; debtor (`person_id` or `payer_institution_id`), payer (`payer_person_id`), `period_label` (`YYYY-MM` for monthly), `payee` `club` / `owner_institution`; statuses `open` → `partially_paid` → `paid`, `cancelled`, `forfeited` | `payments_charges` | Unique (`fee_id`, `person_id`, `period_label`) → idempotent charge generation |
| Payer | Active guardian with `is_primary_payer`; for an adult participant without such a guardian — themselves; for a minor without a payer, the charge gets the `payer_missing` flag | `payments` | `payer_missing` visible on the administrator's list; it cannot be paid online |
| Payment | `method` `cash` / `transfer` / `online`; sum of payments ≤ charge amount; online only for the full amount of open charges; in `payer_pays` mode the online surcharge (`online_fee_percent` × amount + `online_fee_fixed`) is a separate `fee` line of the checkout and is not a payment of the charge | `payments_payments` | Overpayment → 422; duplicate webhook → no-op (unique `gateway_transaction_id` + `charge_id`) |
| Checkout fulfilment (DD-42) | A checkout is fulfilled once, when the provider reports `captured` and the amount received equals the checkout total; the amount is spread over its charges from the oldest. Money received is never ignored: a capture found after the checkout was marked `failed` or `expired` (late capture) is still fulfilled. When a charge of the checkout is no longer `open` at fulfilment (paid in cash, by transfer or cancelled meanwhile), its share becomes an online overpayment flagged "to refund" on the administrator's list instead of a payment of that charge (S10.3, Q-049) | `payments_checkouts`, `payments_payments` | Amount received ≠ checkout total → no booking, the checkout is flagged `amount_mismatch` for the administrator |
| Online refund | Only from Vereino: the administrator refunds an `online` payment (in full or in part, also an overpayment flagged "to refund") through `paymentGatewayService.refundPayment` with an operation id; the result is booked as a reversing payment that references the original and stores the Stripe refund id (S10.4); the charge's status is derived again. A refund made in the Stripe Dashboard does not reach Vereino (Q-050) | `payments_payments` | Refund larger than the not-yet-refunded part → 422; gateway error → 502, nothing booked; retry with the same operation id → the same result |
| Payer code | A permanent, club-unique code of the paying person (e.g., `GW-K7Q2`), shown to the guardian on `/club/payments` and in charge emails; entered in the transfer reference, it unambiguously identifies the payer | `payments_payer_refs` | Unknown code → match by surname (to confirm) or unmatched |
| Transfer matching | 1) payer code in the reference → payer; amount allocated from the oldest open charge; full coverage of charges → `booked` (automatically when `auto_book_bank_matches`), overpayment/underpayment → `suggested`; 2) without a code: exactly one payer whose surname or ward's surname appears as a whole word in the sender or reference → `suggested`; 3) otherwise `unmatched`. Negative amount (debit) → skipped. Duplicate (same hash: date, amount, sender, reference, account number) → skipped | `payments_bank_transactions` | Wrong match → "Undo" removes the payments and restores the charge state (audit entry) |
| Cash payment | A `cash` payment with `collected_by_person_id` and `receipt_number` (`KP/YYYY/MM/NNN`, sequential within the club); amount ≤ sum of the selected charges | `payments_payments` | Overpayment → 422; correction only by a reversing entry |
| Order campaign | An order form for selected groups of one section: products, optional set, personalization (name, number), comments, `closes_on`, `payment_due_on` (≥ `closes_on`); statuses `draft` → `open` → `closed` (manually or automatically after `closes_on`) → `ordered` → `delivered` → `completed`; form editable only in `draft`/`open`; a price change does not change orders already placed | `payments_order_campaigns`, `payments_order_products` | Out-of-sequence transition → 409; deleting a product used in orders → 409 |
| Order | One per child in a campaign; placed by the guardian of a child from the campaign's groups (in the app or from a personal link) or by an adult group member for themselves; products with a required answer must have a size or "not ordering"; at least one line; print number 1–99, unique within the group in the campaign; creates an `order` charge (payer = person ordering, due date = `payment_due_on`, price captured at order time); a change until `closes_on` recalculates the charge, but not below the amount paid; cancellation only without payments | `payments_orders`, `payments_order_lines` | After closing → 409 `campaign_closed`; number taken → 422; new amount < amount paid → 409 |
| Set | A `set` product with at least two component products; the guardian chooses component sizes, and the set price is charged instead of individual prices; at most one set per form; in the supplier summary set components are counted under their own products | `payments_order_products`, `payments_order_lines.component_sizes` | Set without a component size → 422 |
| Order handover | `handed_over_at` + `handed_over_by_person_id`; possible from `delivered`; with an unpaid charge requires confirmation (`confirmUnpaid`), the charge remains open | `payments_orders` | Before delivery → 409 |
| Cash held by collector / cash handover | A `cash` payment with `collected_by_person_id` of a person without `payments.cash.settle` and `cash_handover_id = null`; the treasurer (or administrator) confirms receipt of selected payments of that person — a handover `RG/YYYY/MM/NNN` is created with an amount and a list of payments (the UI calls the queue "Cash to settle" and the route is `/backend/payments/cash-custody`; the entity and the prose use "handover") | `payments_cash_handovers` | Payment already settled or from another collector → 409 |
| Group charges (coach view) | For a child in group G: `fee` charges from group G's fee schedule (current period and all open ones) — when `coach_fee_access ≠ none` — and `order` charges from campaigns covering G (except completed and paid); amount = amount due after discount, without the discount name; a charge without a payer — visible, but cash cannot be accepted | `payments` (view) | Child or charge outside scope → 403; cash for a membership fee with `coach_fee_access ≠ collect` → 403 |

## User Journeys

### Journey J-004 — Membership fees (phase 4)

1. The treasurer, in `/backend/payments/fees`, sets "Żaki fee PLN 150 / month" and the rule "siblings −30%".
2. On the 1st day of the month the system generates charges: Ola (the older child, in a group of another section) — the full rate of her group; Jaś as the second child — PLN 105 (150 − 30%); payer Kasia; payment due on the 10th day.
3. Kasia, on `/club/payments`, selects both charges and pays via Stripe; on return she sees "Paid", and the treasurer sees the online payment attached to the charge.
4. A family paying in cash: the treasurer records the payment manually; partial payment → "partially paid".
5. Errors: payment declined → the charges remain open, message and retry; duplicate webhook → one payment; charge without a payer → flagged on the administrator's list.

### Journey J-009 — Order campaign: kits for the academy (phase 4)

1. The administrator, in `/backend/payments/orders/create`, builds the "Academy kit 2026/27" form: section Akademia piłkarska and its groups, closing date, payment due date, a set (shirt + shorts + socks) at a set price, single products with children's and adult sizes, print name and number, a comments question; "Save and open collection" returns the text with a link, which they paste into the Messenger group chat.
2. Kasia, signed in, opens `/club/orders` (`GET /api/payments/order-campaigns?for=me` lists the campaigns covering her wards) and orders for Jaś: the set with component sizes, number 7 (the taken numbers of Żaki are greyed out), contact details; she sees the live total and, after saving, an `order` charge on `/club/payments` and a copy by email. A guardian without an account orders from the personal link in the email (`/club/o/[token]`).
3. Kasia pays the charge online; another family pays the coach in cash at the pitch (J-010) or by transfer with the payer code (matched from the statement, REQ-030). Until the closing date Kasia may change the size or cancel an unpaid order.
4. After the closing date the campaign closes automatically; the administrator downloads the supplier summary (quantities per product and size, prints, CSV), marks the campaign as ordered from the supplier and later as delivered — orderers receive the "ready for pickup from the coach" email.
5. Coach Marek, in `/club/coach/payments`, marks the handover of Jaś's kit; handing over an unpaid kit requires confirming the warning, and the charge stays open.
6. Errors: order after closing → 409 `campaign_closed`; number already taken → 422; reducing the order below the amount paid → 409; a 14-year-old participant opening the campaign → 403; an expired personal link → 410.

### Journey J-010 — Coach group payments and cash handover to the treasurer (phase 4)

1. Before training, Marek opens `/club/coach/payments`: for each child of Żaki he sees the group's membership fees (current and overdue, per the club setting `coach_fee_access`) and kit orders with their lines — amount due, due date and status, without the discount reason and without charges of other sections.
2. A parent pays PLN 255 in cash for the membership fee and the kit at once: Marek selects both charges (`GET /api/payments/cash/charges?payerPersonId=` lists only the charges he may collect), enters the amount, and the system issues one KP number, applies the amount from the oldest charge and emails the confirmation to the payer; the charge is credited immediately. He sends an overdue reminder to another payer and a reminder to guardians who have not ordered.
3. "Cash with you" shows Marek the total he holds with the KP list; the treasurer Iwona sees the same payments in `/backend/payments/cash-custody` under "Cash to settle".
4. Iwona receives the cash from Marek and confirms the selected payments: a handover `RG/YYYY/MM/NNN` with the amount and the list of payments is created, and the payments leave the collector's balance.
5. Errors: cash for a membership fee when `coach_fee_access ≠ collect` → 403; a child outside the coach's groups → 403; amount greater than the selected charges → 422; settling a payment that is already in a handover → 409.

## Security, Privacy, and Compliance

The authorization, tenant-isolation and sensitive-data rules of `2026-10-20-vereino-club-management.md` apply. Bank statements are kept in the private `attachments` partition and read only with `payments.bank.import`.

- **Orders:** a campaign link from Messenger without a session shows only the name, description and deadline; children and orders are visible after sign-in or via a personal token (hash, valid until the campaign closes, one guardian); a link request always returns 202. The orderer's contact details and the child's name for printing are visible to the administrator and the group coaches; the supplier export contains only name, number, group and size — no parent contact details. A minor participant sees neither orders nor prices. Every cash payment has a KP number, a collector and — after handover — a handover number.

## Risks and Tradeoffs


| Risk / tradeoff | Impact | Mitigation / detection | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicate or late webhooks | double payment | uniqueness (`gateway_transaction_id`, `charge_id`); `payments_checkouts` | low |
| Wrong sibling discount rule (Q-006) | disputes with parents | the rule is configurable by the administrator with the D-022 default and shown explicitly on the charge | low |
| Cost of Stripe fees and BLIK/P24 not exercised yet (Q-016) | parents will not pay online | manual posting always available; the club's own Stripe account and `online_fee_mode` are decided (D-022, Q-016); `gateway_stripe` passes `paymentTypes` straight to `payment_method_types`, so BLIK and P24 are reachable in code (DD-42) — a sandbox run in phase 4 step 1 confirms them | medium |
| Late capture — a payer retries in the Payment Element after a decline, or pays after the checkout expired; the gateway transaction is already terminal and raises no `captured` event (V-5) | money taken, charge still open, reminders sent | the fulfilment runs from the return page and the 15-minute sweep, not only from the event (DD-42); TEST-057 | low |
| Refund made in the Stripe Dashboard | Vereino still shows the charge as paid | refunds only from Vereino (Q-050); Stripe refund webhooks are not subscribed; the nightly `payments:verify-invariants` drift report (S10.6) | medium |
| Webhook rate limit of `payment_gateways`: 60 requests a minute per client IP, one shared bucket behind a proxy without `RATE_LIMIT_TRUST_PROXY_DEPTH` | a burst of Stripe events gets 429; Stripe retries for up to 3 days | subscribe only `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed` and `payment_intent.canceled`; set the proxy depth on Railway; the sweep books what a delayed webhook would | low |
| Cash for kits held by coaches without a handover | cash discrepancies, disputes with parents | KP number on every payment, email to the payer, "Cash with you" for the coach, the "Cash to settle" view, and numbered handovers (DD-31) | low |
| Kit sales and tax obligations (Q-034) | the club must issue sales documents | club setting `order_sales_document` (D-022); 1.0 records orders and payments only, receipt and invoice come later (D-023) | low — accepted by the owner |
