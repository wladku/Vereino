## TLDR

Vereino is an open-source Open Mercato application for a small multi-section club (association) whose main section is a football academy, alongside which non-football sections operate — swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading — as a full-fledged use case (D-011). The reference scale is ~200 players in the academy; this describes scale, not a system limit. Pilot club: to be chosen; it must administer a municipal facility (D-025). The example club used throughout this document is "Dev Academy": five sections (a football academy with eight birth-year groups, a swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading) and an Orlik 2024 pitch complex it manages on behalf of its municipality. Vereino replaces Messenger groups, Excel and phone calls: one person record with multiple roles and one account, sections with coordinators, groups of two equal kinds (team `team` and class group `class_group`), activities with recurrence that generate sessions, per-role calendars — a single one for a parent whose children are in different sections, declared and actual attendance, coach substitutions with automatic unit settlement, announcements with in-app notifications and email, membership fees with online payment via Stripe, and club facilities (including the municipal Orlik) with zones, an owner (municipality) report and booking calendars: staff of any section book a facility immediately (e.g., a coach for a tournament), while an external person submits a booking request in the public facilities calendar, which becomes valid only after administrator approval. The public club page shows sections, classes, schedule, terms, prices, instructors, materials and contact — the club turns each of these blocks on or off in its settings. We reuse Open Mercato modules: `directory`, `auth`, `notifications`, `attachments`, `entities`, `configs`, `audit_logs`, `dashboards`, `scheduler` (from phase 1), and in later phases `dictionaries`, `messages`, `payment_gateways` with `gateway_stripe`. Seven new application modules own the club domain. The smallest coherent outcome is phase 1 (`club_core` + `people`): club, sections, places, institutions, people, legal guardianship, roles and accounts, with a signed-in parent seeing their children; its implementation planning may start (D-021), while the pilot club is still to be chosen (D-025).

## Problem Statement

A small multi-section club today runs its schedule and attendance through several Messenger groups (separately for each section and group), membership fees in Excel, coach and instructor substitutions by phone, and Orlik bookings by calling the club; difficult cases leave no trail [DOCUMENT: note from the founders' meeting of 26.09.2026 — knowledge of the Gwiazda board, not a user interview].

- **Parent** of two children — often in different sections (football and swimming pool) — scrolls through 3–4 groups to find out whether practice is on, misses cancellations and answers "attending / not attending" in each group separately [ASSUMPTION]. The two guardians interviewed qualify this: regular training times are known from the start of the season; the friction is friendlies and tournaments (a gathering time that differed between a post and its poll, an address without a map pin that cost 30 minutes), messenger apps are muted, the schedule is checked the day before, and lifts are arranged in the group chat [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-founder.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-multi-club.md`]. A gathering time separate from the session start is not modelled (brief: Goals gap).
- **Coach or instructor** organizing a tournament or a training course checks by phone whether the pitch or the small training room is free; they cannot see the club's facilities calendar [ASSUMPTION — Władek's requirement of 2026-10-20].
- **External person** (another club, a school, a parent looking for classes) has nowhere to check which sections and classes the club runs, on what terms, and when a facility is free; they book only by phone [ASSUMPTION].
- **Coordinator of a non-football section** (swimming pool, zumba, pilates) has the same needs as the academy coordinator — groups, sessions, attendance, membership fees — but most market tools are modeled around a football team; Vibeclass is the one product found that presents multi-section clubs as a core case [BENCHMARK 2026-10-23: `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`, agent-checked; D-011].
- **Coach** collects declarations in a chat where they get lost; arranges substitutions by phone and then argues about units [ASSUMPTION]. Evidence against the dispute part: the coordinator says substitutions are rare, found among the coaches themselves, and described no dispute; the product owner recalls none in the last year [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`; brief A05].
- **Treasurer** does not see overdue charges in real time, sends reminders manually, and counts coach units and Orlik hours from memory [ASSUMPTION].
- **Coordinator**: the regular training timetable has no collisions — the coordinator and the administrator set it and publish it as a summer and a winter poster — but matches, tournaments and pitch maintenance (line marking, mowing, aeration) collide "very often", because the person who maintains the pitch plans work without the coordinator knowing [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`; D-017]. Orlik has no public schedule [ASSUMPTION].

ProTrainUp (PLN 125–899/month by plan) covers the calendar/attendance/fees core; on 2026-10-23 its pricing page also lists a "facilities and reservations" module from the Club Pro plan and bank statement parsing from Gold, while nothing was found about external rental requests, a public schedule without sign-in or an owner report [BENCHMARK 2026-10-23: `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`, agent-checked, not yet re-checked by a human; the gaps stay an ASSUMPTION until the demo check on the brief's Collection plan]. That it is heavier than a small club needs and keeps data with the vendor is a team assessment. The evidence about users is in `.ai/specs/product-brief.md`: two guardian accounts (one from a co-founder) and the academy coordinator's account (`research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-founder.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-multi-club.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`); nobody else outside the team has been interviewed, so the remaining claims about coaches, the treasurer and other clubs are hypotheses.

## Overview and Success Measures

- **Primary outcome:** the pilot club — the whole club, all sections — runs on Vereino for a full season without returning to Messenger for attendance (the club and the season are open, D-025) — ≥80% of attendance declarations submitted in the app, measured separately for each section (metric: `attendance` with `declared_by_person_id` ≠ null against the number of participants in `done` sessions).
- **Leading indicators:** number of sections with at least one active group in the system (target: all pilot sections, not just the academy); share of guardians with an active account after 4 weeks; share of `done` sessions with actual attendance checked off; share of charges paid before `due_date`; number of "needing a coach" sessions resolved before they start; monthly municipality report generated without manual corrections; rollout time of the pilot club from the first sign-in to the first group with declarations (vision board: one weekend).
- **Business measures tracked outside the product** (vision board): five other academies on Vereino by the end of 2027; an open-source repository with at least three contributors from outside the team.
- **Baseline:** `unknown — measurement plan`: before rollout the treasurer provides the number of families and overdue amounts at the end of the last month (a commitment from the treasurer interview script); Messenger declarations — counted manually in one group for 4 weeks.
- **Market / product reference:** ProTrainUp (benchmark 30.09.2026). We adopt the core: calendar, attendance, membership fees with reminders and online payment, multiple roles. We reject: coaching tools (drawings, exercise library, medical, scouting, statistics), because a small club would use a fraction of the features and PTU is strong there. We add (D-011): the club as a whole — multiple sections on equal footing, including non-football ones, and a single parent calendar across all sections (Vibeclass also presents multi-section clubs, so this is not unique; `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`) — plus the municipal facility administered for the owner (no product found with external rental requests, a public schedule and an owner report; an assessment from vendor pages) and data held by the club (open source, MIT).

## Goals

Priority per `moscow.md`, with adjustments resulting from Q-002 and Q-003. Scope added after discovery (`decisions/D-013-scope-added-after-discovery.md`): REQ-023…REQ-027 (Q-021…Q-024), REQ-028 and REQ-029 (product owner request 2026-10-20), REQ-030 (product owner request 2026-10-20; Q-028 and Q-030 open), REQ-031 (Q-029), REQ-032 (Q-031, Q-033; Q-034, Q-035 open), REQ-033 (Q-029, Q-032; Q-036 open), REQ-034 (Q-037), REQ-035 (Q-038); all of them are Must. REQ-022 was raised from Could to Must on 2026-10-23 (D-017). REQ-032 upgrades kit orders from MoSCoW Could to Must.

- **REQ-001** (Must, D-005) — A person is one record regardless of the number of roles and has at most one login account; permissions add up, the UI has a context switcher, and actions record the role in which they were performed.
- **REQ-002** (Must, D-004; minors without payments — product owner decision of 2026-10-20) — A guardian sees the profiles of all wards; a ward from the club's age threshold (`own_account_min_age`, set by the administrator; default 16, D-022, Q-007) may have their own account with a limited view; a younger ward has no account; consents and payments always stay on the guardian's side — a minor participant (under 18) does not see payments at all (no tile, no screen, no API); payments are seen and paid by the guardian, and an adult participant pays for themselves.
- **REQ-003** (Must, D-001, D-011) — The multi-section club has sections as first-class entities — each with its own coordinator (or several), its own groups, activities and permission scope — plus a place hierarchy (complex → pitch, pool, hall) and institutions (municipality, schools, clubs, companies). The number of sections and participants has no limit in the system.
- **REQ-004** (Must, D-003, D-011) — A group is a single entity of kind `team` (team) or `class_group` (class group: swimming pool, zumba, pilates); both kinds are equal — they have the same memberships with history, sessions, attendance, substitutions, announcements and membership fees; `team` only has extra fields (age category, league, season, matches).
- **REQ-005** (Must, D-002) — An activity with an RRULE recurrence generates sessions; a single session can be edited or canceled without breaking the series.
- **REQ-006** (Must; D-022, Q-012) — The calendar is a view of sessions filtered by role: participant, guardian (all wards across all sections — one calendar), coach or instructor (own groups + substitutions), coordinator (their sections + the "needing a coach" list), administrator (the whole club). Whether a guardian sees which other children are called up for, or declared for, a session is a club setting `show_called_up_children`: `none` (default) or `consented` — only the first name and last name of children whose guardian gave the consent `share_presence_with_guardians_at` in the ward's profile.
- **REQ-007** (Must, D-006, D-022) — Attendance declaration and actual attendance. Two settings apply per club, group or activity type:
  - **what is declared** (`declaration_kind`, Q-009 of the brief): `absence_only` — guardians report only that the child will not come, and silence counts as present for planning; or `confirm` — guardians confirm presence. Defaults: `absence_only` for `training`, `confirm` for `match`, `sparring`, `tournament` and `camp` (the coordinator's model, D-022);
  - **who declares** for a ward with an account (`immediate`, `guardian_confirm`, `guardian_only`) with a time limit and a default value (D-006).
  Actual attendance is always recorded by the coach.
- **REQ-008** (Must, D-007, Q-004) — A substitution changes the session's coach with full history; sessions without a coach go to the coordinator's list; the monthly units report per coach shows planned, handed over, taken over and balance; unit = one session of type `training` (team training or class group class) in status `done`, owed to whoever led it — the same in every section.
- **REQ-009** (Must, D-009 confirmed in Q-002) — Announcements to the club, section, group, activity, session and direct, with threads; recipients receive an in-app notification and an email; the author can generate a text with a link to paste into the group chat in a messaging app.
- **REQ-010** (Must) — A "case" is a thread marked as evidentiary: undeletable, with full history, visible to the section coordinator and the club administrator.
- **REQ-011** (Must; D-022, Q-006) — Membership fee schedule (per group or per activity), sibling discount, automatic charge generation and an overdue list. The sibling rule is set by the administrator in the discount rule's conditions: which child pays the full rate (default: the oldest) and which fee kinds it covers (default: monthly fees only).
- **REQ-012** (Must, D-010 confirmed in Q-003; D-022, Q-016) — A guardian or adult participant pays membership fees online via Stripe (the club's own Stripe account); the administrator manually records cash and bank transfers. The online payment commission is a club setting `online_fee_mode`: `club_pays` (default) — the payer pays exactly the charge; or `payer_pays` — a surcharge of `online_fee_percent` of the amount plus `online_fee_fixed` is shown as a separate line before payment and added to the checkout amount, never to the charge.
- **REQ-013** (Must, D-008) — Facility: hourly zones, closures, rental rates in three categories, manually entered bookings (unit of 90 min, free cancellation up to 24 h before, payment to the municipality's account), detection of conflicts with club sessions and other bookings, facility calendar for the facility attendant.
- **REQ-014** (Must, D-008) — Public facility schedule without sign-in, without personal data, at a permanent address suitable for a QR code.
- **REQ-015** (Must, D-008) — Report for the facility owner for a month or quarter: hours in zones (including club time: sessions and internal bookings), number and value of bookings, payments to the municipality's account, incidents, maintenance work.
- **REQ-016** (Should; D-008 scope, priority per `moscow.md`) — Facility attendant log (opening, closing, condition before and after, groups present), incidents and entry bans that block bookings.
- **REQ-017** (Should, H6) — Personal link for attendance declaration without sign-in, sent by email; a link pasted into the group chat does not reveal data without authentication.
- **REQ-018** (Should) — Camp as an activity of type `camp` with a one-off fee and a participant list.
- **REQ-019** (Should, D-011) — For `team` groups: PZPN (Polish Football Association) age categories as a club-editable dictionary and season as an entity. `class_group` groups do not have these fields and lose nothing.
- **REQ-020** (Should) — For `team` groups: match and friendly with an opponent selected from the list of external teams and with a score.
- **REQ-021** (Should; D-022, Q-014 of the brief) — Monthly coach settlement for export, in the model the club chooses (`coach_settlement_model`):
  - `units_per_contract` — base + units × unit rate + matches × match rate + away trips × trip rate, per contract;
  - `fixed_base_adjusted` — a fixed monthly base, minus a fixed amount for each session of the coach's own groups they did not lead, plus the same amount for each session they led as a substitute, plus a fixed amount per away trip (the academy coordinator's practice).
  The contract carries the rates of the chosen model; the report shows the formula it used.
- **REQ-022** (Must; D-008 scope, raised from Could by D-017, Q-040…Q-042) — Facility maintenance and inspections, split between club and municipality.
  - **Maintenance work in the calendar.** Work with a time window on a place (line painting, mowing, aeration, irrigation, brushing) is planned in the facility calendar. It occupies the slot the way a closure does:
    - a new session, internal booking or rental, or an external request, in that window returns 409 `maintenance_scheduled`;
    - existing ones in that window get the reschedule flag, with the same notifications as a closure;
    - staff see the kind of work and who planned it;
    - the public calendar shows the slot as "unavailable" without details.
  - **Recurring tasks by date** (daily, annual, 5-year inspections) send reminders before the due date.
  - **Recurring tasks by use** ("every N hours of use", e.g. brushing the artificial turf) count the hours the place was occupied since the last completion and send a reminder when the threshold is reached.
  - Planned by people with the feature `facilities.maintenance.manage`. By default these are the administrator and section coordinators; the feature can be granted to anyone, for example the person who maintains the pitch, without a new role.
- **REQ-023** (Must, Q-021, Q-022) — Internal booking: a coach, instructor, coordinator or administrator of any section selects in the facilities calendar any club facility (pitch, small training room, swimming pool…), a date and hours, and books it immediately, without approval, if the slot is free (opening hours, no closure, no conflict) — in any zone. The booking is visible to staff with its purpose and the person who booked it. A booking for a group creates an event in the group's calendar (REQ-027).
- **REQ-024** (Must, Q-021, Q-023) — External request: an external person selects a free "bookable" slot in the club's public facilities calendar and submits a request (contact details, purpose, acceptance of the rules, email confirmation). The request is not valid without administrator approval; until a decision it blocks the slot against other external requests for at most 48 h and expires without a decision; an internal booking can override it (the requester receives an email). After approval a booking is created with a calculated amount and a charge payable to the facility owner's account (as in REQ-013).
- **REQ-025** (Must, Q-021, Q-024) — Public club page `/p/[clubSlug]`: list of sections and facilities; a page for each section with a description, classes (groups marked as public) with a schedule (days, hours, place), age category or level, terms, instructor, downloadable materials and contact. Content is entered in the admin panel on the section and on the group; the club settings decide which blocks are published (description and schedule, terms, prices, instructors, materials, contact, facilities calendar, online requests). The instructor's name additionally requires that person's consent.
- **REQ-026** (Must, Q-024) — Prices block on the section page: membership fee from the group's fee schedule and sibling discount, published only when the club has enabled the prices block.
- **REQ-027** (Must, Q-022; amends D-002 with the `tournament` activity type) — A one-off event for their own group (`tournament` or `other`) is created by a coach or instructor without the coordinator's consent; a tournament has a list of invited external teams.
- **REQ-028** (Must) — The administrator uploads the club logo; the administrator or section coordinator can upload a separate section logo. A section without its own logo shows the club logo. The club logo appears in the header of the admin panel, the member interface and the public page; the section logo — on the section tile, the section page and next to its groups.
- **REQ-029** (Must) — In the facility calendar, the administrator quickly sets weekly booking availability for external requests in bulk: selects one or more facilities, weekdays (shortcuts Mon–Fri, weekend, daily) and hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 16:00–22:00) or different hours for individual days, plus a validity period. By default the new hours replace the existing availability on the selected days; they can also be appended. Availability does not free up occupied time — club classes and bookings and rentals during those hours remain occupied, and only a free segment of at least 90 min can be requested. A day's availability can be removed with one click.
- **REQ-030** (Must) — Bank statement reconciliation: the administrator imports a statement of the club's bank account (CSV in the bank's column layout or MT940); the system matches incoming payments to charges — a transfer with the payer code in the reference and an amount covering whole charges (oldest first) is booked automatically when auto-booking is enabled; transfers matched by surname and amount, with an overpayment or underpayment, go to "to confirm"; the rest go to "unmatched" (assign manually or skip, e.g., a donation). Debits on the account are skipped, a repeated import does not create duplicates, and every booked entry can be undone. Each payer has a permanent payer code shown with payments.
- **REQ-031** (Must) — Cash payment: the administrator (and a person granted the feature `payments.cash.record`), in the admin panel or on a phone, selects the payer, their charges and the amount (a partial payment is applied from the oldest charge); the system assigns a receipt number (KP/year/month/no.), records who accepted it, and optionally sends the payer an email confirmation; the collector sees the total cash accepted that day to hand over to the treasurer, and the administrator — the "Cash payments" view.
- **REQ-032** (Must; MoSCoW Could → Must on the product owner's decision, Q-031…Q-033 — it replaces the existing Google Form) — Order campaign (e.g., kits): the administrator creates an order form in the admin panel — name, description, section and groups, closing date and payment due date, products with children's and adult sizes (the price may depend on the size) and without a size, an optional set of several products at a set price, a required answer (size or "not ordering"), name and number for printing (number unique within the group), a question for comments — and opens collection with a text containing a link to paste into the Messenger group chat. The guardian of a child from the selected groups orders in the app or — without an account — from a personal link sent to the email address known to the club; they receive a copy by email and may change or cancel the order until the deadline. Each order creates a charge for the person ordering, payable online, by bank transfer with the payer code (matched from the statement, REQ-030) or in cash. After the deadline collection closes automatically; the administrator downloads the supplier summary (quantities per product and size, prints, CSV), marks the order as placed with the supplier and as delivered (people who ordered receive an email "ready for pickup from the coach"), and the coach or administrator marks the handover — of an unpaid kit only after confirming a warning. A minor participant does not see orders or prices (REQ-002). The sales document for an order is a club setting `order_sales_document` (`none`, `receipt`, `invoice`; D-022, Q-034); 1.0 implements only `none`, and the other two values are reserved for a later release.
- **REQ-033** (Must) — My groups' payments for the coach and cash handover: the coach (and the coordinator in their sections) sees in the mobile app the charges of children from their groups — membership fees of that group (current period and overdue) and orders (e.g., kits) with their lines — with the amount due, due date and status, but without the discount reason and without charges from other sections. They accept cash for one or more of a child's charges at once (KP number, email confirmation to the payer, partial payment applied from the oldest), remind the payer of overdue amounts, remind guardians who have not ordered, and mark kit handover. In the payment settings the club decides whether coaches see membership fees and whether they may accept cash for them (`none` — orders only, `view` — fees view-only, `collect` — fees and cash; default `collect`). Cash accepted by a person without settlement rights is "held by the collector" until the treasurer confirms receiving it in "Cash to settle" based on the coach's entries; the handover has a number (RG/year/month/no.), a list of payments and an amount. The coach sees the total cash they hold. The payment is credited to the parent immediately, regardless of the handover.
- **REQ-034** (Must) — Treasurer role: a person responsible solely for the club's finances — fee schedules and discounts, charges and overdue amounts, statement imports, cash payments, order campaigns and accepting cash from coaches (handovers) — without access to people records, groups, classes, club settings and facilities. The administrator has the same rights, so in a small club the administrator can be the treasurer; the treasurer role is assigned by the administrator in the person record.
- **REQ-035** (Must) — Multilingual interface: every user-facing text of Vereino (member interface, admin panel, public pages, system emails, notifications, PDF/CSV headers) comes from translation catalogs; 1.0 ships complete English and Polish catalogs, and adding another language means adding catalog files only, without code changes. Each person chooses their interface language (default: the club's default language); public pages follow the browser language with a language switcher; system emails and notifications are sent in the recipient's language. Dates, times, numbers and amounts are formatted for the selected language, in the club's time zone and currency. Content entered by the club (section and group names, descriptions, conditions, announcements, product names) stays in the language in which it was written — one content language per club.
- **REQ-036** (Must; D-023, Q-018, Q-044) — Bulk import of people and families from a file: the administrator uploads a CSV or XLSX export (for example the club's current Google sheet) with columns for the child, the guardian(s), the group and contact details, maps the columns once, sees a preview with every row classified — new person, matched existing person, needs review, error — and commits. A person is matched by first name, last name and the PESEL or another identifier (`external_id`), both stored encrypted and compared through a deterministic hash; a row without a PESEL or identifier is never merged automatically and goes to a manual review queue. A repeated import of the same file creates no duplicates, and every import is recorded with who ran it and what it created.
- **REQ-037** (Must; D-023, Q-045) — Guardians' contact list for coaches, by consent: a guardian gives or withdraws, in their profile, the consent `share_contact_with_coaches_at`; a coach or instructor then sees the phone and email of the consenting guardians of children in their own groups only, on the attendance list and the group's member list, with every view audited. Without consent the coach reaches the guardian only through announcements and threads (REQ-009).
- **REQ-038** (Should; D-023, Q-015 of the brief) — Hours report for facilities the club rents from others (school halls in winter): for each place flagged `rented_by_club`, the hours of `done` sessions per month, with the groups and coaches, exported to CSV, so that the club can check the owner's bill against its own log.
- **REQ-039** (Should; D-023, Q-046) — League fixtures from PZPN / Łączy nas piłka for `team` groups: the coordinator imports the fixtures published for a team (date, time, opponent, venue, home or away) and the system creates or updates `match` sessions, without duplicating fixtures already imported. The source is an exported file (ICS or CSV) until V-14 shows an API the club may use; results and player data are not imported.

## Non-goals

- Coaching tools: training drawings, exercise library, medical module, scouting and assessments, individual statistics (`moscow.md` Won't; benchmark). Kept after the coordinator's wish for training plans tied to a possible PZPN certification (D-016); revisited when the club commits to the certification and coaches confirm the need.
- **Push in 1.0** (Q-002): push notifications return together with the native app. In 1.0 the channels are in-app notifications and email. This is a deliberate deviation from `moscow.md`, where push was a Must.
- Messenger/WhatsApp integration (D-009) — replaced by a text with a link to paste into the group chat.
- Gateways other than Stripe, including a custom Przelewy24/PayU adapter (Q-003).
- Online payment for renters and accounts for regular renters (online requests are in 1.0 — REQ-024 — but payment still goes by bank transfer to the facility owner's account).
- Page editor (CMS) — the public club page is assembled from section, group, session, fee schedule and facility data, not from arbitrary pages; the `content` module in the facts contains only the static `/privacy` and `/terms` pages.
- Projects and grants, shop, development history from devices. (Kit orders are in scope as order campaigns — REQ-032.) Grant settlement — about 12 grants this year, the coordinator's biggest time sink, with a double claim risking a whole grant — stays out of 1.0 (D-016) and has its own discovery track as a possible separate product or module (D-018).
- Accounting for coach payouts and export to payroll — in 1.0 only calculation and CSV export (D-007).
- Multiple clubs on one instance. The architecture does not prevent it: every record has `tenant_id` + `organization_id`, and club = organization. A guardian's view across several clubs is later (D-014, brief N10); a calendar subscription link was considered and left open.
- Translating club-entered content (multilingual section descriptions, group names, announcements) — one content language per club in 1.0 (Q-038); the interface itself is multilingual (REQ-035).
- Native app. (Integration with PZPN league fixtures is REQ-039 and bulk import of people is REQ-036 since D-023.)
- Volunteer duty sign-up for sessions (planned for 1.1).
- Coaches' cash expenses (referees paid from collected cash, with the referee's declaration): they stay in the cash notebook and with the accountant; Vereino records only incoming payments (brief Q16, D-023).
- Receipts and invoices for order campaigns: 1.0 offers only `none` in the `order_sales_document` setting (D-022, D-023).
- Read receipts for announcements — 1.0 shows only the email delivery status per recipient (REQ-009).
- Per-player match history — 1.0 keeps only the membership history (`moscow.md` Could).
- CSV export of charges and payments for the accountant — in 1.0 only the coach units report, the owner report and the supplier summary export CSV.
- PWA packaging (manifest, installability) — 1.0 is a responsive web site.
- Attachments in announcements and threads — materials are published on the section page (REQ-025) and incident files go through `attachments`.

## Proposed Solution

Seven application modules in `src/modules/<id>/`, each with entities, commands, API routes (`makeCrudRoute` or command routes), pages and events:

| Module | Owns | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| `club_core` | club settings with publication settings, sections (with public content), places (hierarchy), institutions, contracts; public club and section page assembled from blocks provided by other modules | 1 (groups and schedule block — 2, prices — 4, facilities — 5; coach contracts — 2, management agreement — 5) |
| `people` | people, legal guardianship with consents, role assignments, record scope service, account creation | 1 |
| `groups` | groups, memberships, seasons, external teams | 2 |
| `activities` | activities with RRULE, one-off events (tournament) created by the coach, sessions, attendance, declaration policy, substitutions, units report and coach settlement, declaration links, public schedule block | 2 (links — 3) |
| `communication` | scoped threads, "cases", shareable text, email distribution | 3 |
| `payments` | fee schedules, discounts, charges, payments, online payment sessions, statement import, cash and its handover, order campaigns (e.g., kits) | 4 |
| `facilities` | zones, closures, rental rates, internal staff bookings, external rentals (entered by staff or requested online with administrator approval), facilities calendar for staff, public facilities calendar, log, incidents, bans, maintenance, owner report | 5 |

No module assumes that a section is a football section: section, group (`team` or `class_group`), activity, session, attendance, substitution, announcement and membership fee work the same for the academy, the swimming pool and zumba. Football-specific elements (PZPN age categories, league, season, match with opponent and score) are extra fields of the `team` group, not a separate path.

How this solves the problems: the parent gets one mobile "Today" screen with the sessions of all children from all sections, one-tap declaration, messages and payments (REQ-002, 006, 007, 009, 012); the coach — an attendance list on the phone and absence reporting, after which the coordinator assigns a substitute and the units transfer automatically (REQ-007, 008); the treasurer — charge generation, discounts and an overdue list with online payment (REQ-011, 012); the coordinator — a schedule with place conflict detection and a public Orlik schedule (REQ-005, 013, 014); the board — an undeletable register of cases (REQ-010).

Why this is the smallest platform-native solution: identity, RBAC, data isolation, notifications, files, custom fields, audit, scheduled jobs, message storage and the payment gateway come from installed modules (see *Reuse and Ownership Map*). Custom code is written only where module-facts contain no equivalent: the club domain, sessions with attendance, per-section/place/ward scope, club charges, and facility rules from the Orlik rules.

Interfaces: staff configure the club in the admin panel (`/backend/...`) using `DataTable` and `CrudForm`; the daily work of all roles — parent, participant, coach, facility attendant — happens in the mobile member interface `/club/...` (module frontend pages with `requireAuth`), per Q-001: one `auth` account for everyone.

**Scope derivation:** `tenantId` and `organizationId` always come from the authenticated context. Every list route adds a scope filter from `people.scopeService` after the organization filter; no linked person or an empty scope yields an empty list, and a write outside the scope yields 403. Operations without a login session are the public club, section and facilities calendar pages, the booking request with its email confirmation, and the declaration link pages: scope is derived from `club_settings.public_slug` (+ section/place slug) or from the token record (hash), never from the request body. The only operation in system scope (`organizationId: null`) is that single read-only, index-backed lookup of the slug or token hash, which immediately narrows every further query to the found `tenantId`/`organizationId` (DD-35, V-12); nothing else runs in system scope.

## Architecture and Data Flow

```text
Guardian /club (Today) ─► GET /api/activities/calendar?as=guardian ─► people.scopeService (ward_person_ids)
                                                                    ─► groups (memberships on the day) ─► activities_occurrences
Guardian ─► POST /api/activities/attendance/declare ─► activities.attendance.declare (policy, scope)
                                                    ─► event activities.attendance.declared ─► notifications (guardian/coach)
Coordinator /backend ─► POST /api/activities/activities ─► activities.activities.create
                                                        ─► RRULE expansion (horizon) ─► activities_occurrences
                                                        ─► [phase 5] facilities guard: conflicts with bookings/closures
scheduler (daily) ─► worker activities:generate-occurrences ─► appends sessions up to the horizon
Coach /club ─► POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/absence ─► coach_person_id = null ─► event ─► notifications (coordinator)
Coordinator ─► POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/coach ─► substitution entry ─► event ─► notifications (substitute)
Coach ─► PUT /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/attendance ─► status done ─► units report (query)
Cancellation ─► activities.occurrence.cancelled ─► [phase 3] communication: session thread + email (messages)
scheduler (1st day of the month) ─► payments:generate-charges ─► groups (members) + people (payer) ─► payments_charges
Guardian ─► POST /api/payments/checkout ─► paymentGatewayService (stripe) ─► redirect to Stripe
Stripe ─► webhook ─► payment_gateways ─► payment_gateways.payment.captured ─► payments subscriber (idempotent) ─► payments_payments
Facility attendant ─► POST /api/facilities/rentals ─► facilities.rentals.create ─► DI payments: charge (payee = municipality) in the same transaction
Guest ─► GET /api/facilities/public/{clubSlug}/places/{placeSlug}/schedule ─► zones + occupancy (no personal data)
Guest ─► POST …/places/{placeSlug}/requests ─► rental pending_verification ─► email with link ─► pending_approval (48 h hold)
        ─► notifications: administrators "request awaiting approval"
Administrator ─► POST /api/facilities/rentals/{id}/approve ─► requested + charge (DI payments) ─► email to the requester
Coach /club/places ─► POST /api/facilities/internal-bookings ─► occupancyService ─► confirmed (no approval)
                   ─► [for a group] DI activities: one-off event (tournament) ─► session occupies the facility
                   ─► conflicting pending_approval requests → superseded (same unit of work) ─► email after commit
Guest ─► GET /api/club_core/public/{clubSlug}/sections/{sectionSlug} ─► club_core (description, materials, contact)
        ─► optional DI: groups.publicCatalog, activities.publicSchedule, payments.publicPrices, facilities.publicPlaces
        ─► filter club_settings.publication + is_public + name consent
```

- **Module boundaries:** `club_core` owns the club structure and contracts; `people` — identity, guardianship and scope (the sole source of `scopeService`); `groups` — rosters over time; `activities` — sessions and everything that happens at a session (attendance, substitutions, units), because attendance and substitution must be transactionally consistent with the session; `communication` — conversation scope and cases; `payments` — club and municipality money; `facilities` — facility rules from the rules document. Dependencies (`requires`): `people` → `club_core`; `groups` → `people`; `activities` → `groups`; `communication` → `activities` (`occurrence` scope), `messages`; `payments` → `groups`, `payment_gateways`; `facilities` → `activities`, `payments`. No ORM relations between modules — only IDs, display name snapshots, events, DI services and mutation guards.
- **Shared packages:** 1.0 keeps all code in `packages/app/src/modules/<id>/`; code moves to a workspace package only when at least two modules reuse it or it is framework-free (AGENTS.md). Candidate content for later extraction: money, discount and period helpers and validation schemas → `@vereino/core`; the schedule-grid components (the V-8 fallback) → `@vereino/ui`; test-database and seed helpers → `@vereino/db`. `yarn typecheck:packages` is part of every phase's validation.
- **Extension points:** frontend and backend pages discovered from files; notification types (`notifications.ts`); message object type for the session (`message-objects.ts`); dashboard widgets (`widgets/dashboard/**`); `facilities` mutation guard on session save (`data/guards.ts`); schedule targets registered in `setup.ts`; encryption maps (`encryption.ts`); search configuration (`search.ts`); custom fields (`ce.ts`). We do not modify the code of installed modules.
- **Alternatives considered:** customer portal for parents (rejected in Q-001); one `club` module with all entities (rejected: phases would not be separately deployable, and permissions and events would get mixed); `staff` + `resources` + `planner` as the core (rejected in the reuse map).
- **Compatibility:** the application is new — there are no existing Vereino contracts. From the first deployment the following are protected per the root `BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md` (§2–§6, tier "protected at first deployment"): API routes and their error codes, IDs of events, ACL features, notification types and schedule targets, DI service interfaces (`people.scopeService` and the optional provider tokens) and the database schema; changes go through additive fields or a deprecation bridge, and every Changelog row that adds a surface names it. Enabling the `scheduler`, `messages`, `payment_gateways`, `gateway_stripe` modules does not change the behavior of already enabled modules.

## User Journeys

### Journey J-001 — Club structure and a family to start with (phase 1)

1. The club administrator, in `/backend/club/settings`, fills in the short name, public slug and account age threshold; in `/backend/club/sections` creates Dev Academy's five sections "Akademia piłkarska", "Basen", "Zumba", "Szachy" and "Cheerleader" (Q-005), and on the section record assigns a coordinator (Marek — academy, Ewa — swimming pool; the other three have no coordinator yet); a section without a coordinator is flagged with a warning; in `/backend/club/places` creates the complex "Orlik 2024" with the children "Boisko piłkarskie", "Boisko wielofunkcyjne", "Zaplecze szatniowe", a hall and an outdoor pool (`is_external`); in `/backend/club/institutions` — the municipality as the owner of the complex.
2. In `/backend/people/persons/create` they create Kasia (guardian) as well as Jaś (9 years old) and Ola (14 years old); on the person record, in the "Guardianship" tab, they link Kasia with the children and check "primary payer" and the consents.
3. On Kasia's record they choose "Create account": the system creates an `auth:user` with the roles `vereino_member` and `vereino_payer` (Kasia is an adult) and sends an email for setting a password. For Jaś the button is disabled with the explanation "below the account age threshold (13)". For Ola they create an account with a limited view — without the `vereino_payer` role, so Ola does not see payments.
4. Kasia sets a password, signs in and lands on `/club` — she sees Jaś's and Ola's profiles and the consents.
5. Errors: email already used by another account → 409 with a message at the field; saving a person record with an outdated version → 409 and the shared conflict dialog; a user without the `people.manage` feature sees the "Access denied" page.

### Journey J-003 — Cancellation and announcement (phase 3)

1. The coordinator cancels a session with the reason "downpour". Recipients (guardians of minors, participants with an account, the coach) receive an in-app notification and an email.

5. Errors: the email is not delivered → event `messages.message.email_failed`, status "not delivered" next to the recipient in the thread; a link request for an unknown email → the same response as for a known one (no enumeration).

3. After submitting: "Check your inbox — confirm your request using the link". Clicking the link → status "awaiting approval"; the slot in the public calendar appears as "requested — pending", and subsequent requests for that time are rejected with a message.
4. Administrators receive a notification; in `/backend/facilities/requests` (and the "Requests awaiting approval" widget) the administrator sees the request, conflicts, any match with the entry ban register, and approves → rental `requested` with a charge payable to the municipality's account; the requester receives an email with the amount, account number, payment deadline and free cancellation deadline. From then on as in J-005 from step 3.
5. Variants: rejection with a reason → email, slot free; no decision within 48 h → `expired`, email, slot free; email not confirmed within 1 h → request deleted; a request is never "booked" until the administrator approves it.

### Journey J-008 — A guest explores the club and its sections (phases 1–5)

### Journey J-009 — Order campaign: kits for the academy (phase 4)

1. The administrator, in `/backend/payments/orders/create`, builds the "Academy kit 2026/27" form: section Akademia piłkarska and its groups, closing date, payment due date, a set (shirt + shorts + socks) at a set price, single products with children's and adult sizes, print name and number, a comments question; "Save and open collection" returns the text with a link, which they paste into the Messenger group chat.
2. ### Journey J-010 — Coach group payments and cash handover to the treasurer (phase 4)

1. ## Data Models

Common columns of every entity: `id` UUID (primary key), `tenant_id` and `organization_id` UUID (required, only from trusted context, composite index with the most frequent filters), `created_at`, `updated_at` (optimistic locking version, returned as `updatedAt`), `deleted_at` (soft delete) — unless the row says otherwise. Amounts as integers in grosz (minor units) + the club currency. Tables prefixed with the module name. No ORM relations between modules: references are scalar IDs, and where a list shows a name from another module, a name snapshot is kept up to date by an event subscriber. Fields marked "encr." go into `encryption.ts` (`defaultEncryptionMaps`) and are read with decryption helpers using both scope IDs.

## API, Command, and Error Contracts

All CRUD routes go through `makeCrudRoute` (per-method `metadata` with `requireAuth` and `requireFeatures`, a separate `openApi` export, `indexer`, scope filter by `organizationId`); writes go through commands (`commands/*.ts`) with audit and undo where it makes sense. Updates and deletions require a version (`updatedAt`) and return 409 on conflict. Action routes are command routes with `metadata`, a Zod schema and `openApi`, running mutation guards before the write and side effects after the transaction commits. Conventions from the engineering standards (`.ai/specs/2026-10-21-engineering-standards-and-patterns.md`) apply to every route below: lists return the installed paged shape `{ items, total, page, pageSize, totalPages }` (S6.4); JSON keys are camelCase, instants ISO 8601 UTC, amounts integer grosz with `currency` (S6.3–S6.6); a missing lock token returns 400 and a stale one 409 (S5.2); every machine code named in the *Errors* column (`occurrence_place_conflict`, `declaration_closed`, `rental_conflict`, …) is declared once in its owning module's `lib/error-codes.ts`, returned as `{ error, code? }` (`code` present for every business error) and checked by `yarn contracts:check` (S6.7).

## Security, Privacy, and Compliance

- **Authorization:** every route has `requireFeatures`; every record read and write goes through `people.scopeService`; an `auth` role is never checked by name. Actions record an `acting_role` determined on the server side.
- **Tenant isolation:** `tenantId` + `organizationId` filters from context on every query, including in workers (the scope in the job payload comes from the schedule registration, not from user data); no scope = no data. Sessionless routes derive the scope from the public slug or the token hash through exactly one read-only, index-backed system-scope lookup (DD-35, V-12) — a deviation from "only an installed contract may use system scope" that the product owner approved on 2026-10-23 (D-024) for the case that V-12 finds no installed contract; every query after the lookup carries the found `tenantId` and `organizationId`.
- **Sensitive data:** field encryption per *Data Models* via `encryption.ts`; bank statements contain third-party data (senders, account numbers) — the file is kept in the private `attachments` partition, transaction fields are encrypted, access only with `payments.bank.import`, retention in line with accounting regulations (to be confirmed with the accountant); PESEL optional, visible only with `people.pesel.view`, duplicate detection via HMAC; link tokens stored as SHA-256; last-name search on encrypted fields per the `search.encrypted-column-list-filter` pattern (V-3); logs without PII or message content (`createLogger`); right to erasure via the anonymization command, which keeps accounting and attendance entries without personal data. Guardian consents with date and author. Children under 13 have no account, and their data is not visible to other parents.
- **External requests and the public page:** requester data encrypted, visible only to staff with `facilities` features; the data processing notice and the acceptance of the regulations are recorded with the request; a request is not valid without administrator approval and email confirmation; strict rate limit per IP and per email, honeypot field, requests blocked for a slot with a valid hold; an entry ban register match is only a hint for the administrator (not disclosed to the requester). The club page public API does not return blocks disabled in the settings or records without `is_public`; the instructor's name only with `publish_name_consent_at`; never lists of participants or guardians, or children's data.
- **Orders:** a campaign link from Messenger without a session shows only the name, description and deadline; children and orders are visible after sign-in or via a personal token (hash, valid until the campaign closes, one guardian); a link request always returns 202. The orderer's contact details and the child's name for printing are visible to the administrator and the group coaches; the supplier export contains only name, number, group and size — no parent contact details. A minor participant sees neither orders nor prices. Every cash payment has a KP number, a collector and — after handover — a handover number.
- **Abuse and failure modes:** public routes with rate limits (`metadata` of `makeCrudRoute` routes, V-10); a link request always returns 202 (no email enumeration); a token is limited to one session and one guardian's wards and expires at the end of the session; the public schedule contains no names; cases cannot be deleted or edited; monetary operations cannot be edited (reversing entries only); duplicate webhooks and retries are idempotent; all edits protected by a version (409). Stripe secrets only in the integration configuration, never in the spec, logs or comments.
- **Compliance (to be confirmed before the pilot):** account age threshold of 13 vs 16 in light of GDPR and information society services (D-004, Q-007) — a club setting, not a constant in code; club and facility regulations as an attachment; retention of former participants' data — Q-019.

## Implementation Phases

Phases in the order of the draft domain model (`research/2026-10-20-domain-model-draft.md`) and the product brief's Scope. Only the current phase may be in progress; in parallel — only independent slices of that phase. Each phase ends with its own evidence. Each phase submits for approval the enabling of modules in `src/modules.ts` and any new dependencies. Validation for each phase: `yarn verify` — the single gate of the engineering standards (S2), which includes `typecheck:packages`, the strict i18n check, `contracts:check` and `build` — phase tests via `yarn test:integration:vereino`, SQL review from `yarn db:generate` (no `yarn db:migrate` without approval). Each new module id is added to `packages/app/vereino-modules.json` in the slice that creates its folder, so the strict lint, formatting, i18n and contract checks cover it. Pure logic — RRULE horizon, declaration policy resolution, discount ranking, transfer matching, the unit and settlement formulas, occupancy and usage-hour counting, rate quoting — lives in each module's `lib/**` with unit tests in `__tests__/` at 100 % line, branch and function coverage, enforced by `yarn test` inside `yarn verify` (AGENTS.md, S13.6); routes, commands, subscribers and pages are covered by the integration tests below, never by mocked unit tests (S13.1). Every module ships `lib/error-codes.ts` and `api/errors.ts` (S6.7).

## Rollout, Migration, and Rollback

- **Migrations:** one per module per phase; `yarn db:generate` as a probe, SQL review, module snapshot; user approval before the migration is committed and before `yarn db:migrate`; post-deployment changes — corrective migrations.
- **Setup and data:** `seedDefaults` (idempotent): `vereino_*` roles with features, club settings, default declaration policies, PZPN (Polish Football Association) age category dictionary; `seedExamples`: the demonstration club "Dev Academy" (five sections — Akademia piłkarska, Basen, Zumba, Szachy, Cheerleader — with coordinators for the academy and the swimming pool; an Orlik (municipal sports pitch complex) with pitches and a swimming pool; the Żaki team, class groups "Basen — początkujący" and "Zumba wtorek"; a family with two children in different sections) — only on request. After adding features in an existing tenant: `yarn mercato auth sync-role-acls`.
- **Pilot rollout order** (at the pilot club, once chosen — D-025; Dev Academy's sections stand in for it): phase 1 → the administrator enters people and families of the club's sections (or imports them, REQ-036) → phase 2 with two groups from different sections (the Żaki team and the swimming pool class group) for 4 weeks with measurement of declarations → the remaining groups of all five sections → phases 3–5. The rollout owner at the pilot club (date, who trains) is open — Q-039, D-025. Each phase requires approval to enable modules in `src/modules.ts`.
- **Observability:** `createLogger` per module; events `scheduler.job.failed` and `messages.message.email_failed` as operational signals; the "unsettled" and "without payer" widgets as data quality indicators.
- **Rollback:** each phase is additive. Rolling back a phase = disabling its module in `src/modules.ts` and unregistering its jobs; data stays in the module's tables and comes back after re-enabling. ## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-001** — A person with guardian, coach, and coordinator roles has one record and one account; switches context in `/club`; actions record the role determined by the server; an unauthorized user gets 403.
- [ ] **AC-002** — The guardian sees and edits the consents of all their wards; a ward at or above the club's age threshold (default 16, set by the administrator) has an account with a restricted view; for a younger ward, account creation returns 422.
- [ ] **AC-003** — The administrator creates any number of sections and assigns each a coordinator (one person can coordinate several sections, a section without a coordinator is flagged); creates a place hierarchy and institutions; a cycle in the hierarchy and a stale version return 409 with a message in the form; deleting a section that has groups and deactivating a section that still has active groups both return 409.
- [ ] **AC-004** — A `team` group and a `class_group` group go through the same cycle (roster with history, sessions, attendance, substitution, announcement, membership fee); an overlapping membership returns 409; team fields and matches for `class_group` return 422.
- [ ] **AC-005** — A recurring activity generates sessions for the horizon; an edit to a single session survives a series change; a place conflict returns 409 with a list of conflicting sessions.
- [ ] **AC-006** — Each role sees exactly its own sessions in the calendar (TEST-008), including the substitute coach — the substitution session.
- [ ] **AC-007** — Declaration works according to the policy in all three modes, with a time limit; in `absence_only` kind (default for training) a guardian reports only absence and the coach's list counts the rest as expected; in `confirm` kind (default for matches) the list shows confirmed, declined and unanswered; after the session starts, a declaration returns 409.
- [ ] **AC-008** — The coach units report shows planned, handed over, taken over, and balance according to the rule "1 `done` training session = 1 unit"; substitution history is not editable.
- [ ] **AC-009** — An announcement to a scope reaches the correct recipients in the app and by email; cancelling a session sends one automatically; the text to paste contains a link without personal data.
- [ ] **AC-010** — Marking as a case is irreversible, and deleting or editing its messages returns 409.
- [ ] **AC-011** — Period generation is idempotent, applies the sibling discount as configured by the administrator (which child pays full, which fee kinds), and flags charges without a payer.
- [ ] **AC-012** — The guardian pays their charges via Stripe (the club's account) and sees the "Paid" status; in `payer_pays` mode the surcharge is shown as a separate line before payment and the charge itself is paid in full; a repeated webhook does not create a second payment; someone else's charge returns 403; a minor participant with an account does not see payments (no tile or screen, API 403).
- [ ] **AC-013** — A booking is possible only in the "bookable" zone without a conflict or ban; it calculates the amount and dates; creates a charge payable to the municipality; cancellation after the deadline results in forfeiture.
- [ ] **AC-014** — The public schedule works without signing in and contains no names of renters, groups, or people; an unknown slug returns 404.
- [ ] **AC-015** — The monthly owner report matches the data (hours in zones, bookings, payments, incidents, maintenance) and exports to CSV.
- [ ] **AC-016** — The attendant keeps a log and reports incidents within the scope of their place; an active ban blocks the renter's booking.
- [ ] **AC-017** — The personal link allows declaring attendance only for the guardian's wards for a single session; the link request does not reveal whether the email exists.
- [ ] **AC-018** — A camp is a `camp` activity with a participant list (phase 2) and a one-off fee that generates one charge per participant (phase 4).
- [ ] **AC-019** — A group has an age category from the dictionary and a season.
- [ ] **AC-020** — A match has an opponent from the list and a recorded score.
- [ ] **AC-021** — Coach settlement calculates the amount in the club's chosen model from the contract, names the model in the report and exports to CSV; the coach sees only their own row.
- [ ] **AC-022** — Planned maintenance work occupies the facility calendar: new sessions, bookings, rentals and requests in its window return 409 `maintenance_scheduled`, existing ones get the reschedule flag, and the public calendar shows the slot as unavailable without details. Tasks by date send reminders before the due date, and tasks by hours of use send a reminder when the threshold is reached. A person holding only the maintenance feature sees and plans only on their assigned places, without personal data.
- [ ] **AC-023** — A coach, instructor, coordinator, or administrator of any section books a free slot at any club facility in any zone without approval; an occupied slot, a closure, or hours outside opening hours return 409.
- [ ] **AC-024** — A request from a facility's public calendar becomes a valid booking only after administrator approval; until the decision it holds the slot against other requests for at most 48 h, then expires; a club booking supersedes it, with an email to the requester; approval creates a charge payable to the facility owner.
- [ ] **AC-025** — The public club and section page shows the description, classes with schedule, terms, instructors, materials, and contact only to the extent enabled in the club settings and for records marked as public; an instructor's surname only with their consent; the API does not return disabled blocks.
- [ ] **AC-026** — Prices and the sibling discount appear on the section page only when the prices block is enabled.
- [ ] **AC-027** — A coach creates a tournament for their own group without coordinator approval; the tournament appears in the calendars of participants and guardians; for someone else's group → 403.
- [ ] **AC-028** — The administrator uploads the club logo, and a coordinator uploads their section's logo; a section without a logo shows the club logo; removing the section logo restores the club logo; an SVG file or a file larger than 2 MB returns 422.
- [ ] **AC-029** — In one step, the administrator sets availability Mon–Fri 16:00–22:00 for several facilities, or different hours per day; the public calendar shows "bookable" only in the free portions of those hours; hours outside opening hours, shorter than 90 min, or in the wrong order are rejected with a message at the day.
- [ ] **AC-030** — After a statement import, transfers with a payer code and the full amount are booked without administrator involvement (when auto-booking is enabled); the rest are in the queue with a suggestion; re-importing creates no duplicates; every booking can be undone.
- [ ] **AC-031** — The administrator accepts cash from a parent in at most three steps (payer → charges → confirm), also on a phone; the payer receives an email with the confirmation number; an amount greater than the charges is rejected.
- [ ] **AC-032** — The administrator creates a kit form (set and products with sizes and prices, print, deadline) and has the text with a link for Messenger in under 10 minutes; the guardian orders for a child in the app or from a personal link in under 2 minutes and immediately sees the charge; after the deadline the administrator downloads the supplier summary (quantities per product and size, prints) without manual counting.

- [ ] **AC-035** — Every screen, email and notification of Vereino is available in English and Polish; a user switches the interface language in the profile and the change applies immediately; adding a third language requires only new catalog files; club-entered content is shown unchanged in every interface language.

- [ ] **AC-037** — A coach sees the phone and email only of consenting guardians of children in their own groups; withdrawal hides them at once; each read is audited.
- [ ] **AC-038** — The rented-hours report lists `done` session hours per rented place, month, group and coach, and exports to CSV.
- [ ] **AC-039** — Fixtures imported from a file become `match` sessions with opponent, venue and home/away, without duplicates on re-import, and conflicts with facility bookings are listed instead of created.
- [ ] Every listed backend surface matches its recorded Open Mercato reference and uses the canonical shell/components, shared API helpers, semantic tokens, and complete loading, empty, error, conflict, keyboard, accessibility, responsive, light-mode, and dark-mode states.
- [ ] Every affected API and UI path has self-contained integration coverage and the configured validation gate passes.
