## TLDR

Vereino is an open-source Open Mercato application for a small multi-section club (association) whose main section is a football academy, alongside which non-football sections operate — swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading — as a full-fledged use case (D-011). The reference scale is ~200 players in the academy; this describes scale, not a system limit. Pilot club: to be chosen; it must administer a municipal facility (D-025). The example club used throughout this document is "Dev Academy": five sections (a football academy with eight birth-year groups, a swimming pool, zumba, chess, cheerleading) and an Orlik 2024 pitch complex it manages on behalf of its municipality. Vereino replaces Messenger groups, Excel and phone calls: one person record with multiple roles and one account, sections with coordinators, groups of two equal kinds (team `team` and class group `class_group`), activities with recurrence that generate sessions, per-role calendars — a single one for a parent whose children are in different sections, declared and actual attendance, coach substitutions with automatic unit settlement, announcements with in-app notifications and email, membership fees with online payment via Stripe, and club facilities (including the municipal Orlik) with zones, an owner (municipality) report and booking calendars: staff of any section book a facility immediately (e.g., a coach for a tournament), while an external person submits a booking request in the public facilities calendar, which becomes valid only after administrator approval. The public club page shows sections, classes, schedule, terms, prices, instructors, materials and contact — the club turns each of these blocks on or off in its settings. We reuse Open Mercato modules: `directory`, `auth`, `notifications`, `attachments`, `entities`, `configs`, `audit_logs`, `dashboards`, `scheduler` (from phase 1), and in later phases `dictionaries`, `messages`, `payment_gateways` with `gateway_stripe`. Seven new application modules own the club domain. The smallest coherent outcome is phase 1 (`club_core` + `people`): club, sections, places, institutions, people, legal guardianship, roles and accounts, with a signed-in parent seeing their children; its implementation planning may start (D-021), while the pilot club is still to be chosen (D-025).

## Problem Statement

A small multi-section club today runs its schedule and attendance through several Messenger groups (separately for each section and group), membership fees in Excel, coach and instructor substitutions by phone, and Orlik bookings by calling the club; difficult cases leave no trail [DOCUMENT: note from the founders' meeting of 26.09.2026 — knowledge of the Gwiazda board, not a user interview].

- **Parent** of two children — often in different sections (football and swimming pool) — scrolls through 3–4 groups to find out whether practice is on, misses cancellations and answers "attending / not attending" in each group separately [ASSUMPTION]. The two guardians interviewed qualify this: regular training times are known from the start of the season; the friction is friendlies and tournaments (a gathering time that differed between a post and its poll, an address without a map pin that cost 30 minutes), messenger apps are muted, the schedule is checked the day before, and lifts are arranged in the group chat [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-founder.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-multi-club.md`]. A gathering time separate from the session start is not modelled (brief: Goals gap).
- **Coach or instructor** organizing a tournament or a training course checks by phone whether the pitch or the small training room is free; they cannot see the club's facilities calendar [ASSUMPTION — Władek's requirement of 2026-10-20].
- **External person** (another club, a school, a parent looking for classes) has nowhere to check which sections and classes the club runs, on what terms, and when a facility is free; they book only by phone [ASSUMPTION].
- **Coordinator of a non-football section** (swimming pool, zumba, pilates) has the same needs as the academy coordinator — groups, sessions, attendance, membership fees — but most market tools are modeled around a football team; Vibeclass is the one product found that presents multi-section clubs as a core case [BENCHMARK 2026-10-23: `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`, agent-checked; D-011].
- **Coach** collects declarations in a chat where they get lost; arranges substitutions by phone and then argues about units [ASSUMPTION]. Evidence against the dispute part: the coordinator says substitutions are rare, found among the coaches themselves, and described no dispute; the product owner recalls none in the last year [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`; brief A05].
- **Treasurer** does not see overdue charges in real time, sends reminders manually, and counts coach units and Orlik hours from memory [ASSUMPTION].
- **Coordinator**: the regular training timetable has no collisions — the coordinator and the administrator set it and publish it as a summer and a winter poster — but matches, tournaments and pitch maintenance (line marking, mowing, aeration) collide "very often", because the person who maintains the pitch plans work without the coordinator knowing [INTERVIEW: `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`; D-017]. Orlik has no public schedule [ASSUMPTION].

ProTrainUp (PLN 125–899/month by plan) covers the calendar/attendance/fees core; on 2026-10-23 its pricing page also lists a "facilities and reservations" module from the Club Pro plan and bank statement parsing from Gold, while nothing was found about external rental requests, a public schedule without sign-in or an owner report [BENCHMARK 2026-10-23: `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`, agent-checked, not yet re-checked by a human; the gaps stay an ASSUMPTION until the demo check on the brief's Collection plan]. That it is heavier than a small club needs and keeps data with the vendor is a team assessment. The evidence about users is in `.ai/specs/product-brief.md`: two guardian accounts (one from a co-founder) and the academy coordinator's account (`research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-founder.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-guardian-multi-club.md`, `research/interviews/2026-10-23-interview-coordinator.md`); nobody else outside the team has been interviewed, so the remaining claims about coaches, the treasurer and other clubs are hypotheses.

## Overview and Success Measures

- **Primary outcome:** the pilot club — the whole club, all sections — runs on Vereino for a full season without returning to Messenger for attendance (the club and the season are open, D-025) — ≥80% of attendance declarations submitted in the app, measured separately for each section (metric: `attendance` with `declared_by_person_id` ≠ null against the number of participants in `done` sessions).
- **Leading indicators:** number of sections with at least one active group in the system (target: all pilot sections, not just the academy); share of guardians with an active account after 4 weeks; share of `done` sessions with actual attendance checked off; share of charges paid before `due_date`; number of "needing a coach" sessions resolved before they start; monthly municipality report generated without manual corrections; rollout time of the pilot club from the first sign-in to the first group with declarations (vision board: one weekend).
- **Business measures tracked outside the product** (vision board): five other academies on Vereino by the end of 2027; an open-source repository with at least three contributors from outside the team.
- **Baseline:** `unknown — measurement plan`: before rollout the treasurer provides the number of families and overdue amounts at the end of the last month (to be requested from the pilot club's treasurer once the club is chosen, D-025; no treasurer account exists yet, D-021); Messenger declarations — counted manually in one group for 4 weeks.
- **Market / product reference:** ProTrainUp (benchmark 30.09.2026). We adopt the core: calendar, attendance, membership fees with reminders and online payment, multiple roles. We reject: coaching tools (drawings, exercise library, medical, scouting, statistics), because a small club would use a fraction of the features and PTU is strong there. We add (D-011): the club as a whole — multiple sections on equal footing, including non-football ones, and a single parent calendar across all sections (Vibeclass also presents multi-section clubs, so this is not unique; `research/2026-10-23-benchmark-competitors.md`) — plus the municipal facility administered for the owner (no product found with external rental requests, a public schedule and an owner report; an assessment from vendor pages) and data held by the club (open source, MIT).

## Goals

Requirements of phase 4 (REQ-011, REQ-012, REQ-026, REQ-030…REQ-034) are in `2026-10-27-vereino-club-payments.md`; those of phase 5 (REQ-013…REQ-016, REQ-022…REQ-024, REQ-029, REQ-038) are in `2026-10-27-vereino-facilities.md` (D-028). Their ids stay global.

Priority per `moscow.md`, with adjustments resulting from Q-002 and Q-003. Scope added after discovery (`decisions/D-013-scope-added-after-discovery.md`): REQ-023…REQ-027 (Q-021…Q-024), REQ-028 and REQ-029 (product owner request 2026-10-20), REQ-030 (product owner request 2026-10-20; Q-028 and Q-030 open), REQ-031 (Q-029), REQ-032 (Q-031, Q-033; Q-034, Q-035 open), REQ-033 (Q-029, Q-032; Q-036 open), REQ-034 (Q-037), REQ-035 (Q-038); all of them are Must. REQ-022 was raised from Could to Must on 2026-10-23 (D-017). REQ-032 upgrades kit orders from MoSCoW Could to Must.

- **REQ-001** (Must, D-005) — A person is one record regardless of the number of roles and has at most one login account; permissions add up, the UI has a context switcher, and actions record the role in which they were performed.
- **REQ-002** (Must, D-004; minors without payments — product owner decision of 2026-10-20) — A guardian sees the profiles of all wards; a ward from the club's age threshold (`own_account_min_age`, set by the administrator; default 16, D-022, Q-007) may have their own account with a limited view; a younger ward has no account; consents and payments always stay on the guardian's side — a minor participant (under 18) does not see payments at all (no tile, no screen, no API); payments are seen and paid by the guardian, and an adult participant pays for themselves.
- **REQ-003** (Must, D-001, D-011) — The multi-section club has sections as first-class entities — each with its own coordinator (or several), its own groups, activities and permission scope — plus a place hierarchy (complex → pitch, pool, hall) and institutions (municipality, schools, clubs, companies). The number of sections and participants has no limit in the system.
- **REQ-004** (Must, D-003, D-011) — A group is a single entity of kind `team` (team) or `class_group` (class group: swimming pool, zumba, pilates); both kinds are equal — they have the same memberships with history, sessions, attendance, substitutions, announcements and membership fees; `team` only has extra fields (age category, league, season, matches).
- **REQ-005** (Must, D-002) — An activity with an RRULE recurrence generates sessions; a single session can be edited or canceled without breaking the series.
- **REQ-006** (Must; D-022, Q-012) — The calendar is a view of sessions filtered by role: participant, guardian (all wards across all sections — one calendar), coach or instructor (own groups + substitutions), coordinator (their sections + the "needing a coach" list), administrator (the whole club). Whether a guardian sees which other children are called up for, or declared for, a session is a club setting `show_called_up_children`: `none` (default) or `consented` — only the first name and last name of children whose guardian gave the consent `share_presence_with_guardians_at` in the ward's profile.
- **REQ-007** (Must, D-006, D-022, D-026) — Attendance declaration and actual attendance. Two settings apply:
  - **what is declared** (`declaration_kind`, Q-009 of the brief) — a club setting per activity type (`club_settings.declaration_kinds`); a group policy does not change it (D-026): `absence_only` — guardians report only that the child will not come, and silence counts as present for planning; or `confirm` — guardians confirm presence. Defaults: `absence_only` for `training`, `confirm` for `match`, `sparring`, `tournament`, `camp` and `other` (the coordinator's model for matches, friendlies and tournaments, D-022; extended to `camp` and `other` by this specification);
  - **who declares** for a ward with an account (`immediate`, `guardian_confirm`, `guardian_only`) with a time limit and a default value — a policy per club, group, away trip or activity type (D-006).
  Actual attendance is always recorded by the coach.
- **REQ-008** (Must, D-007, Q-004) — A substitution changes the session's coach with full history; sessions without a coach go to the coordinator's list; the monthly units report per coach shows planned, handed over, taken over and balance; unit = one session of type `training` (team training or class group class) in status `done`, owed to whoever led it — the same in every section.
- **REQ-009** (Must, D-009 confirmed in Q-002) — Announcements to the club, section, group, activity, session and direct, with threads; recipients receive an in-app notification and an email; the author can generate a text with a link to paste into the group chat in a messaging app.
- **REQ-010** (Must) — A "case" is a thread marked as evidentiary: undeletable, with full history, visible to the section coordinator and the club administrator.
- **REQ-017** (Should, H6) — Personal link for attendance declaration without sign-in, sent by email; a link pasted into the group chat does not reveal data without authentication.
- **REQ-018** (Should) — Camp as an activity of type `camp` with a one-off fee and a participant list.
- **REQ-019** (Should, D-011) — For `team` groups: PZPN (Polish Football Association) age categories as a club-editable dictionary and season as an entity. `class_group` groups do not have these fields and lose nothing.
- **REQ-020** (Should) — For `team` groups: match and friendly with an opponent selected from the list of external teams and with a score.
- **REQ-021** (Should; D-022, Q-014 of the brief) — Monthly coach settlement for export, in the model the club chooses (`coach_settlement_model`):
  - `units_per_contract` — base + units × unit rate + matches × match rate + away trips × trip rate, per contract;
  - `fixed_base_adjusted` — a fixed monthly base, minus a fixed amount for each session of the coach's own groups they did not lead, plus the same amount for each session they led as a substitute, plus a fixed amount per away trip (the academy coordinator's practice).
  The contract carries the rates of the chosen model; the report shows the formula it used.
- **REQ-025** (Must, Q-021, Q-024) — Public club page `/p/[clubSlug]`: list of sections and facilities; a page for each section with a description, classes (groups marked as public) with a schedule (days, hours, place), age category or level, terms, instructor, downloadable materials and contact. Content is entered in the admin panel on the section and on the group; the club settings decide which blocks are published (description and schedule, terms, prices, instructors, materials, contact, facilities calendar, online requests). The instructor's name additionally requires that person's consent.
- **REQ-027** (Must, Q-022; amends D-002 with the `tournament` activity type) — A one-off event for their own group (`tournament` or `other`) is created by a coach or instructor without the coordinator's consent; a tournament has a list of invited external teams.
- **REQ-028** (Must) — The administrator uploads the club logo; the administrator or section coordinator can upload a separate section logo. A section without its own logo shows the club logo. The club logo appears in the header of the admin panel, the member interface and the public page; the section logo — on the section tile, the section page and next to its groups.
- **REQ-035** (Must) — Multilingual interface: every user-facing text of Vereino (member interface, admin panel, public pages, system emails, notifications, PDF/CSV headers) comes from translation catalogs; 1.0 ships complete English and Polish catalogs, and adding another language means adding catalog files only, without code changes. Each person chooses their interface language (default: the club's default language); public pages follow the browser language with a language switcher; system emails and notifications are sent in the recipient's language. Dates, times, numbers and amounts are formatted for the selected language, in the club's time zone and currency. Content entered by the club (section and group names, descriptions, conditions, announcements, product names) stays in the language in which it was written — one content language per club.
- **REQ-036** (Must; D-023, Q-018, Q-044) — Bulk import of people and families from a file: the administrator uploads a CSV or XLSX export (for example the club's current Google sheet) with columns for the child, the guardian(s), the group and contact details, maps the columns once, sees a preview with every row classified — new person, matched existing person, needs review, error — and commits. A person is matched by first name, last name and the PESEL or another identifier (`external_id`), both stored encrypted and compared through a deterministic hash; a row without a PESEL or identifier is never merged automatically and goes to a manual review queue. A repeated import of the same file creates no duplicates, and every import is recorded with who ran it and what it created. In phase 1 the commit creates persons and guardianships and keeps the group column on the row; group memberships are created in phase 2 through an optional hook registered by `groups` (D-026).
- **REQ-037** (Must; D-023, Q-045) — Guardians' contact list for coaches, by consent: a guardian gives or withdraws, in their profile, the consent `share_contact_with_coaches_at`; a coach or instructor then sees the phone and email of the consenting guardians of children in their own groups only, on the session's attendance list, with every view audited. Without consent the coach reaches the guardian only through announcements and threads (REQ-009).
- **REQ-039** (Should; D-023, Q-046) — League fixtures from PZPN / Łączy nas piłka for `team` groups: the coordinator imports the fixtures published for a team (date, time, opponent, venue, home or away) and the system creates or updates `match` sessions, without duplicating fixtures already imported. The source is an exported file (ICS or CSV) until V-14 shows an API the club may use; results and player data are not imported. *V-14 (2026-10-27) found no export and no usable API, and the portal's terms forbid extraction: the source is a CSV/XLSX template filled by the coordinator or a paste of the portal's `Mecze` rows; re-import updates by (date, home, away).*
- **REQ-040** (Should; D-027) — A person who already holds an invited account connects Google (first) and Facebook (later) from `/club/profile` and then signs in with one click on `/login`; no social sign-in creates an account, matches an e-mail or widens an account, and a sign-in without a link, a replayed state or a provider failure shows one generic message. Design: `.ai/specs/2026-10-27-vereino-social-sign-in.md` (REQ-S01…S04, phases 1–2), delivered after the foundation spec's phase A; the sign-in lookup on `vereino_identity_links` (`provider`, `subject`) is the DD-35 extension approved by D-027.

**Readiness** (the brief's Definition of Ready addendum, D-021): phases 1–3 and the facility design may be planned now. Work that rests only on an untested assumption stays designed here but is **held back from implementation planning** until the review after the phase 2 pilot or until an account exists: the treasurer role and screens (REQ-034, DD-33, phase 4 step 10 — brief A02) and the automatic substitution and coach settlement (the settlement parts of REQ-008 and REQ-021, phase 2 step 7 — brief A05).

- Volunteer duty sign-up for sessions (planned for 1.1).

- Receipts and invoices for order campaigns: 1.0 offers only `none` in the `order_sales_document` setting (D-022, D-023).

- Attachments in announcements and threads — materials are published on the section page (REQ-025) and incident files go through `attachments`.

## Proposed Solution

Seven application modules in `src/modules/<id>/`, each with entities, commands, API routes (`makeCrudRoute` or command routes), pages and events:

| Module | Owns | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| `club_core` | club settings with publication settings, sections (with public content), places (hierarchy), institutions, contracts; public club and section page assembled from blocks provided by other modules | 1 (groups and schedule block — 2, prices — 4, facilities — 5; coach contracts — 2, management agreement — 5) |
| `people` | people, legal guardianship with consents, role assignments, record scope service, account creation | 1 |
| `groups` | groups, memberships, seasons, external teams | 2 |
| `activities` | activities with RRULE, one-off events (tournament) created by the coach, sessions, attendance, declaration policy, substitutions, units report and coach settlement, declaration links, public schedule block | 2 (links — 3) |
| `communication` | scoped threads, "cases", the member inbox; e-mail through the foundation `dispatch` (foundation III DD-M02) | 3 |
| `payments` | fee schedules, discounts, charges, payments, online payment sessions, statement import, cash and its handover, order campaigns (e.g., kits) | 4 — `2026-10-27-vereino-club-payments.md` |
| `facilities` | zones, closures, rental rates, internal staff bookings, external rentals (entered by staff or requested online with administrator approval), facilities calendar for staff, public facilities calendar, log, incidents, bans, maintenance, owner report | 5 — `2026-10-27-vereino-facilities.md` |

No module assumes that a section is a football section: section, group (`team` or `class_group`), activity, session, attendance, substitution, announcement and membership fee work the same for the academy, the swimming pool and zumba. Football-specific elements (PZPN age categories, league, season, match with opponent and score) are extra fields of the `team` group, not a separate path.

How this solves the problems: the parent gets one mobile "Today" screen with the sessions of all children from all sections, one-tap declaration, messages and payments (REQ-002, 006, 007, 009, 012); the coach — an attendance list on the phone and absence reporting, after which the coordinator assigns a substitute and the units transfer automatically (REQ-007, 008); the treasurer — charge generation, discounts and an overdue list with online payment (REQ-011, 012); the coordinator — a schedule with place conflict detection and a public Orlik schedule (REQ-005, 013, 014); the board — an undeletable register of cases (REQ-010).

Why this is the smallest platform-native solution: identity, RBAC, data isolation, notifications, files, custom fields, audit, scheduled jobs, message storage and the payment gateway come from installed modules (see *Reuse and Ownership Map*). Custom code is written only where module-facts contain no equivalent: the club domain, sessions with attendance, per-section/place/ward scope, club charges, and facility rules from the Orlik rules.

Interfaces: staff configure the club in the admin panel (`/backend/...`) using `DataTable` and `CrudForm`; the daily work of all roles — parent, participant, coach, facility attendant — happens in the mobile member interface `/club/...` (module frontend pages with `requireAuth`), per Q-001: one `auth` account for everyone.

**Scope derivation:** `tenantId` and `organizationId` always come from the authenticated context. Every list route adds a scope filter from `people.scopeService` after the organization filter; no linked person or an empty scope yields an empty list, and a write outside the scope yields 403. Operations without a login session are the public club, section and facilities calendar pages, the booking request with its email confirmation, and the declaration link pages: scope is derived from `club_settings.public_slug` (+ section/place slug) or from the token record (hash), never from the request body. The only operation in system scope (`organizationId: null`) is that single read-only, index-backed lookup of the slug or token hash, which immediately narrows every further query to the found `tenantId`/`organizationId` (DD-35, V-12); nothing else runs in system scope.

## Architecture and Data Flow

```text
Guardian /club (Today) ─► GET /api/activities/calendar?as=guardian ─► people.scopeService (ward_person_ids)
                                                                    ─► groups (memberships on the day) ─► activities_occurrences
Guardian ─► POST /api/activities/attendance/declare ─► activities.attendance.declare (policy, scope)
                                                    ─► event activities.attendance.declared ─► notifications (guardian/coach)
Coordinator /backend ─► POST /api/activities/activities ─► activities.activities.create
                                                        ─► RRULE expansion (horizon) ─► activities_occurrences
                                                        ─► [phase 5] facilities guard: conflicts with bookings/closures
scheduler (daily) ─► worker activities:generate-occurrences ─► appends sessions up to the horizon
Coach /club ─► POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/absence ─► coach_person_id = null ─► event ─► notifications (coordinator)
Coordinator ─► POST /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/coach ─► substitution entry ─► event ─► notifications (substitute)
Coach ─► PUT /api/activities/occurrences/{id}/attendance ─► status done ─► units report (query)
Cancellation ─► activities.occurrence.cancelled ─► [phase 3] communication: session thread + email (foundation dispatch)
scheduler (1st day of the month) ─► payments:generate-charges ─► groups (members) + people (payer) ─► payments_charges
Guardian ─► POST /api/payments/checkout ─► paymentGatewayService (stripe) ─► redirect to Stripe
Stripe ─► webhook ─► payment_gateways ─► payment_gateways.payment.captured ─► payments subscriber (idempotent) ─► payments_payments
Facility attendant ─► POST /api/facilities/rentals ─► facilities.rentals.create ─► DI payments: charge (payee = municipality) in the same transaction
Guest ─► GET /api/facilities/public/{clubSlug}/places/{placeSlug}/schedule ─► zones + occupancy (no personal data)
Guest ─► POST …/places/{placeSlug}/requests ─► rental pending_verification ─► email with link ─► pending_approval (48 h hold)
        ─► notifications: administrators "request awaiting approval"
Administrator ─► POST /api/facilities/rentals/{id}/approve ─► requested + charge (DI payments) ─► email to the requester
Coach /club/places ─► POST /api/facilities/internal-bookings ─► occupancyService ─► confirmed (no approval)
                   ─► [for a group] DI activities: one-off event (tournament) ─► session occupies the facility
                   ─► conflicting pending_approval requests → superseded (same unit of work) ─► email after commit
Guest ─► GET /api/club_core/public/{clubSlug}/sections/{sectionSlug} ─► club_core (description, materials, contact)
        ─► optional DI: groups.publicCatalog, activities.publicSchedule, payments.publicPrices, facilities.publicPlaces
        ─► filter club_settings.publication + is_public + name consent
```

- **Module boundaries:** `club_core` owns the club structure and contracts; `people` — identity, guardianship and scope (the sole source of `scopeService`); `groups` — rosters over time; `activities` — sessions and everything that happens at a session (attendance, substitutions, units), because attendance and substitution must be transactionally consistent with the session; `communication` — conversation scope and cases; `payments` — club and municipality money; `facilities` — facility rules from the rules document. Dependencies (`requires`): `people` → `club_core`; `groups` → `people`; `activities` → `groups`; `communication` → `activities` (`occurrence` scope), `messages`; `payments` → `groups`, `payment_gateways`; `facilities` → `activities` (the rental charge goes through the optional DI token `payments.chargeService` when `payments` is enabled — DD-19 in the facilities specification, D-028). No ORM relations between modules — only IDs, display name snapshots, events, DI services and mutation guards.
- **Shared packages:** 1.0 keeps all code in `packages/app/src/modules/<id>/`; code moves to a workspace package only when at least two modules reuse it or it is framework-free (AGENTS.md). Candidate content for later extraction: money, discount and period helpers and validation schemas → `@vereino/core`; the schedule-grid components (the V-8 fallback) → `@vereino/ui`; test-database and seed helpers → `@vereino/db`. `yarn typecheck:packages` is part of every phase's validation.
- **Extension points:** frontend and backend pages discovered from files; notification types (`notifications.ts`); audience providers `vereino.audience.<kind>` and e-mail templates for the foundation `dispatch` (foundation III); dashboard widgets (`widgets/dashboard/**`); the optional DI contract `vereino.occupancyProvider` consulted on session preview and save, implemented by `facilities` (DD-20 as amended by foundation II DD-G05); schedule targets registered in `setup.ts`; encryption maps (`encryption.ts`); search configuration (`search.ts`); custom fields (`ce.ts`). We do not modify the code of installed modules.
- **Alternatives considered:** customer portal for parents (rejected in Q-001); one `club` module with all entities (rejected: phases would not be separately deployable, and permissions and events would get mixed); `staff` + `resources` + `planner` as the core (rejected in the reuse map).
- **Compatibility:** the application is new — there are no existing Vereino contracts. From the first deployment the following are protected per the root `BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md` (§2–§6, tier "protected at first deployment"): API routes and their error codes, IDs of events, ACL features, notification types and schedule targets, DI service interfaces (`people.scopeService` and the optional provider tokens) and the database schema; changes go through additive fields or a deprecation bridge, and every Changelog row that adds a surface names it. Enabling the `scheduler`, `messages`, `payment_gateways`, `gateway_stripe` modules does not change the behavior of already enabled modules.

The club administrator, in `/backend/club/settings`, fills in the short name, public slug and the account age threshold (Dev Academy lowers the default 16 to 13, so that Ola below can have an account); in `/backend/club/sections` creates Dev Academy's five sections "Akademia piłkarska", "Basen", "Zumba", "Szachy" and "Cheerleader" (Q-005), and on the section record assigns a coordinator (Marek — academy, Ewa — swimming pool; the other three have no coordinator yet); a section without a coordinator is flagged with a warning; in `/backend/club/places` creates the complex "Orlik 2024" with the children "Boisko piłkarskie", "Boisko wielofunkcyjne", "Zaplecze szatniowe", a hall and an outdoor pool (`is_external`); in `/backend/club/institutions` — the municipality as the owner of the complex.
2. In `/backend/people/persons/create` they create Kasia (guardian) as well as Jaś (9 years old) and Ola (14 years old); on the person record, in the "Guardianship" tab, they link Kasia with the children and check "primary payer" and the consents.
3. On Kasia's record they choose "Create account": the system creates an `auth:user` with the roles `vereino_member` and `vereino_payer` (Kasia is an adult) and sends an email for setting a password. For Jaś the button is disabled with the explanation "below the account age threshold (13)". For Ola they create an account with a limited view — without the `vereino_payer` role, so Ola does not see payments.
4. Kasia sets a password, signs in and lands on `/club` — she sees Jaś's and Ola's profiles and the consents.
5. Errors: email already used by another account → 409 with a message at the field; saving a person record with an outdated version → 409 and the shared conflict dialog; a user without the `people.manage` feature sees the "Access denied" page.

### Journey J-002 — Training with declaration, substitution and settlement (phases 2–3; draft §7)

1. The coordinator creates the activity "Żaki training": group Żaki, Tuesdays 17:00–18:30, Boisko piłkarskie, coach Marek, weekly until 30.06. The wizard shows a preview of the first sessions and place conflicts before saving.
2. The system generates sessions for a 16-week horizon; the role calendars fill up.
3. In parallel, the swimming pool coordinator Ewa creates in her section a class group (`class_group`) "Basen — początkujący" with the activity "Class" on Thursdays and an instructor; Jaś is a member of both groups. Kasia, on `/club`, sees in one calendar "Tuesday 17:00 — Jaś, training, Boisko piłkarskie" and "Thursday 16:00 — Jaś, class, Basen"; trainings and classes use `absence_only`, so she taps "Report absence" only for the Thursday class Jaś will miss, and the Tuesday training needs no answer. Ola (14) declares for a Saturday match (`confirm`) herself → status "awaiting guardian confirmation"; Kasia receives a notification and approves, or does not respond — after 24 h the policy's default value applies.
4. On Monday Marek reports an absence → the session goes to the "needing a coach" list and the coordinator receives a notification; the coordinator assigns Bartek → an entry in the substitution history, Bartek receives a notification, the session appears in his calendar.
5. After training Bartek checks off attendance at `/club/occurrences/{id}/attendance` and closes the session → `done`. In the monthly report Marek has a "handed over" unit, Bartek a "taken over" one.
6. Errors: declaration after the start → message "declarations closed"; two users check off at the same time → the second gets a version conflict and a refreshed list; a coach from outside the group opens the list → 403.

### Journey J-003 — Cancellation and announcement (phase 3)

1. The coordinator cancels a session with the reason "downpour". Recipients (guardians of minors, participants with an account, the coach) receive an in-app notification and an email.
2. The coordinator copies the ready-made text "Żaki training on Tuesday 17:00 canceled — details: {link}" and pastes it into the Messenger group chat.
3. A parent who is not signed in opens the link → sees only the activity name, date and status plus a "Send me a link" form (email); receives a personal link and uses it to declare attendance for the next session.
4. The parent writes to the coach in the group thread; the coordinator marks the thread as a case → from then on the messages are undeletable.
5. Errors: the email is not delivered → event `foundation.dispatch.delivery_failed` (foundation III), status "not delivered" next to the recipient in the thread; a link request for an unknown email → the same response as for a known one (no enumeration).

### Journey J-008 — A guest explores the club and its sections (phases 1–5)

1. A parent looking for classes for a child goes to `/p/dev-academy`: club name and description, section tiles (Akademia piłkarska, Basen, Zumba, Szachy, Cheerleader) and a link to the facilities calendars.
2. On the "Basen" page they see the section description, the groups "Basen — początkujący (6–9 lat)" and "Basen — średniozaawansowani", with days, hours and place, terms (age, required attire), membership fee and sibling discount, the instructor (with her consent), materials (section rules, membership declaration) and contact details for the coordinator.
3. In a club that has disabled the prices block, the same page contains no prices — the API does not return them.
4. A group not marked as public and a non-public section do not appear; unknown slug → 404.

|---|---|---|---|
| Guardian / participant | `/club`: Today → Calendar → Messages → Payments → Profile | "Today" tiles | sign-in → `/club` → "Attending" on a session (2 taps) |
| Coach / instructor | `/club`: Today (my sessions) → Calendar → Facilities → Messages; "as coach / as guardian" switcher | "My sessions today" tile | sign-in → `/club` → session → attendance list (3 taps); sign-in → Facilities → slot → "Book" (3 taps) |
| Guest | `/p/[clubSlug]`: Club → Sections → Facilities | — | club page → facility → slot → "Request a booking" (3 clicks + form + link from the email) |
| Coordinator | backend: Club → People → Classes (Groups, Activities, Sessions with the calendar perspective, Coach units report) → Communication; `/club/places` for internal bookings (and maintenance planning when granted `facilities.maintenance.manage` with place assignments, D-024) | "Sessions needing a coach", "Unsettled sessions" widgets (click → session list tab) | sign-in → dashboard → widget → substitute coach assignment (3 clicks) |
| Facility attendant | `/club/facility` + backend: Facility (Calendar, Bookings, Log, Incidents) | "Today at the facility" widget | sign-in → `/club/facility` → "Open facility" |
| Maintenance planner (feature only) | `/club/places`: their assigned places only | — | sign-in → `/club/places` → slot → "Plan maintenance"; a task → "Done" |
| Administrator | backend: Club → People → Classes → Communication → Finance (Fees, Discounts, Charges with the Overdue perspective, Bank statements, Order campaigns, Cash to settle) → Facility (+ Requests awaiting approval, Zones, Closures, Rates, Bans, Maintenance, Municipality report) → Settings (Club page publication) | "Overdue", "Charges without payer", "Requests awaiting approval" widgets (click → queue) | sign-in → dashboard → "Overdue" → recording a payment (3 clicks) |
| Treasurer | backend: Dashboard → Finance only (Fees, Discounts, Charges with the Overdue perspective, Bank statements, Order campaigns, Cash to settle) | "Overdue", "Charges without payer", "Order campaigns" widgets | sign-in → dashboard → "Overdue" → recording a payment (3 clicks) |

Redirect after sign-in: an account whose user is linked to a person (`GET /api/people/me` → 200, feature `people.self`) lands on `/club`; an account without a linked person (e.g., the technical administrator) lands on `/backend`. The decision reads the person link and the feature, never a role name (DD-06).

## Data Models

The `payments` entities are in `2026-10-27-vereino-club-payments.md`, the `facilities` entities in `2026-10-27-vereino-facilities.md`.

Common columns of every entity: `id` UUID (primary key), `tenant_id` and `organization_id` UUID (required, only from trusted context, composite index with the most frequent filters), `created_at`, `updated_at` (optimistic locking version, returned as `updatedAt`), `deleted_at` (soft delete) — unless the row says otherwise. Amounts as integers in grosz (minor units) + the club currency. Tables prefixed with the module name. No ORM relations between modules: references are scalar IDs, and where a list shows a name from another module, a name snapshot is kept up to date by an event subscriber. Fields marked "encr." go into `encryption.ts` (`defaultEncryptionMaps`) and are read with decryption helpers using both scope IDs.

## API, Command, and Error Contracts

`/api/payments/*` routes and the gateway subscriber are in `2026-10-27-vereino-club-payments.md`; `/api/facilities/*` routes are in `2026-10-27-vereino-facilities.md`. The conventions below apply to all three specifications.

All CRUD routes go through `makeCrudRoute` (per-method `metadata` with `requireAuth` and `requireFeatures`, a separate `openApi` export, `indexer`, scope filter by `organizationId`); writes go through commands (`commands/*.ts`) with audit and undo where it makes sense. Updates and deletions require a version (`updatedAt`) and return 409 on conflict. Action routes are command routes with `metadata`, a Zod schema and `openApi`, running mutation guards before the write and side effects after the transaction commits. Conventions from the engineering standards (`.ai/specs/2026-10-21-engineering-standards-and-patterns.md`) apply to every route below: lists return the installed paged shape `{ items, total, page, pageSize, totalPages }` (S6.4); JSON keys are camelCase, instants ISO 8601 UTC, amounts integer grosz with `currency` (S6.3–S6.6); a missing lock token returns 400 and a stale one 409 (S5.2); every machine code named in the *Errors* column (`occurrence_place_conflict`, `declaration_closed`, `rental_conflict`, …) is declared once in its owning module's `lib/error-codes.ts`, returned as `{ error, code? }` (`code` present for every business error) and checked by `yarn contracts:check` (S6.7).

## Security, Privacy, and Compliance

- **Authorization:** every route has `requireFeatures`; every record read and write goes through `people.scopeService`; an `auth` role is never checked by name. Actions record an `acting_role` determined on the server side.
- **Tenant isolation:** `tenantId` + `organizationId` filters from context on every query, including in workers (the scope in the job payload comes from the schedule registration, not from user data); no scope = no data. Sessionless routes derive the scope from the public slug or the token hash through exactly one read-only, index-backed system-scope lookup (DD-35, V-12) — a deviation from "only an installed contract may use system scope" that the product owner approved on 2026-10-23 (D-024) for the case that V-12 finds no installed contract and that `AGENTS.md` records as an exception (D-026); every query after the lookup carries the found `tenantId` and `organizationId`.
- **Sensitive data:** field encryption per *Data Models* via `encryption.ts`; bank statements contain third-party data (senders, account numbers) — the file is kept in the private `attachments` partition, transaction fields are encrypted, access only with `payments.bank.import`, retention in line with accounting regulations (to be confirmed with the accountant); PESEL optional, visible only with `people.pesel.view`, duplicate detection via HMAC; link tokens stored as SHA-256; last-name search on encrypted fields per the `search.encrypted-column-list-filter` pattern (V-3); logs without PII or message content (`createLogger`); right to erasure via the anonymization command, which keeps accounting and attendance entries without personal data. Guardian consents with date and author. Children below the club's `own_account_min_age` (default 16, D-022) have no account, and their data is not visible to other parents unless the club enables `show_called_up_children` and the guardian consents (REQ-006).
- **External requests and the public page:** requester data encrypted, visible only to staff with `facilities` features; the data processing notice and the acceptance of the regulations are recorded with the request; a request is not valid without administrator approval and email confirmation; strict rate limit per IP and per email, honeypot field, requests blocked for a slot with a valid hold; an entry ban register match is only a hint for the administrator (not disclosed to the requester). The club page public API does not return blocks disabled in the settings or records without `is_public`; the instructor's name only with `publish_name_consent_at`; never lists of participants or guardians, or children's data.
- **Abuse and failure modes:** public routes with rate limits (`metadata` of `makeCrudRoute` routes, V-10); a link request always returns 202 (no email enumeration); a token is limited to one session and one guardian's wards and expires at the end of the session; the public schedule contains no names; cases cannot be deleted or edited; monetary operations cannot be edited (reversing entries only); duplicate webhooks and retries are idempotent; all edits protected by a version (409). Stripe secrets only in the integration configuration, never in the spec, logs or comments.
- **Compliance:** the account age threshold is the club setting `own_account_min_age`, default 16, not a constant in code; the owner waived a legal opinion and the club may lower it (D-022, Q-007); club and facility regulations as an attachment; retention of former participants' data — Q-019 (to be confirmed before the pilot).

## Rollout, Migration, and Rollback

- **Migrations:** one per module per phase; `yarn db:generate` as a probe, SQL review, module snapshot; user approval before the migration is committed and before `yarn db:migrate`; post-deployment changes — corrective migrations.
- **Setup and data:** `seedDefaults` (idempotent): `vereino_*` roles with features, club settings, default declaration policies, PZPN (Polish Football Association) age category dictionary; `seedExamples`: the demonstration club "Dev Academy" (five sections — Akademia piłkarska, Basen, Zumba, Szachy, Cheerleader — with coordinators for the academy and the swimming pool; an Orlik (municipal sports pitch complex) with pitches and a swimming pool; the Żaki team, class groups "Basen — początkujący" and "Zumba wtorek"; a family with two children in different sections) — only on request. After adding features in an existing tenant: `yarn mercato auth sync-role-acls`.
- **Pilot rollout order** (at the pilot club, once chosen — D-025; Dev Academy's sections stand in for it): phase 1 → the administrator enters people and families of the club's sections (or imports them, REQ-036) → phase 2 with two groups from different sections (the Żaki team and the swimming pool class group) for 4 weeks with measurement of declarations → the remaining groups of all five sections → phases 3–5. The rollout owner at the pilot club (date, who trains) is open — Q-039, D-025. Each phase requires approval to enable modules in `src/modules.ts`.
- **Observability:** `createLogger` per module; events `scheduler.job.failed` and `foundation.dispatch.delivery_failed` as operational signals; the "unsettled" and "without payer" widgets as data quality indicators.
- **Rollback:** each phase is additive. Rolling back a phase = disabling its module in `src/modules.ts` and unregistering its jobs; data stays in the module's tables and comes back after re-enabling. No dropping of tables or columns without a separate decision. Payments: disabling `gateway_stripe` leaves manual posting in place. The `facilities` guard disappears together with the module, so `activities` works as in phase 2. Since D-028 `facilities` does not require `payments`, so either module can be rolled back without the other; rentals recorded without `payments` keep their amount and get no charge retroactively.
- **Open outside the specification:** the hosting model for clubs without an IT person and the domain (D-012) do not block implementation.
