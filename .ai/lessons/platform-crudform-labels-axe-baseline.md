# Scan Vereino pages with the platform axe baseline; CrudForm 0.9.0 labels are not associated

**Context**: ENG-TEST-25 (2026-10-29) scanned the ten `club_core` admin pages with axe for the first time. List pages were clean, but every form page reported critical `label` and `button-name` violations, and the settings page also reported `color-contrast` and `label-content-name-mismatch`.

**Problem**: All of them come from platform markup in `@open-mercato/ui` 0.9.0, not from Vereino code. `CrudForm` renders each field's `<label>` without `htmlFor` and its inputs, textareas and select triggers without an id or `aria-label` (`node_modules/@open-mercato/ui/src/backend/CrudForm.tsx`, field wrapper with `data-crud-field-id`). The backend AppShell sidebar group headings failed contrast (fixed since #481 by a Vereino rule in `globals.css`, so their baseline entry is gone), and its "Back to Main" link has an `aria-label` that differs from its text. Field configuration cannot fix this without ejecting `CrudForm`, and a baseline by rule id alone would also hide the same rule on Vereino markup.

**Rule**: Scan Vereino pages with `scanBlockingViolations(page, testInfo, { platformBaseline: true })` from `packages/app/.ai/qa/tests/_fixtures/axe.ts`. The baseline matches by rule and node (`data-crud-focus-target` controls, `data-testid="appshell-` links; until #481 also headings inside a `[data-sidebar-group]` element), so Vereino markup with the same rule still fails. Match a node by what it is in the page, not by the text of axe's selector: axe reports the shortest unique selector, so adding a sidebar group (`communication`, #35, 2026-11-02) turned two headings' selectors into `.pb-2.border-b.px-3:nth-child(1) > …` and ENG-TEST-25 failed until the matcher checked `closest('[data-sidebar-group]')` in the page. The dashboard `/backend` (#88, 2026-11-04) added one more platform node: the AppShell breadcrumb's current-page `<span aria-current="page" aria-label="Dashboard">` (`aria-prohibited-attr`, matched by `data-slot="breadcrumb-page"`). The schedule family (`ScheduleView`, #94, 2026-11-04) adds two more platform sources: react-big-calendar nests `role="rowgroup"` inside `role="row"` in the week grid (`aria-required-children`, `aria-required-parent`, matched by an `rbc-` class) and the `ScheduleToolbar` date-range trigger is labelled "Date range" while it shows the range (matched by `data-slot="date-range-picker-tr…"`). Never widen it to a whole rule. Remove an entry as soon as an upgrade of `@open-mercato/ui` fixes it, and report the defect upstream instead of patching `node_modules`.

**Update (E20-S18, #497, 2026-11-20)**: the `CrudForm` (`label`, `button-name`), breadcrumb (`aria-prohibited-attr`) and AppShell brand/back link (`label-content-name-mismatch`) entries are gone from the baseline. `PlatformA11yRepairs` (`@vereino/ui`, `packages/ui/src/admin/platform-a11y.ts`, mounted in the backend layout) repairs those nodes in the page: it names each `CrudForm` control by its field label through `aria-labelledby` (a control that shows text, such as the select trigger, keeps that text in its name), names table search boxes by their placeholders, gives the icon-only breadcrumb page `role="img"` and starts AppShell link names with their visible text. A repair only adds or corrects ARIA attributes and is idempotent, so its `MutationObserver` settles after one extra pass. A scan that reports one of these rules again means the repair missed a control: fix the repair, do not restore the baseline entry. Remove a repair when the platform fixes its gap (upstream reports linked from #497).

**Applies to**: integration tests of every Vereino page (`packages/app/.ai/qa/tests/**`), engineering standards S7.4, upgrades of `@open-mercato/ui`.
