## Decisions

- **One test, steps as `test.step`.** The journey runs serially on one fresh tenant, and the exit-gate scans run on the same data.
- **Exit-gate scans.** Each scan is in light and dark mode at 375 px, with the 320 px reflow check, on one page from each area:
  - `/backend/club/sections`
  - `/backend/people/persons/[id]`
  - `/club`
  - `/p/[clubSlug]` and its section page
- **"No permission".** A coordinator who has `people.view` but not `people.manage` opens `/backend/people/persons/create` and sees "Access Denied". The API answers 403.
- **Enabled blocks only.** The club publishes "description" and "contact" but not "terms". The section has terms text, and the page and the public API leave it out.
- **Locales.** The test does not switch locale. Locales are covered by:
  - TEST-047;
  - the en/pl runs of E02-S05;
  - the `i18n:check-hardcoded` gate step.

## Installed behaviour that differed from J-001's wording

The test asserts the installed behaviour accepted with #25–#29:

- **Public slug.** It is set on `/backend/club/publication` (`club_core/components/PublicationForm.tsx`), not on `/backend/club/settings`.
- **Institution before places.** The place owner is chosen on the place form (`PlaceForm.tsx`, `ownerInstitutionId`), so the municipality is created first.
- **Hall and outdoor pool.** They are top-level places; only the three named places sit under "Orlik 2024".
- **Used e-mail.** The 409 `email_taken` is shown as a flash message, not at the field (`people/components/AccountPanel.tsx`).
- **Conflict dialog.** The "shared conflict dialog" is the platform's conflict notice, the same one TEST-006 checks.
- **Tab name.** The tab is "Guardianship and consents".
- **Kasia's payments.** They are not visible before the `payments` module (phase 4), so her `vereino_payer` role is checked instead (lesson `future-module-gates-and-auth-login-interceptors`).
- **Ola's payments.** She sees none: `/club/payments` answers 404 and the age gate answers `payments_minor`.
