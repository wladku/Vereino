## Decisions

- **Step 3 and step 5's link request are not walked:** they belong to REQ-017 (foundation I phase C); the test asserts only that the public page offers sign-in and no e-mail form.
- **The failed e-mail of step 5 is forced on an announcement,** through the messaging probe as in TEST-M05: the probe cannot send the lock header the cancellation needs.
- **Locales:** the club reads Polish; one guardian with an account and one guardian with an e-mail only read English, so both languages are asserted in the capture.

## Where the installed product differs from J-003's wording

Recorded in the spec note: the share text is copied from the session thread's page; the text names the activity and the date in the club's language; the public page also shows the club name and the end time and offers sign-in; the in-app notice of step 1 is `activities`' phase 2 notice while the e-mail comes from `communication`; a parent replies in a staff announcement's thread; "undeletable" is the API's 409 `case_immutable` (no edit or delete controls in the UI); the delivery list shows the first message's deliveries to the author, the section's coordinator and the administrator.
